user on the state of mind it rests on ("Good, proceed"). Built the same day (cf66874, 44064a5): §2-7 as written
with three departures found by the smokes (onepass-smoke-1..4, `docs/experiments.md`): the dimension nouls of §3

## The ruling

The user, 2026-09-26, after the inventory of what Jev controls (three deciders in one call: the threat passes for
parties, per-actor Choices for builders and labs, the executor arms for whatever a rule names; groups with no threat
in reach asked nothing at all):

"I want to lean hard into the jev prefilter+world concept to see how far it can work with a single order set. Our
follow-up jev_experiment tests showed it working well, and the more we can reclaim as general jev flexibility versus
lingering heuristics the stronger the final system will be." On the queue: "earlier setups for jev couldn't handle
building things in the correct order and to the correct quantities. In hindsight that was the combinatorial problem.
One rapid decision a human has to make in bar is how to build a structure: does the commander start it? does a
construction bot? Who assists? If metal is running dry, do you pull one constructor off to start walking towards a
metal spot? ... The day we figure out how to have jev make those decisions, we should drop the queue and return
responsibility of that to jev. I am ok with us keeping the queue logic in, but it has to not get in the way ... Given
how fast we can write code now, I am honestly not worried about ripping out something that works fine in order to
simplify the question set we are asking right now."

Read as: every patch built because Jev, asked one actor at a time, could not sequence, count or coordinate (the
queue tool, the executor arms, the per-actor menus and their gating, the lab's one-order-ahead) is suspect by
construction now that the question shape is known to be the fault. Build the one-pass version to completion and

  over joined worlds with computed consequences 0.97 (§1) and 0.93-1.00 up to 128 worlds (§2); a two-pass pipeline
  (pre-pass nouls extracting per-group facts and the raid's need, code pruning the worlds, a post-pass Choice with
  consequences) 1.00 at 1024 worlds and 0.80 on the 729-world assignment where one bare pass managed 0.15 (§5);

- Live, the threat form of this (`threats.rs`, threats-smoke-1..5): gates on change, quiet seconds, picks that run
  as plans, hunts closing on quarries. Its faults were mechanical and fixed in a day.
- K-jev-a-lookup-table-counts: a count as a lookup table in the state's words is read; a threshold in prose is not.
- K-jev-a-packet-decompresses-to-standing-orders: a question in the packet's own idiom is answered at 0.97-0.99.
- K-hands-sequences-are-not-followed: the per-ask form built four extractors and three solars for "an extractor,
  then an extractor, then three solars, then the plant" because each ask was judged alone. That is the queue's

## 1. The shape

Three ideas, all already in the threat passes, now over every actor:

1. **Code enumerates, Jev picks.** Each second code lists, per actor, the action states it could execute now.
   Every fact is code's: distances, walking seconds, odds, counts, the store against a cost. Jev never computes.
2. **Two calls: relevance first, then one Choice.** The pre-pass asks nouls about which actors and which kinds of
   action matter this second, given the picture and the player's instructions. Code composes the worlds from what
   was flagged, each world with its consequence in words. The post-pass is one Choice over them.
3. **One plan, in force until the picture changes.** The pick is a plan for every actor at once. Code executes it
   to its end. Nothing is asked while the picture's signature holds; a change asks again.

The standing orders are pruning and defaults throughout, never a decider: a rule removes states or makes one the
base. The queue's lists are a bypass: a builder on a list is not in the pass while its list runs. No other path
depends on lists, so dropping the tool later is a tool removal, not a rebuild.

## 2. The state families

A state is executable by code alone and worded for Jev. Each actor has `keep` (index 0): what it does now, in
words ("keeps helping the plant since 1:40", "keeps building the plant, 40% done", "idle at home", "holds spot_45").

**Builders** (the commander, each constructor; a builder on a list is absent; a builder whose started build is
under 60% and unthreatened has only `keep`, H-HANDS-STARTED as pruning; at 60% and over its other states are
offered as what to do next, ordered behind the build, H-HANDS-QUEUE as before):

- `extractor@spot_N`: the nearest free spot by this builder's walking, and the nearest one no enemy is near when
  the nearest has enemies near: at most two. Words: walking seconds, ground, enemies near, our count after.
- `build:<kind>`: one per kind the allowance permits (else the faction's usual roster), placed by code as now
  (`build_site_for`); a kind placed at a place (a turret, a radar) is offered at the places code can justify: beside
  each extractor of ours within reach that no turret covers (nearest two), at a place the player marked with that
  kind's word. A sea kind only where there is water; a construction turret only beside a standing factory.
- `assist:<factory>` the nearest factory; `assist:<frame>` a build another builder has started within reach, one
  per frame, nearest two. This is "who assists".
- `reclaim` the richest field within reach, `repair` the nearest hurt building or commander, `retreat_home` when
  away, `attack:party_N` a party it outweighs alone within reach (H-HANDS-COMMANDER-FIGHTS as a state).
- `walk:<place>`: home, a place a rule or the player names for it. Not the map.

**Labs** (each factory with nothing waiting behind the unit being made; one order ahead, H-HANDS-MENU):
`keep` (the unit being made, or idle), `next:<unit>` per unit the allowance permits (else all), `nothing`. Words: our
count of that unit standing and being made, the soldiers we have.

**Groups**: `keep`; the threat states of `threats.rs` as built (whole, hunt, back per party within reach; the
engine's hunt underneath, H-MICRO-HUNT); `walk:<place>` and `advance:<place>` to the station, home, a place the
player marked or a rule names (not the diet's list of the map); `retreat`, `fall_back` (to the last hold, when
something calls for it, as now); `scout` (one out at a time); `split:N→<place>` for a marked or rule-named place;
`join:<group>`.

**Coupled pairs** (code's knowledge of the game, composed as one world): a `build` of one builder with an `assist`
of that frame by another; a `hunt` with the rest of the group keeping its course (as now). Nothing else is paired.

## 3. The pre-pass

One call, all nouls, over the picture and the player's instructions:

- Per actor with any state beyond `keep`: `<actor>.change`: "Given `actors.<actor>`, `economy`, `enemy` and the
  player's `instructions`: should <actor> do something other than what it does now (<keep words>)? Yes when the
  instructions call for a different job now, when what it does is finished or pointless, or when something near it
  needs answering. No when its course is what the instructions want and nothing has changed."
- Per kind of action any actor could take this second (the dimensions: extractor, generator, factory, turret, radar,
  assist, army unit, reposition, fight, back): `dim.<kind>`: "Given `economy`, `ours` and the player's
  `instructions`: is <kind words> what we should put the next metal and hands into now?" (battery J's dimension
  nouls). The kinds' words are fixed strings in code.
- Per threat, as now: `party_N.answer` and one per open state.

A state's rank is its actor's `change` times its dimension's noul (a threat state: the party's `answer` times the
state's own noul). `FLAG` 0.5 on the actor noul opens the actor; a dimension under `FLAG` closes its states
everywhere. Where the packet says exactly what stands (the hold packet's commander table: "with 2 extractors and 0
solar collectors: a solar collector"), the standing vocabulary does not carry it and the pass is not extended to
carry it: the counts are in the state's words as a lookup table and the `dim` nouls read the table against them.

## 4. Composition and consequences

World 1 is the base: every actor's `keep`, or, for an actor with a rule default and an idle course, the default
(the threat form's `current` else `default` else `keep`, for every family). Every other world is the base with one
actor's state changed, or one coupled pair changed. Ranked by the state's rank; capped at `CAP` 8 (the offline study
saw the pick diffuse past 8 over near-equivalent worlds; battery J held 1.00 to 128 with sharp consequences, so
`CAP` is a one-constant experiment once the smokes give p(top)). Joint changes across several actors accumulate
over successive asks: a change picked alters the signature, so the next second asks again over the new base.
Rejected: the full product of the flagged actors' states in one question (the assignment form, 0.73-0.80 in
battery J), because ours are near-equivalent worlds and one change a second is fast enough; reopen if the smokes
show the plan lagging a picture that changes faster than it is asked.

Each world's line is its moves, then the consequences code can compute, all already in words somewhere in the
present menus:

- a build: its cost against the store and the income ("620 metal against 340 stored and 4.1 a second coming in:
  starts slow"; energy draw as `build_draw_words`), what of that kind stands and is under way ("our 3rd extractor",
  "one plant being built at G4, 40% done"), the walk in seconds, what the builder leaves (a frame at N% that will
  decay, the plant's assist);
- an assist: whose frame, its progress, the build power added;
- a lab unit: our count of it, the army it joins;
- a group move: as `threats::consequence` now (met with what metal and odds, turrets counted; left to nobody);
- for every world, what is left unanswered: the threats to nobody, the free spots nobody takes when `dim.extractor`
  is open, an idle factory.

The question is one Choice: "which plan is best this second? w1 is what stands (the courses kept and the player's
standing rules); every other world changes one actor's course."

## 5. Execution and the signature

`apply_plan` runs the picked world: a builder state becomes its `Task` (build, assist, reclaim, repair, walk) and
the engine order; a lab state its production order; a group state as the threat form now (engage, hunt, walk,
retreat, split, scout, join). A state already `current` is untouched. The base world is applied every second
without a call, so a rule's default (the station walk, the standing job, a turret beside an uncovered extractor)
fires the second it is due.

The signature that gates the pre-pass: the threats' signature as now; per builder its task kind, whether its build
started or finished, whether it is idle; per lab whether it is free; the economy's stock words (the buckets of
`stock_words`, STALLING or not); the frame of the last packet or tool change; the scheduler's events (a hit, a
loss, a sighting, a party entering a builder's alarm reach); the pick itself. Unchanged for `RE_ASK` 20 s asks
anyway. A quiet second logs `quiet`.

## 6. The standing orders and the queue

Every rule of `GROUP_RULES` and `BUILDER_RULES` keeps its vocabulary and its decompression; each becomes pruning or
a default, none an order:

| rule | effect in the pass |
|---|---|
| `station`, `station_mode` | the group's default `walk`/`advance` there when away; `keep` at it |
| `raiders_lone/party`, `engage_party`, `no_chase`, `no_detachments`, `never`, `fall_back_to`, `hold_line`, `join` | as the threat form now: defaults and pruning of the threat states; `join` a default; `hold_line` prunes `back` |
| `job: help_factory` / `expand` / `follow_list` | the idle builder's default `assist` / `extractor@nearest` / the list |
| `attack_raiders` | `attack` the default when offered |
| `retreat_when_enemy_near` | `retreat_home` the default when a party it does not outweigh is within the alarm reach (a lone scout excluded, as now) |
| `solar: only_when_stalling` / `never` | generator states pruned unless STALLING / always |
| `turrets: beside_each_(outer_)extractor` / `none` | the turret state at the nearest uncovered (outer) extractor the default when idle / pruned |
| `never <places>` | those places pruned from every state |
| the `produce` allowance | the lab's and builders' kinds pruned to it |

A tool-set rule outranks the packet's, as now; the tool's synchronous check stays.

The queue: a builder with a list is not enumerated; its entry in `actors` says "on the player's list: next <step>".
The list's steps play as now (H-HANDS-SCRIPT). When the list ends the builder is back in the pass. The player's
`queue` tool stays; the packet README keeps its words. Nothing in the pass reads a list.

## 8. What Jev reads

The picture as now (`instructions`, `economy`, `ours`, `enemy`, `places`, `actors`), trimmed to the actors and
places in play (H-HANDS-DIET's places in play kept). Added: per actor its `keep` words; under `ours` the counts as a
lookup table ("extractors: 4 standing, 1 started; solar collectors: 3; plants: 1; constructors: 2, 1 being made;
soldiers: 6, 540 metal"); the free spots nobody has taken, nearest home first. The state is sent once per call
pair; the second call reuses it.

## 9. Validation

Read every game with the scorecard (`run/floor.py --ledger`) plus, per game: asks a minute, the quiet share, p(top)
median of the pick, flips (one actor given two different plans within 20 s), the bill.

1. **Smokes** (the pianist alone, easy, the hold packet `run/packets/comet-west-hold-1.md` with its lists as it
   stands): the mechanics: every family enumerated, gates on change, quiet seconds, picks applied, no actor played
   by two paths. The hold packet's curves are the yardstick (extractors and plant time by minute).
2. **The same packet with no lists**: the pass reads the commander's opening table itself. Extractors at 3:00, 5:00,
   10:00 and the plant's minute no worse than step 1's. This is the "drop the queue" test; it fails if the counts
   drift the way K-hands-sequences-are-not-followed recorded.
3. **The pianist alone against medium**, the hold packet: the hold games' 15-25 minutes matched or beaten.
4. **A player game** against hard_aggressive on standing-2's brief (claude2, usage snapshot first), read as worlds-2
   was: the player's notes on the hands, the raids' first minutes, the nest.
