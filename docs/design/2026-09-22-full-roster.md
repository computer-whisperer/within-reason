# The full roster: every unit of both factions reachable by the player, the hands and the policy

Written 2026-09-22 before the rebuild (the user: "the full reachable structure and unit set for both cortex and
armada should be filled in for the harness... Air units, naval units, t3, mid-game energy, etc are all going to start
being important soon. The only thing I see to manage carefully is if the option space is going to start overwhelming
jev -- but the whitelist tool should be able to tame that. I am less worried about opus, since the 1M context length
should be able to easily include the full unit set and game rule descriptions").

## What stands today

- The bot's `Kit` (`crates/bot/src/brain/roster.rs`) names 24 role units a faction (commander, extractor, solar, wind,
  lab, plant, turret, radar, nano, constructors, raider, line, skirmisher, artillery, tier-2 lab and its units,
  resurrector). The bot's own heuristics and the arena's other modes run on it.
- The builder menus (`menu.rs`) offer the Kit's buildings under role keys (`lab`, `vehicle_plant`, `wind_generator`,
  `solar_collector`, `converter`, `advanced_lab`, `construction_turret`, `turret_at`, `radar_at`) plus the extractor.
  Nothing else a builder can make is ever offered: no tier-2 energy, no air or sea factory, no advanced radar, no
  anti-air, no long-range turret, no storage.
- The factory menus offer every unit the factory's definition can build, keyed by the game's internal name (`armpw`),
  worded by `Picture::unit_words`: a role sentence for the Kit's units and the bare name for the rest.
- The player's `produce` tool whitelists what a factory may build (with counts); there is no equivalent for builders.
- The player's brief (`docs/briefs/player.md`) describes the tier-1 bots by hand; the role text names units by internal
  name. Enemy sightings are internal names with no words.
- The record header carries the game's whole definition list (587 defs): 190 are reachable from `armcom` by build
  lists, 187 from `corcom`. The largest single build list is 45 (the construction hovercraft); the constructor bot's
  is 30. Jev's cap of 255 options a question is never near.
- The game source is checked out at `upstream/Beyond-All-Reason` (shallow, master at 54199a0d, 2026-09-19); the arena
  plays `Beyond All Reason test-31362-baea311`, a build of the same week. `language/en/units.json` there has an English
  name and a one-line description for every reachable unit. `units/**/*.lua` has the numbers (health, weapons, ranges,
  damage, sight, build power) that the shim's `UnitDefInfo` does not carry.

## Decisions

1. **The build tree comes from the game, the words from a checked-in glossary.** What a builder can make is
   `UnitDefInfo::build_options` from `Hello`, as the factory menus already use. The words for a unit come from
   `crates/bot/data/units.json`, one entry per reachable unit of both factions:

   ```json
   "armpw": { "name": "Pawn", "faction": "armada", "tier": 1, "made_by": ["armlab"], "class": "raider bot",
              "metal": 54, "energy": 941, "health": 300, "speed": 90, "range": 180, "dps": 20, "sight": 400,
              "gloss": "fast light bot; kills builders, extractors and lone turrets, loses to line units",
              "prose": "Three to six sentences: what it is for, what it beats and loses to, when to build it, what
                        its numbers mean against the usual opponents, and anything the rules make special
                        (paralysis, cloak, EMP, transport, resurrection, submerged, needs energy to fire)." }
   ```

   The numbers, `name`, `made_by`, `tier` and `class` are generated by `run/unit_stats.py` from the game source (the
   Lua unit files and the language file), so a game update regenerates them; `gloss` and `prose` are written once, by
   a subagent reading the unit files and the game's wiki, and kept by hand. A unit in `Hello` with no entry gets its
   internal name and the generated numbers only, and a warning in the log.
2. **Internal names everywhere; the role keys go.** Builder options are keyed by the unit's internal name like the
   factory options, and the `queue` tool's steps, the policy's option names and the briefs name units the same way.
   `lab`, `vehicle_plant`, `wind_generator`, `solar_collector`, `converter`, `advanced_lab`, `construction_turret`,
   `turret_at`, `radar_at` are deleted first (menu, scripted steps, the policy role text's examples, the briefs), then
   the general form is built. Non-build actions keep their keys (`continue`, `wait`, `assist_lab`, `reclaim`, `repair`,
   `walk_to`, `retreat_home`, `attack`, and the extractor stays `extractor` with its spot question).
3. **Where a building goes is a property of the building, not of the option.** Extractors at a spot (`where_extractor`).
   Anything with a weapon or a radar, sonar or jammer at the place answered in `where`. Everything else in the base
   yard by the placement search the factories use today (it sizes by the definition's footprint). A sea unit
   (factory or building the definition places on water) is offered only when the placement search finds water within
   the builder's reach; `no_site` events stay the check that this works.
4. **The words on a Jev option are generated plus gloss.** For a building: "Build a Solar Collector (armsolar; 155
   metal, 20 energy a second; building it draws no energy) beside itself. We have 3 solar collectors and 2 wind
   generators standing or started. Our energy now: ...": the numbers from the definition, the gloss from the glossary,
   the situational sentence from the picture (counts of what stands, the factory count, the energy and metal words),
   kept exactly for the cases that have one today (generators, factories, converters, nano) and generalised by class
   for the rest (energy makers report the energy words; storage reports the store's fill; defences report what is
   near the place).
5. **Jev is tamed by a default list and the player's whitelist, not by the harness hiding units.** Each builder class
   has a default list of what is offered when the player has said nothing: the Kit's buildings plus the storage,
   the advanced radar, the long-range turret and the anti-air turret (about fourteen for a tier-1 constructor). The
   `produce` tool grows to cover builders (`commander`, `constructor_N`, `all_builders`, or `all`): a list names what
   is offered, with counts, replacing the default; `null` restores the default. The full build list is never put in
   front of Jev at once (K-jev-options-split-the-vote: six spot options lost to one alternative). The whitelist is the
   whole menu for that builder, so a player who wants a fusion reactor from the commander lists it.
6. **The policy reaches everything.** A policy order for a unit the builder can build is legal whether or not that
   unit is on the Jev menu; the menu gating is for Jev's sake only. `a.options` in the policy state carries the
   whole build list keyed by internal name (with the default list marked), so a script can dispatch on any of them.
7. **Opus gets the roster once a session, and details on demand.** The session's first report carries the faction's
   reachable roster as a table (name, internal name, made by, tier, metal, one gloss each: about 190 lines, 6,000
   tokens) and the opponent faction's the same way when it is known; the prose is behind a `units` tool that returns
   the full entry for any names asked, ours or theirs. The report's sightings and losses use the glossary's names
   ("2 Grunts (corak, raider bots)") so the picture reads the same as the roster.
8. **The Kit stays** for the bot's heuristics, the openings and the arena's non-player modes. The player, the hands
   and the policy no longer go through it for what may be built.

## What this does not decide

- Prose quality: the subagent's entries are a first draft to be corrected by play (every wrong gloss is a claim to
  retire in `docs/knowledge/units.md`).
- Tier-3 gantries, nuclear and anti-nuclear, long-range cannons and the like are offered when the build tree reaches
  them; nothing in the harness knows their use yet beyond the prose.
- Legion is out (not in the arena's games).

## Steps

1. `run/unit_stats.py`: from the game checkout, the generated part of `crates/bot/data/units.json` for every unit
   reachable from `armcom` and `corcom` by build lists (the record header of a recent game is the reachability
   check). Records the checkout commit in the file.
2. The glossary prose, by a general-purpose subagent with the game files and the wiki as data (`gloss` and `prose`
   for all 377 units); verified by sampling against the unit files and the duel claims in `docs/knowledge/units.md`.
3. Delete the role keys (menu, scripted steps, policy examples, briefs, tests). Builder menus from the build list and
   the default list; the `produce` tool over builders; placement by property; sea gating.
4. `units` tool; the roster table on the session's first report; glossary names in sightings and losses.
5. The policy state's full build list and the legality rule.
6. Tests: every reachable building of a faction is offered to some builder under a whitelist naming it; the default
   list without one; a sea factory is not offered on Comet; the `queue` tool takes internal names; the policy may
   order an off-menu buildable unit.
7. One policy game and one Jev game on Comet against easy with the new menus, read by `run/floor.py` and
   `run/jev_audit.py --section options`, before the roster is used in anger.
