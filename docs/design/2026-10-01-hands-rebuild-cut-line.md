# The hands' rebuild: the cut line, row by row (2026-10-01)

Companion to `2026-10-01-hands-rebuild.md`. Every place where code chooses, caps, infers, holds or acts without a pick
today (the inventory, `docs/studies/2026-10-01-hands-inventory.md` §4a to §4d, 88 rows), with what the rebuild does
with it. Proposed; nothing is ruled until the user rules.

- **goes**: deleted; the move it hid or chose is on the menu under the laws.
- **words**: the fact it keyed on is said on the move's line or in the picture; nothing is removed.
- **stays (date)**: the user's ruling or design of that date.
- **stays, never ruled**: kept because it carries a picked move or a list step out, or is geometry below a move, or
  is the cost of asking. Each is the user's to strike.
- **to rule**: I do not know which way the user wants it; my proposal is given.
