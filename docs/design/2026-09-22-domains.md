# Domains: aircraft, hovercraft and ships in the hands, the lane and the routes

Written 2026-09-22 before the rework, from the review `docs/studies/2026-09-22-micro-air-sea-review.md` (the user:
"proceed with the basic rework and rot cleanup"). The goal is the minimum that makes the three objective games a fair
test of the roster (kill the commander with Stormbringers, with Bulls, with Crocodiles and Possums), plus the rot the
review found beside it. Proper air micro (altitude, pads, gunship kiting) is left for games to teach.

## Decisions

1. **A domain on every definition**, read off it in `World`: `Air` (mobile, no move class), `Hover`, `Ship`,
   `Ground` (tank or bot), by `MoveKind`; buildings are `Ground`. Nothing asks the Kit.
2. **Aircraft skip the 10 Hz lane entirely** until it has air rules: not among the lane's soldiers, not in its
   standing orders. Hovercraft and ships stay in the lane (their footwork is a tank's) on their own fields (5).
3. **Groups have a domain.** A group is born with its first member's domain; a newcomer joins only a group of its
   own domain; holding groups merge only within a domain; `join_group_X` is offered only across the same domain.
   Aircraft never take part in the march.
4. **Air groups hold by move state, not by Stop.** `Command::MoveState { unit, state }` is added to the protocol
   (the engine's `CMD_MOVE_STATE`: 0 hold position, 1 manoeuvre, 2 roam). An air group told to hold gets hold
   position and a move to its centre, so the engine's automatic target hunting (`AirCAI`, radius 1000 × move state)
   is off; one given an order gets manoeuvre back. Ground groups are untouched. A protocol change: the user
   re-runs `run/install_to_bar.sh` before lobby play.
5. **`attack_unit` is a group order for everyone.** With `whom` a party, every member gets `Command::Attack` on the
   party's commander if it has one, else its dearest member. `GroupTask::Engage` carries `target: Option<UnitId>`;
   follow re-issues the attack while the target is seen and the group holds when it is lost. An air group's
   `engage` is the same attack (a fight order makes a bomber bomb the nearest thing on its line); its `fight_to`
   stays a fight order (it bombs what it passes).
6. **The odds know what can hit what.** The simulator's `Unit` gets `reach_air`/`dps_air` beside the ground pair
   (a weapon hits air unless its target category is `SURFACE`, `NOTAIR`, `MINE`; a `VTOL`-only weapon hits only
   air). `Force` counts turret metal that hits ground and turret metal that hits air separately;
   `known_enemy_force` sorts each armed building by what it can hit; `power` uses the turret metal that can hit
   the opposing force's domain and drops the units that cannot hit it. `odds_words` says "it cannot hit us" and
   "we cannot hit it" when one side has nothing for the other's domain. The lane's threat grid stays one grid
   for ground (aircraft are out of the lane); an air grid comes with air rules.
7. **The simulator's table covers every reachable unit.** `extract_units.py` follows build lists from both
   commanders as `run/unit_stats.py` does, instead of naming four factories; the bot's `sim_stats` falls back to
   the glossary's range, damage and speed for a name the table lacks, never to a stand-in by metal.
8. **Routes per movement class.** `Walker` becomes `Air` or a movement class (kind, slope, depth), with fields
   built lazily on the survey thread for every class among the units the commander reaches; the soldiers' fields
   (the raider's class) remain the default for the picture's "our half", passages and the sketch. Snapping,
   reachability and travel time take the unit's or the group's walker; for `Air` they return the point, true, and
   the straight line at the unit's speed.
9. **Placement:** a factory of any kind gets the factory gap; an `on_water` building is anchored at the nearest
   under-water cell within the builder's reach.
10. **The record's class** tells `aircraft`, `air_builder`, `hover` and `ship` from `army` and `builder`.
11. **Rot:** the mark warning that could never fire is replaced by a check against the mark's own snap distance
    per domain; the builder `attack` offer needs a weapon; `Pick::Scout` prefers any raider-class unit by the
    glossary rather than the Kit's one.

## Status (2026-09-22, built)

All eleven decisions built on branch `roster` (H-HANDS-DOMAINS, K-engine-aircraft-hunt-with-an-empty-queue). The
simulator's table went from 156 to 374 units; its tests pass (a chase test that had not compiled since
`pursuer_buildings` was added is fixed). `Command::MoveState` is a protocol addition: re-run `run/install_to_bar.sh`
before lobby play. Untested in play until the objective games run.

**2026-09-22 evening, evidence-1-bombers:** the air hold cancels the strike (K-hands-air-hold-cancels-the-strike):
the hold's move and move-state orders are re-issued every lane tick and whenever the followed target leaves sight,
so 233 bombers made 220 attacks and 4,000 moves and never hurt the commander. The next pass, built the same evening (H-HANDS-AIR-TARGET):

12. **The hold is issued once.** A group's hold orders go out on entering the hold (a task change, a give-up, a lost
    party), never again on a repeated `hold` pick: a holding group picked `hold` again gets no orders. Before, an
    air group got a move and a move state per member every ask.
13. **An air group keeps its target.** With a named target the attack order stands while the target is seen; when it
    leaves sight the group is not held: after six seconds it is sent once with a fight order to the target's last
    position (the search), and only after sixty seconds out of sight does it hold. A target seen again after being
    lost gets the attack order again at once. Ground groups keep the old rule (hold six seconds after the party is
    lost), since a ground group holding does not die.

## Not in this pass

Air footwork (flee from anti-air, pad returns, gunship kiting), two threat grids, mixed-domain groups on purpose,
ships on maps with sea lanes (the fields exist after 8; nothing plans for them).
