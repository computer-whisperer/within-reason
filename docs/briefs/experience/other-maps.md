## Other maps: what we have seen there

Much less than on Comet Catcher: a handful of games each, most against one person. Each map's sheet is in the map
sheets; set it beside Comet Catcher's before carrying a number across.

### Quicksilver Remake (half water, the starts 3,400 apart, wind 3 to 17)

- **The ground.** 38 of the 44 spots can be walked to; six are on islets. The nearest spots are on and just below
  the start plateau; the middle rows change hands all game.
- **Energy.** People who play it: the wind averages 12; "about twenty energy for every metal spent"; "two
  constructors is very greedy on this map" (human-3, human-6). A lab draws about 80 energy a second while it is
  built, and a wind generator costs energy to build.
- **A strong player's first three minutes** (Matt, a replay): two extractors beside the start (0:03, 0:10), three
  wind generators (0:16 to 0:28), the lab at 0:33, standing 0:50, with energy at 490 and rising when it started.
  From 0:50 the commander helped the lab; at 0:53 it queued seven wind generators and a solar and built them back
  to back by 1:30, one every six seconds; from 1:36 it helped the lab for good. The lab made one constructor first,
  then Pawns: the first at 1:12, one every eleven seconds alone and every four to fourteen with the commander
  helping; a second constructor at 1:45, after six Pawns. His first far extractor stood at 1:45, its turret at
  2:32. The raiders gathered at home into a handful and went for extractors and builders from about 2:30. The store
  dipped to 15 and was full again by 1:35; the metal bank was at 8 by 2:00. His commander did not leave the base.
- **Ours against people there.**
  - human-1 (the first game against a person): Pawns at 2:10, straight at the field constructors; we had no army;
    all builders dead by 2:55, the lab by 4:46. The lab had made three constructors while three wind generators
    ran, and energy sat at zero from 1:00.
  - human-3: the lab before any generator; the store at zero from 0:46 to 1:54, the first constructor 43 s in the
    making, no soldier before 3:01, the lab dead at 3:03.
  - human-5: a second constructor at 1:15 put the first Pawn at 1:40, thirty seconds behind Matt's. The commander
    built its generators one at a time, eight and a half seconds each against his six, and began helping the lab at
    2:24 against his 1:36.
  - human-8: Matt's opening minute for minute, ten Grunts by 2:43, and lost: the Grunts traded one for one with
    Pawns and died in ones and twos away from the commander (eight by 3:14, all 380 to 1,231 from it).
- **What players said there.** "30 pawns before mace." "Moving the commander around costs build power." "About 20
  energy for every metal, and more than one or two constructors makes defending hard."
- **Against BARb medium** (the pianist-player series): twelve Blitzes of his held us at 0 to 3 extractors for
  fifteen minutes in one game; in another 25 Maces walked through fourteen artillery pieces and seven towers to his
  commander at 8:51 from an army of 3,200 against 870 seen.

### Cape Violet V1 (10,240 by 5,120, east against west, 40% sea, wind 8 to 20)

- **The ground.** 72 spots: 44 reachable on foot from the east strip, 28 under water. The land ways west run
  through two passages (D2 and B3); the sea runs round both.
- **One game with a person** (thebluegecko, three seats of ours on the east strip, lost 18:18): he took every
  under-water spot with construction ships and advanced submarines by 10:00; we took none. He brought Welders
  (amphibious tanks) over the water into our strip from 12:00; they broke on 40 Stouts at spot_29 at 13:00. 6,800
  metal of our Blitzes died in the Beamer camp at B3 at 8:45. Liche bombers killed two commanders at 17:07, and
  Longbow cruisers (range 1,550) shelled from 17:49.
- **human-16** (2026-10-03, three seats against a person): Incisors standing in water 10 to 20 deep had an enemy
  in their reach for 86 unit-seconds and fired in 2% of them.

### Water (SailAway 2: 10,240 square, wind 5 to 20, the seats on islets)

- **How the sea works** (the engine's rules). A submarine, a ship's hull below the line and a commander walking the
  seabed are seen by sonar only and hit by torpedoes and depth charges only. A shooter under the surface hits only
  what is in the water; a commander under water shoots nothing. A tidal generator (`armtide`, 90 metal) gives a
  steady rate the map sets and is built on the water. A spot's line says "under water" (a construction ship or an
  amphibious constructor takes it) or "on ground our walkers cannot reach" (an islet).
- **Reach at sea.** Riptide frigate `corpship` 480; Dolphin `armdecade` 280; Ellysaw `armpship` 500; Longbow
  cruiser 1,550.
- **Game 11** (three seats against thebluegecko, resigned 24:33): a seat on an islet had three of 96 spots
  reachable on foot. One commander that started at sea walked to a shore for a solar. One seat's army was thirteen
  Mangonels to four line hovers; his Riptides and Buccaneers beat it. thebluegecko: "it is better than barbs at
  water. barbs are bad at water." The host: "I am told hovers are bad generically."
- **Game 12** (resigned 14:47): shipyards on every seat by 1:09. Every yard was held to Dolphins and Supporters by
  a `produce` list of 4:09 that was never changed, while the report said from 5:25 that his Riptides "outrange
  everything here (480 against our 280)"; the first Ellysaw came at 10:05, after the fleet was gone. The sonar
  tower was ordered at 12:40. His seaplane platform was seen at 9:05 and our first anti-air stood at 14:05. The ten
  construction ships built tidal generators, floating converters, torpedo launchers and three extractors between
  them.

### Coast To Coast (6,144 by 4,096, water, 24 metal spots, wind 5 to 20, tidal 16)

- **One game, human-17** (2026-10-03, three seats of ours against thebluegecko, lost at 16:00; a map we had not
  played, and the user's word at 0:36: "this is a tiny map with water"). One seat built a hover platform and the
  other two a shipyard each. At 8:00 the seats had 7, 2 and 1 extractors and 16, 6 and 4 metal a second; at 16:00,
  3, 0 and 0. The hover seat made 13 Crocodiles, 13 Possums (the rocket hover) and 3 Seekers and lost 40 units;
  the two shipyard seats made three construction ships each and eight and seven warships. Told at 13:29 to make
  resurrection submarines; at 14:07 we said his destroyers owned the water.
- **thebluegecko afterwards:** "Hovers are good for harass, bad for army bulk. Only build a handful of the rocket
  ones. The harass units needed to not exist or be harassing the enemy."
- With 24 spots for four seats, a seat with one or two extractors for eight minutes had no economy to build
  anything with; SailAway's game 12 had the same shape (every seat a yard before expanding, 14 extractors at 8:00).

### Great Divide V1 (3,072 by 4,096, north against south, no water, wind 0 to 20)

- **The ground.** Rows 1 to 3 and 6 to 8 are open; rows 4 and 5 are a cliff from edge to edge with one pass at E5,
  700 to 840 wide where the ways between the starts are 1,600 to 1,800 wide at the median, 55 to 65% of the way
  across. 22 spots, eleven a side; the twelfth of the north's count, spot_11 at E5, sits at the pass's south mouth.
  Everything that walks between the halves goes through the pass.
- **Reach, for a camp at a pass.** Light turret 430, Beamer 490, heavy turret 620, Pit Bull 730, Gauntlet 1,220,
  Cortex Punisher 1,245; Shellshocker 710, Pillager `armmerl` 1,300 (advanced vehicle plant), Wolverine 710, Tremor
  1,470; flak 850, Ferret 950.
- **What we are told people do there:** a camp at the pass (light turrets by 3:00, heavy by 6:00, then Pit Bulls or
  Gauntlets with artillery behind) and the army waiting behind the guns. An experienced player's word: an all-air
  side loses.
- **Ours.** Three team games of the old bot (2026-09-20) sat at home on five extractors for 35 minutes and never
  went to the pass. The games with people there since have not been read into cases yet.
- **On Comet Catcher, the nearest thing to that camp:** the nest at E3 with a Gauntlet took 9,000 and 11,000 metal
  of tier-1 tanks in two games (16k lost for 6k, then 10k for 1k).
