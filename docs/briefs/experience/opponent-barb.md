## How this opponent decides (BARb)

From its own configuration and our games against it; the table of what it has by the minute follows from the truth
files. It has no resource or vision cheats.

- **Raids.** Its raiders gather at its base and leave as a squad of about four; after that every new raider joins
  the raid at once. They go for buildings where the threat is low, builders and extractors first, the outermost
  first, and skip a target whose defence is worth more than about three quarters of the squad.
- **Turrets.** It builds a turret beside nearly every extractor, and forward turret nests in our half later. Its
  base gets turrets on a clock.
- **Its army** is kept at home as one block and sent when it judges itself stronger than what it has seen of ours;
  the block walks to our nearest weakly defended cluster. After a wave of ours dies in its half it resurrects the
  wrecks, and they fight for it.
- **It counter-builds** against what it has seen: riot units against raiders, assault units against static defence.
- **Its commander** builds at home; killing it ends the game. In one easy game it walked into our half with a party
  and died to a group of ours that outweighed it.
- **It techs on a clock,** whatever we do (the table below).
