## What happened in games: cases

These are situations from recorded games, on every map we have played, with what was done and what came of it. None
of them is an instruction. Each says which map it was on, because the same situation on other ground can go the
other way: set the case's map against the map sheet of the one you are on. Where the same situation went two ways,
both are here, and the difference between them is the thing to look at. A person's words are quoted and named; our
own reading of a record is marked as ours. The reference tables say what experienced players' games look like in
numbers; these cases say what particular decisions cost or bought.

Names: `player-N`, `escalate-N`, `comet-N` are our duels against the AI opponent BARb on Comet Catcher (flat, open,
vehicles, starts 5,900 to 7,300 apart). `bluegecko-3v1-N` are three seats of ours against one person (thebluegecko,
OS about 48), mostly on Comet Catcher. `human-N` and `pianist-player-N` are on Quicksilver (half water, starts 3,400
apart, wind) unless said. "The pool" is 80 sides of duels between people at OS 40 and above on Comet Catcher.

### The first soldiers and his half

**The pool.** Every side had a soldier in his half by 2:06 at the median (Armada 1:40, Cortex 2:48). Every side but
one killed a building of his, the first at 3:49 (Armada 2:48). Winners were 40 s earlier than losers, with 78 of
their 280 army metal in his half at 3:00. The same pressure came back at them: they lost 1.85 extractors a
game-minute, and won.

**What the first soldiers met there at 2:00 to 4:00:** a commander and constructors out on spots, with one or two
light turrets among them.

**Cases.**
- player-40 to player-45 (Comet): the first soldiers were written as a guard for our own strip ("the raid answer for
  our strip", "enemy parties far away in his half are not its business"). The first building of his died at 3:10,
  never in eleven minutes, 9:24, 5:56 and 8:20. In those games the report's line on his army read "at least that
  and may be much more": nothing of ours had been there to see.
- sonnet-low-1 (Comet, lost 20:05): the first eight soldiers and then all 31 walked a route through the empty
  south-east corner. First contact was at 7:27. His factory was never seen. His army at 10:00 was 4,510; in
  player-52 on the same seed, with soldiers in his half from 2:21, it was 1,681 (our reading: nobody was costing him
  anything).
- player-52 (Comet, won 25:26): first soldier in his half 2:21, first building of his killed 3:17, 13 of his units
  and buildings dead by 8:00.

**What players said.** "The first handful of units need to run to the other side of the map to harass the opponent.
In an RTS like this the open space of what the enemy may have is so broad that you need some kind of pressure to
stop them from extremely greedy economic openings." (experienced players, relayed by the user, 2026-10-03)

### A lead in army, and what was done with it

**The pool.** A Comet Catcher duel ends 3.4 minutes (median) after one side first holds twice the other's army.

**Cases.**

  unseen. The order was a raid loop through six of our own spots, and two more constructors. By 8:00 he had the
  bigger army. His base was not seen in 36 minutes. (the user: "look even earlier -- 6:00")
- player-10 (Comet, lost 29:06): Rovers had his lab in sight at 3:45 with our army at 4.1 times his. The next packet
  made the Blitzes a home guard that "never goes east of spot_59". The lead was spent at 8:27 on a push after his
  commander into a turret line at F3: 18 of 20 Blitzes dead by 10:46.
- escalate-7 (Comet, won 16:00): one body gathered at spot_39/24 with pickets on the flanks, broke his block at D4 at
  11:33, took the far-north route past the E2/E3 turret nest to G1, and went onto his commander the moment it was
  seen.
- pianist-player-3 (Quicksilver against BARb medium, won 11:00): at 8:51, with the army at 3,200 against 870 seen
  and 17 extractors, the whole block was committed by one route. 25 Maces walked through fourteen artillery pieces
  and seven towers to the commander.
- pianist-player-2 (Quicksilver, lost 27:00 with a hundred soldiers by 18:00): three waves went at his base one
  after another, and a body of 82 stood five minutes under artillery.
- player-18 (Comet): five launches from 10:56 with two to three times his army. Three went at the southern end of
  his strip, the spots the Rovers had passed on their way in, which was empty: "found little (killed an LLT and an
  extractor)". His base at G1-G2 had been in the picture since 5:00. Each launch was turned back within two minutes
  by a party of his in our middle (a 1,200-metal party, a fifth of his army at 11:00), with the body strung out: at
  13:29, 12 of 37 were in the fight and the tail was 3,700 back.
- player-50 (Comet, called at 33:21): army five to ten times his from 13:00, standing at D5-E5 in the middle; his
  base was not attacked.
- sonnet-medium-1 (Comet, lost 27:20): army 1.5 to 2.9 times his from 12:00 to 22:00, none of it in his half in

In these games the size of the lead did not separate the wins from the losses (player-9's was 2.4 to 1 at 6:00,
player-10's 4.1 to 1 at 4:00). Where the body stood did: in the wins it was in his half, together, on a route
something of ours had already travelled, within about two minutes of the report showing the lead (our reading).

**What players said.** "Up by almost 400% army value, time to go fight and win; if you dont use that army advantage
this will soon go bad." (thebluegecko, spectating the 3v1s of 2026-09-27)

### How many of one unit

**The pool** (`docs/studies/2026-09-28-body-sizes.md`). The largest body of one type in a typical game is a dozen
raiders or line tanks, eight Stouts, seven of a long-range type, four Maces or Pounders. Bodies over thirty come

### Tier 2: his and ours

Little here is settled. Duels between people at this level on Comet Catcher end at 12 to 15 minutes at the median,
and 4 of 80 sides ever started an advanced factory, so the pool does not say when it pays. What follows is the few
cases we have and what people said. The arithmetic of what tier 2 costs and returns is in the economy tables.

**BARb on Comet Catcher** starts an advanced factory at 10:41 at the median (9:55 to 11:44), in 73 of 74 games that
lasted fifteen minutes, whatever we did. His army's value falls or stands still while he pays for it (median 3,302
at 12:00, 2,852 at 16:00), then doubles every five minutes from 20:00 (5,069, then 9,389 at 25:00 and 16,360 at
30:00) as Fatboys, Sharpshooters and Razorbacks arrive.

**Cases.**
- player-9 (Comet): his advanced lab started at 10:32 and his army fell from 2,736 to 2,220 by 12:00 while ours rose
  to 6,565. Our mass stood at D5-D6 in our own half. The lab was never seen, and its Fatboys, Sharpshooters and a
  Razorback ended the game.
- player-10 and player-11 (Comet): his lab at 11:42 and 12:10. Both times we started our own; it stood at 14:15,
  with our energy at 0 at 11:37 to pay for it. Both lost.
- bluegecko-3v1-9 (Comet, a person): we stayed tier 1; 94 tier-1 tanks died to 15 Bulls.
- bluegecko-3v1-10 (Comet, lost 26:10): our army was a mass of Lashers. "You made many lashers, so I was safe to go
  T2." His Tzars and Tigers beat them. On our side one seat's advanced plant sat at 0% from 15:40 until it was
  abandoned at 19:15, with one constructor on it, while that seat banked 1,432 metal; no metal was sent between
  seats all game.
- bluegecko-3v1-12: three transfers between seats, a dying seat's units and 700 metal to a live one at 11:41 to
  12:13.

**What players said.**
- "Two seats can gift resources to one seat, that seat can build t2, then share t2 constructors to let advanced
  mexes go up." (the user, 2026-09-27)
- "The top player had only one constructor applying build power to it ... Two players gifting to one doesn't help
  if that player doesn't have the build power to push it through." (the user, 2026-09-28)
- "Top should stop building other units while doing t2 transition." (thebluegecko, game 10)
- In a close duel the tech is a weak point: his is the moment to find his base. Past about 25:00, with more than
  two players, or against an opponent that techs on a clock whatever happens, that weak point is covered. (the
  user, 2026-09-28)

### Income past the spot count

**Arithmetic (Armada; the unit files).**

| Source | Metal | Energy to build | Gives |
|---|---|---|---|
| Extractor on a Comet spot | 50 | 500 (3 a second to run) | 2.3 metal a second |
| Advanced extractor over it (an advanced constructor builds it) | 620 | 7,700 (20 a second to run) | four times the spot: 9.2 on Comet |
| Basic solar | 155 | 0 | 20 energy a second |
| Advanced solar (a tier-1 constructor builds it; the commander cannot) | 350 | 5,000, drawn over the build | 80 energy a second |

The advanced solar gives the energy of four basic ones for 44% less metal and 24% less build time. Its 5,000 energy
is 62 seconds of its own output; one constructor vehicle alone builds it in 88 s at about 57 energy and 4 metal a
second. With the energy store empty it crawls.

**Cases.**
- player-42 (Comet): 27 to 35 extractors held from 15:00; metal income never passed 128 a second.
- human-10 (three seats): 35 extractors and 70 metal a second at 12:00. The opponent: "not a unit choice problem, a
  not enough mex problem".
- player-49 (Comet, won 15:01): 39 basic solars, 25 of them after 8:00, and no advanced one: about 1,700 metal more
  than the same energy from advanced solars.
- player-52 (Comet, won 25:26): nine advanced solars from 8:21.
- A game whose advanced solars were started at 18:22 into an empty energy store: they did not finish for minutes.

**What players said.** "We need to be building a lot more t2 mexes and advanced solar." (the user, 2026-10-02) "We
keep using just basic solar collectors." (the user, 2026-10-03)

### The first three minutes: energy and metal

**The pool, Comet Catcher, Armada.** 22 of 41 sides: extractor, extractor, solar, solar, vehicle plant, the plant
started at 0:34. The plant's first units: a Blitz, about five Rovers, then the constructor at 1:30.

**Arithmetic.** A vehicle constructor costs 1,950 energy. The plant alone draws 48 energy a second making one; with
the commander helping, about 220. Three solars and the commander make 90. A Rover costs 370 energy: the helped plant
makes one every 3 s. The start's 1,240 energy pays for one helped constructor.

**Cases.**
- human-3 (Quicksilver): the lab before any generator; the store was empty from 0:46 to 1:54, the first constructor
  took 43 s, no soldier before 3:01. In a strong player's replay on that map (Matt): one solar, then wind, the lab
  at 0:33, the commander helping it from 0:50.
- human-5 (Quicksilver): a second constructor at 1:15 put the first Pawn at 1:40, thirty seconds behind that
  replay.
- comet-3 (Comet): six solars before anything else, at an income of 6. The metal store sat at zero from minute two
  to thirteen and the plant made 28 Blitzes in seventeen minutes.
- player-12 (Comet, lost 26:28): the plant's list began with two constructors back to back under the commander's
  help. The second emptied the energy store at 1:30 (drain 223 against 90). The answer at 1:45 was four solars, 620
  metal at an income of 6 to 8, so the metal store sat at 0 from 2:04 to 3:10 while the energy store was full again
  from 2:15. Three extractors stood at 3:00 (six the game before on the same seed), two at 4:00 after single raiders
  took four at home with no turret affordable, nine at 8:00 against his fifteen.

**What players said.** The commander should be assisting the lab when it first comes up. (thebluegecko, game 10)
