## Several seats: what happened in team games

Games in which one player of ours commanded two or three seats. The `bluegecko-3v1-N` games were three seats on
Comet Catcher's east strip against one experienced person (thebluegecko, OS about 48; game 4 against Irishstud14,
OS 48, Cortex). `human-N` games are with people on the map named. Each seat has its own economy, builders and
commander; the tools that cross seats are `transfer` and a shared packet. As in the duels: what was done and what
came of it, on that ground.

### One person against three seats: what he did

- bluegecko-3v1-1 (lost 21:00, from 31 extractors and 65 soldiers at 12:00): single Rovers and Blitzes at our outer
  spots from 2:06. Stouts and Janus from 7:47; one block of ten to twenty Stouts at the nearest seat's home by
  13:00. Thunder bombers and then Banshees from 17:00. He scouted throughout; we never saw his commander or
  factories.
- bluegecko-3v1-2 (lost about 20:30, the trade 56.7k to 33.5k in our favour): Janus and Stouts against our Blitzes;
  a Bull and an air plant by 18:32.
- bluegecko-3v1-3 (lost 26:00, with 110k destroyed against 77k lost, 34 extractors and 9.4k of army at 10:45): twenty
  Banshees at 14:00, a Mauser shelling from out of sight at 16:00, Bulls at 18:30, a Razorback at 25:00. Nothing of
  ours reached or out-gunned them; we stayed tier 1.
- bluegecko-3v1-4 (Irishstud14, lost about 20:30, the trade 85k to 30k in our favour): Pounders stopped our push at
  his base at 8:00. From 11:00 Incisor swarms of ten to fifteen slipped past our Brute body and took our extractors
  from 33 to 9 by 19:00, while we won every fight we had.
- human-15 (2026-10-03, Comet Catcher, three seats against thebluegecko): not yet read.

In three of these we were ahead on units traded when we lost.

### The seats' armies

- bluegecko-3v1-1: the north seat lost 17 extractors from 2:06 and answered none; its Blitzes stood in one body far
  from the spots. The north commander died at 14:29 while the middle seat's body of 50 Blitzes sat at spot_48 for
  six minutes and then chased side parties.
- bluegecko-3v1-2: the seats' groups broke his second wave when they fought together on our turrets at 13:19 to
  13:58. Every loss came when one group met him alone. Two dives of Blitzes at his commander cost about twenty to
  its D-gun (5:33, 8:23). Eleven Blitzes were lost to Janus arriving alone at 12:09.
- bluegecko-3v1-3: the seats' groups reached spot_24, spot_37 and spot_60 one at a time all game, and each was worn
  down alone. Four attack orders were refused because the party's name in the packet had changed since the picture
  it was read from; the hands attack what stands at a place.
- bluegecko-3v1-1: Shellshockers walked ahead of the tanks that were to screen them and were pulled back.

### The commanders

- bluegecko-3v1-2: a commander told to stay home died at home when the block arrived (15:51, 19:13).
- bluegecko-3v1-1: the north commander died at 14:29 with the nearest friendly body six minutes away.

### Air against several seats

- Cape Violet (below): two commanders died to Liche bombers at 17:07; one bomb kills a commander.

### Tier 2 with several seats

- bluegecko-3v1-10: one seat's advanced plant sat at 0% from 15:40 until it was abandoned at 19:15, with one
  constructor on it and that seat's store at 1,432; a second constructor given a list naming the plant at 18:07
  started a second frame 224 away. No metal was sent between seats in that game or in game 8. The seat's other
  plants kept producing until 17:39, when thebluegecko said to stop them.
- bluegecko-3v1-12: three transfers, a dying seat's units and 700 metal to a live one at 11:41 to 12:13.
- human-10 (three seats): 35 extractors and 70 metal a second at 12:00.

What the people said about it is in "What players said" under tier 2.

### The opening with several seats

- human-10: the lobby set the factions at the start, the lists were written with internal names, and so they
  waited for the first report; the hands played the packet's prose for twenty seconds and built a lab at 0:10 on
  two seats. Role words (`solar`, `plant`) are filled in by each seat's faction and can be written before it is
  known.
- human-11: four constructors in a row went to help the lab at birth. Two of them were under a paragraph saying
  "constructors: work their lists" and had no list. The lab's line in the picture names the unit on its pad and the
  name it will have, and a `queue` for that name waits for it.
- game 12 on SailAway 2: every seat built both a shipyard and a hover platform before expanding; the side stood at
  14 extractors at 8:00 against 51 the game before. (the user: "One problem with that match was lack of
  coordination.")
- bluegecko-3v1 game 7: "The human player is taking mexes so much faster that 3v1 is about fair." (u6bkep)

### Writing one packet for several seats

- human-12: the sentence "It attacks party_8 (the Centurions at home, spot_13, spot_11) with the whole group until
  they are dead", followed by "Against a party that outweighs it, it walks to spot_11 and holds", had 28 Maces
  against two Centurions walking to spot_11 and back every other second for a minute while the lab died. Asked again
  offline with "group_G1 attacks party_8 now with the whole group and keeps attacking until it is dead" as a
  sentence of its own, the hands kept the attack. The hands read every place named in a sentence as a place to go.
  Where the sentence sat in the packet made no difference.
