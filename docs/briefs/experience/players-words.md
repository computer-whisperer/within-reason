## What players said

Their words, as they wrote them in the game chat or to the user afterwards, with who said it and the game it was
said in. thebluegecko and Irishstud14 are about OS 48; u6bkep about OS 19. The games were three seats of ours
against one of them on Comet Catcher unless said (2026-09-27 and 28). The full lines are in
`docs/knowledge/_inbox/players-chat-2026-09-27.md`.

**On the opening**
- "The first handful of units need to run to the other side of the map to harass the opponent. In an RTS like this
  the open space of what the enemy may have is so broad that you need some kind of pressure to stop them from
  extremely greedy economic openings." (experienced players, relayed by the user, 2026-10-03)
- The commander should be assisting the lab when it first comes up. (thebluegecko, after game 10)
- "Two constructors is very greedy on this map." (a player on Quicksilver, human-6)
- About twenty energy for every metal spent. (players on Quicksilver, human-3, as a rule of thumb for that map,
  where wind averages 12)

**On economy and build power**
- "This is a metal heavy map." (on Comet Catcher, about energy converters)
- "Orange and red both need nanos." "You dont have enough build power."
- "Get that reclaim asap." "Eat as you push." "Eat your bot lab."
- "The human player is taking mexes so much faster that 3v1 is about fair." (u6bkep, game 7, 14:11)
- "Not a unit choice problem, a not enough mex problem." (the opponent, human-10)

**On pressure and raids**
- "An aggressive response is needed to prevent a large metal lead."
- "Spread out for the raid, attack both corners." "Focus on mexes and solars." "Try top corner, not much defence
  there."

**On unit mix**
- "Too many lashers lose to med tanks." "3 med tanks per pounder."
- "I think you made too many lashers." (thebluegecko, game 10, 24:09)
- "This isn't a static problem, it is adversarial, that is, you made many lashers, so I was safe to go T2."
  (thebluegecko, game 10, 25:10)
- "Brutes would have given you the ability to apply more pressure." (thebluegecko, game 10)
- "Mongonals are better than t1 arty because they outrange things. They can't get in a straight fight, so you want
  1-3 of them with other units in front. Same with t1 artillery -- it shouldn't be the primary unit body."
  (thebluegecko, after game 11, on water)
