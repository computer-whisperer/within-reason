## The sea: what people do on it

### Sea duels above OS 40: the shipyard inside the first minute

Sixteen sides of eight duels on SailAway 2 and Coast To Coast, OS 43 to 66.

- *The first factory.* A shipyard for ten sides, begun between 0:11 and 1:19 (0:54 at the median). An aircraft plant
  for five (0:49 to 0:57), whose shipyards followed at 2:55 to 6:11; a hovercraft platform for one (0:58), with
  shipyards at 3:01 and 4:35. No side opened with a bot lab or a vehicle plant; three of the aircraft sides added a
  vehicle plant at 5:26 to 6:42 as their third factory.
- *Before it.* Two or three extractors and three or four generators: tidals for five sides, wind turbines for the
  rest. One side (OS 57) built one tidal and then the yard at 0:11.
- *Out of the yard.* Construction ships first for seven of the ten (two or three of them, the first at 0:54 to
  2:03), a gunboat first for three. The first warship at 1:16 to 2:50 (2:00 at the median), and it is the gunboat every time:
  the Supporter for Cortex, the Dolphin for Armada. By 5:00 the ten had 2 to 22 warships (9 or 10 at the median; 8
  to 22 for the five sides above OS 55); one side made 44 Supporters in thirteen minutes.
- *The other openings.* The three aircraft sides read made two to five construction aircraft, a transport and a
  gunship or a scout; the five had their first warship at 3:50 to 7:40; the hovercraft side three construction hovercraft and ten
  Seekers by 4:38. In the four games that set a shipyard opening against an aircraft one, the aircraft side won three
  (OS 45 to 51) and the shipyard side one (OS 60 against 51).
- *Defence.* Ten sides built a floating torpedo launcher by 8:00, the first at 2:35 to 7:54 (about 5:00 at the
  median): after the fleet, not before it.
- *Economy by 4:00.* 4 to 15 extractors (13 at the median) and 0 to 9 tidals.

### Two people against our three seats on Glacial Gap: the game came through the water

Our seats stood in the east, two on land and one under 95 of water; the people in the west. What came into our
sight, by its first clock: Supporters at the water seat's tidal generators at 4:10 (four of them at its base by
5:00), a Herring at 5:49, a construction ship 3,000 west of the water seat at 6:08 and a torpedo launcher planted
there that killed three of our extractors and a constructor at those spots between 6:00 and 8:00, a submarine at 7:50, Oppressor
destroyers from 9:05, a scout plane at 11:48 and Salamanders (tier-2 amphibious tanks) from 12:23. The Supporters
killed eight tidal generators of the water seat and none of its extractors on the floor; the destroyers killed its
shipyard, five construction ships, a torpedo launcher and two radar towers. At 16:42 and 16:46 Salamanders that
had come up out of the sea killed the two land seats' commanders in their bases.
