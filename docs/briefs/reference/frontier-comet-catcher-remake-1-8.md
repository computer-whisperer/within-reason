### The late-game frontier: Comet Catcher Remake 1.8

40 spots a side (80 on the map), the arm side, from the recorded game `1791511650-rove-task-2`; the build-order simulator's best orders over 30 minutes (`run/frontier_set.py`). **A ceiling, not a target:** no enemy, nothing dies, every spot on our half is free, and the simulator runs 15 to 25 % high on income by minute 8 against quiet recorded openings. The tax is the enemy it knows: metal a second taken for soldiers before any build, from each time. Standard: 3 a second from 2:00, 5 from 4:00, 9 from 8:00, 12 from 12:00, 15 from 16:00 (the experienced players' standing army on Comet grows at that rate, before losses); heavy: one and a half times that. The arithmetic the frontier rests on is the engine's (a frame builds at the sum of the power on it until the store empties; a stockpile at its fixed rate, helped by nobody: K-t2-build-power-for-a-project-follows-the-spend, K-rules-a-stockpile-takes-no-build-power). The search is stochastic (simulated annealing, 20,000 steps x 8 restarts a cell, the reach rows from two seeds); a cell a heavier tax beat was searched again from other seeds (1 cells improved so). A cell still out of order is the search's noise, not the game's.
