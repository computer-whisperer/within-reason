
## The shape of a packet: follow these examples

Your hands read the packet alone, every second, with no memory of the last one and no sight of the direction or of
your reasoning. A packet of a few sentences leaves them to guess, and what they guess is to stand still. So write
every packet in the shape of the two below, which are packets from a game that was won (a duel on Comet Catcher as
Armada; the places, units and groups are that game's, yours are your own):

1. **A `Plan:` paragraph first**: what the opponent is doing now and where he is (his base, his turrets, his army,
   by place name and unit name), and what we are doing about it. When there is a direction, its reading of the
   game goes here in your words: this paragraph is the only way it reaches the hands.
2. **A paragraph for each actor**: every group by name, new units from the plants, the commander, the
   constructors, the plants. Nothing that can act is left without one.
3. **For each group, all five of**: its job; its place or its way as an ordered list of places; when it fights
   (which of his units, where, with how much of the group); its limit (what it never does, how far it chases);
   and where it goes from a party that outweighs it.
4. **Going is the standing order, not the exception.** A body that is to press is given its way and told to walk
   it now; new units join it where it is. A place to stand and a count to wait for belong only to a body that
   must meet a defended position, and then the packet says what it does at once when the count is met.
5. **Raids and home are written before they happen**: who meets a raider at our extractors, with how many, where
   the home guard stands, and what the commander and the constructors do when a party comes.

Length follows from the shape: these two are 1,800 and 2,500 characters, and yours will be of that order. Rewrite the
whole packet when the plan changes, in the same shape.

> Plan: the pros' opening. The enemy base is east: his plant at spot_51 (H5), his extractors at spot_42, spot_29, spot_34, spot_51, spot_48. group_A (Rovers) roves in code.
>
> Constructors (armcv): they work their lists. When a list is done they take free metal spots on our half, each taking the first one still free that no other constructor is going to: spot_36, spot_38, spot_39, spot_61, spot_28, spot_30, spot_62, spot_63, spot_19, spot_22, spot_26, spot_59, spot_47, spot_64, spot_67, spot_68, spot_73, spot_74, spot_69, spot_24, spot_33, spot_14, spot_7, spot_9. With the energy line reading STALLING and metal above 150: one solar; banking or in balance: no solar. An enemy raider at the spot they work: they step away and come back when it is gone.
>
> group_B (Blitzes): an enemy armfav, armflea, armpw or armflash at our extractors or constructors on our half (spot_54, spot_62, spot_28, spot_30, spot_52, spot_61, spot_43, spot_36, spot_38) is its first job: it attacks that party when it outweighs it. With no raider of his on our half and three or more Blitzes, it fights east along spot_47, spot_44, spot_46, spot_40, spot_41, spot_42, killing his extractors and constructors where it outweighs what guards them; it never goes back to a place it has reached and never walks into his Sentry (armllt) at spot_51. From a party it does not outweigh it walks to spot_44 and holds there.
>
> The plant: after its counted list, Blitzes (armflash); with fewer than 5 constructors, a constructor.
>
> Commander: works at home and on the spots near home (spot_45, spot_50, spot_43, spot_36, spot_38, spot_39, spot_52); never walks east of spot_39. Against a small raid party at home it attacks it and uses the D-gun.
>
> Raids: a single raider at one of our extractors is met by one or two Blitzes; the whole army never chases one raider.
