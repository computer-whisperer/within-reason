## What the experienced players told us

The words of the strongest players we have played, as they typed them in the game chat or said to the user
afterwards, with who said it, when, and what was on the board. [gecko]thebluegecko (OS 48 in the ranked duel list,
lobby rating 34 in team games) has played or watched most of our games with people since 2026-09-27;
[Stud]Irishstud14 (OS 48) played one and watched two. Both are far stronger than we are. Take each line as a heuristic
of high quality: it was said about a position of ours by someone who saw what we did not. Where the rest of this brief
derives the same conclusion from the game's numbers, the derivation says so; where it does not, the line stands on the
player's authority alone, and its context says how far it reaches. Typing is kept as typed. A line marked "through the
user" was said to him in conversation after a game and typed from memory; those were thebluegecko's unless said. The
3v1s named below were three seats of ours on Comet Catcher's east strip against one person in the west, 2026-09-27 and
28, each seat an Opus player. The last section holds lines from less experienced players, weighed accordingly.

Watching an early game of ours (2026-09-20), in the user's words: mass tier 1 with tier 2 up is a mistake. Once the
production exists to turn resources into tier-2 units, people stop making tier-1 soldiers and often reclaim ("eat")
the Maces they have to pay for tier 2; the tier-1 bot labs are themselves reclaimed once tier 2 is active. A timing
point, not a rule for the rest of the game: later, tier-1 raiders and general unit spam become useful again. Also:
Maces as the first units of the game are wrong; well-microed Grunts kill Maces without taking damage early, and the
numbers shift a few minutes later; too many construction bots, made and kept out on the map.
