### The gantry: what a tier-3 unit is bought for

**The heuristic.** The user, after the Comet game against u6bkep (M-u6bkep): both sides had tier 3 and only his was
used correctly. His was one Thor in front and Starlights behind it; ours were eleven Razorbacks and three Vanguards
that walked to the fight one at a time and killed 4,120 of metal for 51,700. The plain line: a gantry unit is bought
for a job inside a body, not for its tier. Most of them lose a straight fight to their own metal in tier-2 tanks.

**The quantity.** Three numbers say what a gantry unit is before it fights: health per metal against a Bull's 4.9;
how far each of its guns reaches, since a giant standing off fires only its long one; and whether it fires at
aircraft at all. The fourth is measured: the margin of a straight fight at equal metal, the share of its metal a
side has left minus the other's (+100 a flawless win).

**The mechanism.**

- *What each is.* The cheap reach: Shiva (1,600 metal; plasma at 650 and a rocket at 800; 5.9 health a metal),
  Karganeth (2,500; missiles at 600), Keres (2,600; 8.1 a metal), Marauder (970; 350, speed 94), Javelin (1,200):
  line tanks that out-reach line tanks, made in 18 to 52 seconds by a gantry alone. The front that stuns: the Thor
  (9,000; lightning at 540; beams that stun whatever closes to 350; 56,000 health; cannot be stunned). The fast
  and thin: Razorback (3,800; 1,050 a second at 475; 3.5 health a metal; speed 69), Demon (6,000; flame at 500; 3.0),
  Praetorian. The guns that need a front: Vanguard (3,300; 1,450 of reach; speed 33; 2.6 a metal), Catapult (4,900;
  1,350; 1.2 a metal), Myrmidon, Daedalus (rails at 1,000), Astraeus (3,100). The giants: Titan (13,500; a beam of
  800 a second at 800, blasters only inside 465; 69,000 health; can be stunned), Juggernaut (29,000; 149,000),
  Sol Invictus (23,500; 110,000), Behemoth (20,000; 335,000 health at speed 16.5). A giant is not damage per metal:
  a Titan standing off is 930 a second where its metal in Bulls is 3,500. It is health in one body that fights
  whole until it dies.
- *The straight fight at equal metal* (the engine, 30,000 a side, both attack-moving on open ground). Bulls beat
  twelve of the seventeen gantry units, and so do Tigers. The ones that beat tier-2 tanks: Behemoth (+86 against
  Bulls), Shiva (+55), Thor (+42), Keres (+42). Among themselves: the Behemoth beats everything; the Thor everything
  on the ground but the Behemoth; the Shiva and the Keres everything but those two; the Karganeth next. The
  Razorback loses to Bulls (-66), Tigers, Fatboys, Tzars, Shivas (-69) and the Thor (-65) and beats only what cannot
  hold it off: artillery, rocket bots, Starlights, Sharpshooters. Two Titans lose to Bulls (-65), Shivas (-76), a
  Thor, Pulsars (-75).
- *Aircraft are the scissors.* The Thor, Shiva, Keres, Vanguard and Catapult fire at nothing in the air: their
  metal in Hornets kills them all and loses next to nothing. The Razorback, Demon, Juggernaut and Sol Invictus lose
  badly, two Titans narrowly (the Titan is the gantry's anti-air, 1,050 a second, and it is still not enough alone).
  The Karganeth, the Javelin and the Behemoth beat gunships.
- *The long tower is the rock for the giants,* and the D-gun for all of them. Pulsars beat their metal in Titans,
  Sol Invictus and Juggernaut and lose to anything cheap and numerous and to the Thor. A Bastion line beats
  everything but the Thor and the Behemoth. A commander's D-gun kills any of them in one shot: a quarter of the
  Behemoths that died in people's team games and a sixth of the Juggernauts died to one.
- *A nuclear missile on one of them* (measured, the unit standing on the aim): 12,500 from Armada's, 15,000 from
  Cortex's and Legion's. It kills everything of the gantry up to the Karganeth, the Shiva and the Vanguard among
  them, and leaves a Razorback 6 percent from Armada's. A Thor keeps three quarters, a Titan four fifths, a
  Juggernaut nine tenths, a Behemoth 96 percent: 23 Cortex missiles on one spot to kill one, 34,500 metal and 69
  minutes of a silo, where the D-gun takes one shot. A nuke is for what cannot walk and for the cheap body packed
  together, not for a giant.
- *The front and the gun.* u6bkep's body, one Thor in front of eight Starlights (18,600 metal), fought against its
  metal in each answer: it kept 72 percent of itself against five Razorbacks, 54 against twenty Bulls, 79 against
  Bulls with Vanguards, 84 against a Titan with Bulls, 51 against eleven Shivas, 59 against two Thors, 31 against
  five Pulsars, and won every one. Eight Starlights alone lose to all of those. Fifteen Hornets killed the whole
  body and kept 88 percent; ten Bulls with seven Hornets won too. A front that cannot be stunned with the long gun
  behind it beats every ground answer at its price; nothing in it fires up.
- *What people's units do.* Half the people's seats in team games of thirty minutes have a gantry, the first at
  24:00 to 34:00. By last hit the units that earn their price are the ones that fire from behind something:
  Daedalus 1.5 of its metal, Astraeus 1.4, Vanguard 1.3, Karganeth and Shiva 1.1, Catapult 1.0. The brawlers and
  the giants return 0.4 to 0.8 (Razorback 0.76, the most built), a quarter to a half of every type kills nothing,
  and a brawler that dies has lived two and a half minutes.
- *The gantry is a plant.* Build power 1,800: alone it makes a Shiva in 22 seconds, a Razorback or a Vanguard in 67,
  a Thor in 178, a Titan in 200, a Juggernaut in 406, a Behemoth in 433. The turrets beside it are what make a
  giant a five-minute purchase or a one-minute one.
- *Our record.* Ahead at 30:00 of that game (358 metal a second, 12,000 banked, three gantries with no turret on
  them), the fourteen units arrived in fourteen pieces over eight minutes, the Vanguards with nothing of
  ours within 700 of them and setting the body's pace at 33, and the body went into the Thor on the ground. The one
  bomber sent at his Starlights at 36:57 found them with no anti-air.

**Consider, and why it may work.**

1. *Name the job before the unit.* A front: the Thor; Shivas or Keres in number. Guns for behind it: Starlights,
   then the Vanguard, Catapult, Daedalus. Something for his guns once his front is engaged: Razorbacks or Demons,
   as a group, round the side. Why: it is the only reading under which the Razorback and the Vanguard are worth
   their metal, and the composition is what won the game we lost.
2. *The gantry's units go as one body with the front, never as they finish.* They gather at the gantry or the hold
   until the front and its guns stand together, and the artillery walks behind the front at the front's pace, not
   the body at the artillery's. Why: fourteen arrivals killed a twelfth of their price; the bench's five Razorbacks
   together lose to the Thor body, one at a time they do not scratch it.
3. *Something that fires up walks with it.* A Karganeth, a Titan, flak vehicles or anti-air bots in the body, and
   fighters over it once his air has shown (air.md). Why: every ground body without it loses to its own metal in
   gunships outright.
4. *Read his body for what it cannot do, and send that.* A Thor in front: aircraft first, since it fires at none;
   on the ground stay outside 540 and kill what stands behind it. No anti-air in sight: gunships and bombers
   before the army. Titans: Pulsars at the hold, or numbers of the cheap reach. Anything of his at our door: the
   commander's D-gun, 250 of reach and 500 energy. A Behemoth: walk away from it at three times its speed and
   shoot it from beyond 590; never a nuke, which takes a twentieth of it.
5. *Turrets on the first gantry before a second gantry.* Why: with 12,000 banked we built three gantries bare and
   each made a Razorback in 67 seconds; forty turrets on one make a Titan in under a minute, and the bank was the
   sign that build power, not metal, was short (late-economy.md).
6. *A giant for what only a giant does:* reach with health behind it, anti-air on the same body (the Titan), no
   stun (the Thor, the Juggernaut, the Behemoth), and one body where thirty cannot fire. Not for damage: by metal
   it has a third of the Bulls'.

**Where it bends.** (1) The bench is the straight fight: attack-move, open flat ground, 1,100 apart at the start,
no escort, no terrain. That start is inside the artillery's reach, so its rows show the Vanguard and the Catapult
caught, not used; in people's games they return the most. A narrow lane muzzles thirty Bulls and not one Titan, and
nothing here prices that. (2) Bonused BARb and Apex build Razorbacks, Thors, Catapults and Karganeths past 25:00
(M-barb-bonus); they send them as they finish, which is the Razorback's failing and our D-gun's chance. (3) Legion's
Keres takes half a stun and its Daedalus fires at aircraft; the Javelin beats gunships. (4) The sea: the Marauder,
Shiva, Titan, Juggernaut and Sol Invictus walk the sea floor.

**When to depart from the line.** When the game is being decided now by what already stands: a gantry begun at a
level or losing fight is 8,000 metal that fires in two minutes at the earliest (lead.md). When his air is winning:
anti-air and fighters before any gantry unit. When our commander is what he is walking at: nothing of this matters
more than its feet.

**How it fails.** *One at a time* (fourteen arrivals, 4,120 killed). *The thin unit as the front* (Razorbacks into a
Thor: stunned at 350, shot from 950). *The artillery ahead of its front, or setting the body's pace.* *Ground into a
Thor.* *No anti-air in the body.* *The bare gantry beside a full bank.* *The giant bought for damage.* *The giant
walked onto a commander.*

**The tests.** docs/studies/2026-11-08-tier-3.md: the one game against u6bkep read from our seat's record (his side
as we saw it); the unit files for every gun's reach; 169 public team games, 166 on one map, for what people build
and what kills it, by last hit; 748 engine duels at 30,000 metal a side plus 72 of his body against its
answers, and one nuclear missile on each unit in the engine, on a harness whose energy feed was found broken and fixed that night. No game of ours has been played on
this article.
