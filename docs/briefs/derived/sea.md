### A start at the sea: the shipyard is the plant

**The heuristic.** The players' own lines on water are few: "tidals are good here" (L-water-tidals) and hovers
"good for harass, bad for army bulk" (L-mix-hovers). What their play says, as a line: a seat whose home is at the
sea opens at sea. Its first factory is the shipyard, inside the first minute, and its gunboats are in the water by
two minutes (M-sea-duels).

**The quantity.** Two clocks: when the first warship of ours is in the water, and when the first of his is at our
generators. Whatever a sea base floats (its tidal generators, its yard, its construction ships) dies to the first
gunboat that finds it alone, and an Incisor or a Pawn on the shore reaches no boat that stays 300 off it.

**The mechanism.**

- *The commander is at home on the floor.* It walks the sea floor and builds there at its full 300: the extractor at
  any depth, the tidal generator in 7 seconds, the shipyard in 17, the torpedo launcher in 12 (F-sea-builders,
  F-map-build). A start under water with spots beside it is a start with no walk; the walk to dry ground is the
  thing to avoid. Ours on Glacial Gap stood 95 deep with four free spots 111 to 264 from it and dry ground 900
  away, 36 seconds at its speed under water (F-sea-commander).
- *The yard is the cheapest factory and the only one whose units hold water.* 450 metal against 570 to 590 for a
  vehicle plant. Its construction ship is the best tier-1 builder there is at sea: 125 of build power at speed 60 for 200
  metal in 25 seconds, where the amphibious constructor from a vehicle plant has 85 at speed 40 and takes 46
  (F-sea-builders). A land constructor builds no tidal generator at all, so a seat at sea that opens with a land
  plant builds its energy with the commander and with amphibious constructors at 46 seconds a piece.
- *The gunboat is the opening's unit.* A Supporter is 100 metal and 9 seconds of the yard, a Dolphin 175 and 16; they
  run at 105 to 110 and do more damage for their metal than any other tier-1 ship (F-sea-reach). What they kill
  is what an opening is made of: constructors, tidal generators, the other side's gunboats. The pool's first warship
  is a gunboat on every side, at 2:00 at the median, and the sides above OS 55 have 8 to 22 warships by 5:00
  (M-sea-duels). A yard that makes them without pause draws 11 metal and 107 energy a second: five tidals at a tide
  of 23, and five or six extractors at two metal a second each.
- *The tide is the sea seat's energy.* A tidal generator makes the map's tide, steady, for 85 or 90 metal
  (F-map-weather, F-eco-energy): at a tide of 23 that is 3.7 metal for each energy a second, at 20 it is 4.3, where a
  solar is 7.5 and a turbine about 3 under a wind that tops at 19 or 20. The turbine is as cheap and stands only on
  land; the tidal stands where the commander is. Five of the pool's sixteen sides opened on tidals and the rest on
  turbines beside their shore.
- *Reach decides what a thing can answer* (F-sea-reach). A gunboat reaches 280, so the commander's under-water
  laser (300, 200 a second) and a torpedo launcher (500, 187 a second, 170 metal) both kill it when it comes to them;
  a frigate (470 to 480) comes just inside a launcher; a missile boat (650) and a destroyer (700 to 710) stand
  outside both and are answered only by ships. The commander has no D-gun under water (F-sea-commander).
- *What a gunboat does not reach.* In our game his Supporters killed eight tidal generators of the water seat and
  none of its extractors 95 down on the floor; the extractors there fell to a torpedo launcher and to amphibious tanks, and
  more of them to killers our records do not name (M-sea-glacial-gap). Seen once: the floor's extractors outlast the first boats, the floating
  generators do not.

**Consider, and why it may work.**

1. *The home spots and tidals where the commander stands, then the shipyard, all inside the first minute.* Why: no
   walk; the pool's order is two or three extractors and three generators and the yard at 0:47 to 1:04 for eight of the ten, and every
   piece of it is on the commander's list from the floor.
2. *Out of the yard: two construction ships, then gunboats without pause.* Why: the ships take the under-water spots
   and the tidals at twice an amphibious constructor's pace; the gunboats are what meets his first boats and what
   kills his constructors, and the pool has them from 2:00.
3. *A torpedo launcher at the generators once his boats have come, or when the fleet leaves home.* Why: 170 metal
   out-reaches the gunboat and the frigate; the pool builds its first at about 5:00, after the fleet and not in its
   place, and a destroyer stands outside it.
4. *In a team game, the seat at the sea is the side's navy.* Why: the land seats' plants make nothing that reaches a
   boat. On Glacial Gap the people's boats were at our water seat at 4:10, their destroyers from 9:05, and their
   amphibious tanks came up out of that water and killed both land commanders at 16:42 and 16:46.
5. *A land or air plant second, for what the sea does not reach.* Why: the pool's other opening is the aircraft
   plant first with the yard at 2:55 to 6:11, for construction aircraft that take the islets' spots, and it won
   three of four against the yard-first sides at OS 45 to 51; hovercraft are for harassing, not for the body
   (L-mix-hovers).

**Where it bends.** (1) Water on a map is not a sea game: two of the pool's duels on maps with water opened as land
games with no ship by 8:00. The test is whether the spots lie under water and whether a boat can reach the bases.
(2) Every side of the pool stood on land beside the sea; a commander under water is our one game, and what it adds is
arithmetic, not anyone's play. (3) The tide is the map's and the lobby may set it to 13, 18 or 23; at 13 a tidal is
6.5 metal for each energy a second and the turbine on the shore is the better buy. (4) Depth: a tidal needs 20 of
water and the yard 30 (F-map-build). (5) Submarines and sonar come later than this article goes: an Orca or an Eel is
unseen without sonar and kills what is on the floor.

**When to depart from the line.** When nothing of his can come by water, the yard is still the builder's plant and
the fleet is not bought. When the seat's dry ground holds most of its spots, the land plant comes first and the yard
second, as the aircraft sides did. When his first boats are already at the generators and no yard stands, the
launcher comes before the yard: it is 12 seconds of the commander against 17 and then 9 a boat.

**The tests.** Eight public duels, none of them ours, read by their build clocks alone; where a fleet went and what
it killed is not in them. One game of ours with a seat under water (the Glacial Gap 3v2 of 2026-11-07, lost): the
seat's commander walked to dry ground for a vehicle plant, its first extractor stood at 1:52 where the land seats'
stood at 0:09, his boats were at its tidals at 4:10, its shipyard stood at 6:30, its first torpedo launcher at 6:56
and its first ship at 7:49; six of its tidals were dead by 6:00 with 1,338 metal banked and no energy, and it held no
extractor by 15:00. Our seats against thebluegecko on SailAway 2 and Coast To Coast lost to a yard by 3:30 and
Riptides from 4:10, and to a navy from the start (M-gecko-other-maps). One game of ours has opened at sea on this
article (2026-11-14, a duel against BARb from that same under-water start, won at 26:50): the sea list ran from the
first second, the first extractor stood at 0:09, the yard at 1:09 and a construction ship at 1:36; the first
Supporter came at 4:01, two minutes behind the pool, after a vehicle plant on dry ground at 2:15; destroyers from
9:42 and cruisers from 14:39 did the killing at sea, and 46 extractors fell to raiders nothing of ours saw.
