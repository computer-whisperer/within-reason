Debrief: Glacial Gap v1.1, corner W, Cortex, vs BARb stable hard_aggressive. Won at 26.8 min.

What the map actually is
The lobby put our commander at M4 on the east side even though our start box reads west (A1-C8). Do not trust the box; read the commander's actual position on turn one. At M4 the commander starts under water (tide 23), so the sea opening is the one that runs. BARb mirrored: his start was B4, also under water, and he built a sea base there (tidals plus shipyard at B5) with no land base and, for most of the game, no land army at all. There are roughly 24 water metal spots and 29 land spots; 8 spots on the M6/N7 islets near home are reachable by nobody else and are free income for air constructors.

Opening that worked
Tidals, third extractor, shipyard; construction ships first (not gunboats) to take the water spots; then walk the commander to land north of home (south of the M3 spots) for a vehicle plant, because the land spots and any land fight need Incisors and a land constructor. Expect metal and energy to alternate as the shortage every 20-30 seconds for the first five minutes; put every idle builder on whichever is empty. Solars, not wind. Do not place an early land group's home at "home" — home is under water and the first Incisor got stuck at the shore; base land groups at the plant site.

The sea war is the whole game, and it is won by unit type
BARb's one early Supporter gunboat harassed our construction ships for minutes and killed one; Supporters and Riptides cannot hit submarines, and his corsubs killed ships and extractor frames at E5/J4/J5 with near impunity. The answer is Oppressor destroyers (sonar, seeker torpedoes) early, then Buccaneers from an advanced shipyard (sub-capable torpedoes) as the main line. Get Oppressors the moment a sub is seen, and get the advanced shipyard started by about 12:00 — Buccaneers are what finally cleared his fleet and his base.

Two mistakes to not repeat
Ships reinforced one at a time into his fleet and lost two fights (H5, G5) despite a large lead, because the fleet group's place sat forward at I5/J5 so each new ship sailed alone to it. Anchor the naval group at home, gather the body, and release it with a single signal. Separately, I built 20 Garpikes, Salamanders and Poison Arrows as an "amphibious" force and discovered at 20:56 that amphibious tanks cannot fire while submerged — a 17.7k army that was useless at sea. Do not build land or amphibious units for an under-water enemy base; build ships.

Air was a near-total loss: bombers into Herring anti-air at E5 lost 4 of 5, and more died to his seaplane fighters off a corplat. Use the air plant for air constructors to take the islet spots and keep them home; fighters only to answer his corcut gunships.

The nuke
With 5,400 metal banked at 16:22 and his base under water and hard to reach by ship, a silo on the block was the right hedge (first missile ~3 min after it stands). The first missile at E5 was almost certainly intercepted — he had an advanced shipyard and likely an Oasis mobile anti-nuke ship, and the target stood intact. The second missile killed his corsy and 24 tidals at B4/B5 and effectively ended him.

Read this twice: our own second missile killed 48 of our units, 21,370 metal, including 6 Buccaneers and 5 Oppressors. I held the fleet "at C5, east of B5" trusting the fire tool's 640 radius; Cortex's missile actually reaches 960 (the brief says so) and the navy stood ~730 away. Hold own units 1,200+ from the aim point and check their measured distance in metres, never the cell name.

Endgame
He never showed a factory on land, and his commander was never seen until 26:29, sitting under water at B4 (1893,3454). Ships with torpedoes are the only thing that can kill it; the amphib army could not, and 16 of them died in its death blast. Keep two cheap air scouts roving the never-seen cells from about 20:00 so you find the commander before you need to. The navy killed it at C4 at 26:49 and the game closed.

Housekeeping that cost time: constructors twice parked in the advanced shipyard's exit lane and blocked Buccaneers; watch for it and send them to reclaim the K4 wreck field instead. By 25:10 the metal store was full at 31k — give every idle factory and constructor a list well before that.
