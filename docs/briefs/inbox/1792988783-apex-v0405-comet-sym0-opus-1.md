We won at 30:42 by killing his commander at H3, but the middle twenty minutes were bad and you can do much better. Here is what I'd tell myself.

The opening works. Commander from our box (we started B8): two extractors, two solars, vehicle plant by ~0:34, commander assists low. Plant list: constructor, two Rovers, constructor, then Blitzes with a constructor every third or fourth. By 4:48 we held nine extractors and 23 metal a second, which is above the strong players' pace, so don't change this. The Rovers find his base fast — Apex spawned in the north-east corner (G1) with commander, solars, a construction turret and extractors at spot_5 and spot_10.

Apex's early pressure is single Pawns, from about 2:40. They come one at a time at half-built extractors on our north edge (spot_63, spot_64, spot_69). Blitzes beat them easily because they are faster, but I lost an extractor, a half-built extractor and constructor_4549 before the Blitzes got there. Keep two or three Blitzes standing in that north strip from 2:30 rather than reacting, and don't send a lone constructor north of spot_64 without one. Also: don't scout his base corner with Rovers that linger — his commander shot two of mine dead at G1. One pass, then leave.

The real lesson is the middle game, 11:00 to 20:00, where I threw away most of my lead. At 11:00 I had 6.8k of army against about 1.3k of his seen and I still spent nine minutes losing. Two causes:

First, Apex builds a defensive nest and stands behind it. On this map it was Beamers and HLTs around F3, later a Fatboy plus Recluse plus HLTs at E3-E4. I marched Stouts into it three separate times and took about 12k damage for nothing; meanwhile his Mace/Centurion army roamed our west and I lost nine extractors in three minutes. Do not push a Stout body into an unscouted turret cluster. The nest is a toll you do not have to pay: either stay out of its reach (I ended up holding at spot_44, outside its ~700), build Mausers (820 reach) to shell the turrets from outside, or go around. Going around his south through spot_58-55-53-57-60-70-72-77-65 was the right idea and I kept postponing it for a charge; when I finally committed at 18:47 we killed three Fatboys and a Hound at E5 for 4.9k in a minute — the first good trade in eight minutes. Commit earlier.

Second, the hands will undo you if the packet leaves a crack. Three specific failures, all mine to prevent: eight Stouts detached to chase one Pawn home, so write "no group sends detachments" from the first packet, not at 11:00 after it happens; a charge clause pulled groups north into the Fatboy nest and cost seven Stouts, so never leave a standing "charge when you see his army" clause near a nest — say instead that the army only fights his army when it comes to our half; and above all, groups stall. Repeatedly — at F1, at H1, at the dead bot lab, at spot_12 — the hands kept a group "advancing" on a place it had already reached or a target it could not hit (Stouts cannot shoot his advanced construction aircraft). The symptom is a group sitting still while a Sentry chews it. Give routes as explicit lists of named spots with the next stop beyond the current one, re-read the group's position every turn, and when a group hasn't moved between two reports, rewrite the route rather than repeating it.

Economy notes. Metal banking means build power is short, not metal — at 4:01 I had 407 banked with two constructors and did too little about it. Late, the reverse: at 23:40 metal was full at 6.7k and energy was stalling, with five idle constructors; I had each queue four solars and that unstuck the plants and the fusion. Advanced vehicle plant at home with the commander plus two constructors at about 11:00 was right, and Mausers then Bulls out of it is what broke the stalemate. His air (Banshees and bombers) becomes the main drain around 20:00; two Chainsaws at spot_52 and spot_38 plus Falcons handled it. The 37 Falcons were also my best scouts at the end.

Endgame: destroying every factory does not end it. I killed his advanced air plant (28:34) and his tier-1 bot lab (28:40) and he still had a commander rebuilding a fusion at H1 that then vanished for nearly three minutes. Don't grind his base buildings hoping he's in them — the moment his factories are gone, send the Falcons sweeping the unseen east and south for the commander and split the ground army into two or three sweeps of his box. That is what found him at H3 alone with one air constructor. When you do find it, attack with the whole group at once from beyond 300 (D-gun reaches 250, death blast 350), order every group near it onto it and nothing else, and then stop rewriting the packet — a fresh packet seconds before contact only makes the groups re-pick their moves.

If I replayed this: same opening, Blitzes stationed north from 2:30, no detachments ever, advanced vehicle plant at 11:00, and then the southern sweep around his nest at 12:00 instead of 19:00. That should finish well inside 20 minutes.
