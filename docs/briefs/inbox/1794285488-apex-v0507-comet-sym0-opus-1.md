# apex-v0507-comet-sym0-opus-1: the player's debrief

Match `run/matches/1794285488-apex-v0507-comet-sym0-opus-1/00`, build 46e3ce5b8183, against Apex v0.5.07 standard on Comet Catcher Remake 1.8: Win after 18.5 min (called by the referee); 90 turns. Debriefed by claude-opus-5 (effort low) in 31 s, in a shape of its own (DECIDED / NEXT PLAYER / WISHED was asked); since snapshot: 7-day +0.0 points, extra usage +0.00.

We won on Comet Catcher Remake 1.8 from corner W (start B6, south-west) against Apex v0.5.07 standard, called at 18.5 minutes with 54 extractors, ~208 metal a second and a 60-tank army worth ~19,000 metal against about 1,600 of his. The economy was never the problem; almost every mistake I made was in how the army was asked to move and fight, and the one real cost was that the win took eighteen minutes instead of twelve.

What Apex did, so you can expect it: he opens a bot lab, not vehicles, and a single Pawn is in our home extractors by about 2:25. His start is in the north-east, around G1-H2, roughly 70 seconds away for a Blitz. His base grows a turret ring (Beamers and Sentries) at G1-G2 and a Gauntlet at G3, and those turrets are the only thing on the map that killed our tanks — the main army lost 7 Stouts in one turn at 16:28 to two Beamers and two Sentries while it already outweighed everything living there three to one. He tried tier 2 late: an advanced bot lab at 15:59 and an aircraft plant at 16:21, both of which our army killed as frames. His own constructors and bare extractors are undefended early and his commander wanders (seen at F1 at 18:20).

The opening worked and I would repeat it: two extractors, two solars, vehicle plant at ~0:34, third extractor, more solars, commander guarding the plant at low priority. Because he goes bots with Pawns in our half, make a Rover for scouting and then constructors plus Blitzes rather than a Rover mass — Blitz beats Pawn one on one. Rovers roving his half killed an extractor and two of his construction bots in the first three minutes for nothing, and that is probably the cheapest damage available on this map.

The Pawn cost me two extractors and several minutes of attention, and the reasons are worth copying down. First, my only Blitz backed away from it, because the plant had put it in a roving group and a roving group keeps out of anything that can shoot it; you must put Blitzes in their own non-roving group with an explicit job if you want them to kill a raider. Second, I sent the commander after it. A Pawn runs at 87 and the commander at 38: it chased for over a minute, caught nothing, left its two solars unbuilt and the plant unassisted, and the energy store emptied. Never chase with the commander; a D-gun standing rule near the plant is fine, a chase is not. Blitzes from the next group finally killed it at 3:57.

The army lesson is the whole game. Left to themselves the hands will shred one ball into detachments (at 10:17 I had J 8, W 6, Z 2, plus T and Y), send Blitzes after outranging Rocketeers, and issue attack orders at targets 4,000 away so a 16-Stout ball loiters near home for two minutes chasing raiders. They will also merge a group you told to fall back to G2 into the reinforcement group at home, which gave up our foothold in his base at 16:38 and made the next push walk the whole map again. And they will hold a 30-tank group outside our own base because three tanks are boxed in by one of our solars (A5, 16:45 to 17:08, fixed by reclaiming armsolar_28831). What finally made the army move and fight was a packet that named one group as "our one army", gave a route as an ordered list of spots with the word advance, and said in plain words: never go back to a place it has reached, never go home, never wait for stuck soldiers left behind, send no detachment of 1, 2, 4 or 8 after anything, shoot Pawns and constructors as you pass, close on turrets whole and fast and never hold inside a Beamer's reach, fight his commander with the whole body. Write that packet by 9:00, not by 17:00. Also note that a vague destination like "his half" makes the hands advance on their own line rather than your route, and that a group standing still for 20 seconds means the route needs rewriting, usually because it is waiting on stragglers.

Economy notes: two plants alone only spend about 20/s, so by 9:17 I had 1,169 metal banked — add the third vehicle plant and construction turrets earlier. From roughly 10:00 onward energy, not metal, was the constant limit: full store with 600/s surplus at one point and an empty store with metal at 3,900 later. Solars cost no energy to build and the commander builds them much faster than a constructor, so bank-dumping into commander-built solars is the reliable fix; converters when energy is actually surplus; the fusion is worth starting but it came too late to matter. Watch plant lists running dry — plant_144 and plant_29823 both stood idle with empty queues — and keep a tail of "then keep making Stouts" on every list.

If I played it again: same opening, Blitzes in their own answer group from the first one, no commander chases, the single-army packet written the moment the first push is contemplated around 9:00, and the third plant plus turrets at 7:00 rather than 9:17. The army is strong enough to walk into his base by 12:00; nothing he had could stop it except his turret ring, and that only hurt us when the ball sat inside it.
