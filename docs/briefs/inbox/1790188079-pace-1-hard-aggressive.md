# Debrief: Comet Catcher, Armada from corner W (B5) vs BARb hard_aggressive. Lost at 34.5 min.

## What happened

We were even or ahead on army value from about 16 to 28 minutes, and at 27:48 the army stood at 14.4k against 5k seen. Then we lost the game in five minutes. The cause was not their strength. We spread our army thin, starved our energy, and let our economy stop growing at about 22–25 extractors from minute 16.

## Their timeline (what to expect)

- **Their bot lab** is at G2 (6784, 928), in the north-east. Raiders come from there and down the east side.
- **2:30–5:30:** single Pawns and Ticks walk our extractor line (spot_36/43/38/61/52/19/30) and kill extractors one by one. By 4:00 we had 5 extractors where a good game has about 9.
- **~10 min:** a block of Maces, Rocketeers and Centurions comes to spot_26/24.
- **~16 min:** tier 2 appears: Gunslinger, Lazarus, snipers.
- **By ~19:30:** a Gauntlet nest sits at E3. It covers everything east of spot_24.
- **~22 min:** a Fatboy arrives with Centurions.
- **~29–32 min:** a wave of Razorbacks, a Marauder and more Fatboys. This wave ended the game.
- **Their commander was never seen.** No scout got past G1–H8 all game.

## What worked

- **Fighting on our own turrets.** We won every fight at spot_24/26/39:
  - 10:12: killed 3 Rocketeers.
  - 16:28: pincered their block between two groups and killed about 10 units for 4 Stouts.
  - 25:22: killed a Fatboy and 6 Centurions.
- **Stouts plus Bulls** as the core of the army, with the commander D-gunning Fatboys at home.
- **A turret on every extractor**, and building the turret before the extractor at exposed spots.

## What lost the game (do these differently)

1. **Energy hit zero four times** (6:50, 17:25, 23:51, ~30:00). Each time the turrets and the D-gun went silent.
   - Build a Fusion Reactor by about 20:00.
   - Advanced solars cost energy to build, so don't start them during a stall.
   - Raiders kill solars. Keep them behind the army.
2. **The Advanced Vehicle Plant was late.** It was started at 11:11 and stood at 17:13, because one builder was on it (338 s alone). Put 3+ builders on it from the start and have it up by 13–14 min.
3. **Extractors stopped growing at 16 min** while free spots (61/69/19/7/2) sat open. Keep two constructors expanding, each with a turret, all game.
4. **Pushes split the army.**
   - **21:02:** we went north with Whistlers into a Fatboy block. Then we retreated under its 700 range and lost about 4k in a minute. Once you are engaged with a Fatboy, retreating costs more than fighting it.
   - **26:00:** the second push arrived piecemeal. It left the home guard thin, and trickle raids pulled home apart.
   - **Rule:** commit the whole army to one target, or hold at home. Never hold a detachment somewhere in between.
5. **Chasing bait into E3.** Stouts following retreating units toward the Gauntlet nest bled all through 14–21 min. Don't chase past spot_24 east.
6. **The opening lost 285+ metal to lone raiders.**
   - The commander on its build list ignored a Pawn beside it.
   - Our Blitzes chased one Tick to the south map edge and never caught it.
   - Give the first 1–2 Blitzes a leash: kill raiders near our extractors, but don't follow them off our ground.
