# Debrief — Altored Divide Bar Remake, corner N (G1), vs Apex v0.3.2 at +50%

Won at 33:24, 182 turns. Read this before your first turn; most of it is paid for in metal I lost.

## The map from G1

You start north-east at G1; allies sit at D1 and C1 to your west. You are the flank seat, and the whole eastern half of the map is the opponent's. **Wind is live here but unstable** — it read 15 at 2:00 and 6 at 14:00. Three or four wind turbines are the right opener because they are cheap and instant, but do not plan an economy on them: I stalled energy completely at 14:03 on wind 6 with 2,600 metal banked.

The pros' Comet line transfers with one change: two extractors (spot_3, spot_5), **three wind turbines instead of two solars**, then the vehicle plant, commander guarding/helping the plant on low priority. Energy ran dry at 1:50 anyway (85 in against 86 out) — queue four more turbines right behind the first extractor walk, not after the plant.

At +50% his raids and army arrive early. Put a turret beside each outer extractor as it goes up, from the first one. Do not wait to see a raider.

## The east is a nest. Do not raid it.

This is the single lesson of the game. His G7/H7/F6 complex is layered long-range cover, and it grows:

- 6:54 — heavy laser tower (corhlt 620) at H7, aircraft plant with corshad bombers.
- 8:03 — corthud + corstorm under that laser bled my Blitz body at F6: 800 metal for 480.
- 13:02 — a Punisher at G7 took ~1,500/min off a body merely *passing* spot_53.
- 14:22 — the route to the west push past D6 ran into two more Punishers and another corhlt.
- 16:11 — an Agitator at G7, reach 1,245, covering his builders at H6/G6 and spots 52/53.
- 25:55 — an Intimidator at C7, reach **4,950**, covering most of row 7-8.

Total for my eastern raiding: about 2,400 metal spent for 600 killed, across three attempts, each of which looked locally reasonable. **Spots 52, 53, 57, and the F6-H8 cluster are not yours to take in the first 25 minutes.** Hold spot_44 / spot_41 as the east-home guard, keep your extractors alive, and spend the tempo on economy. Your army becomes useful when it joins the allied push on the west, and the east base dies at 31:00 to the whole team.

Corollary on movement: **every route from G1 to the west crosses his gun cover unless you go north-about** — spot_42, spot_43, spot_51, or out through exit_north (E4). Plan the route, don't let a body take the straight line; I lost seven units at 14:42 to exactly that.

## Air

His air comes in two waves, and both are your problem alone because they arrive before any ally can help: corshad bombers by 6:54, picking off the H4 extractors, and corhurc tier-2 bombers by 16:41. Nettles at home and beside the outer extractors (spot_40/41) by 7:00; a Chainsaw next to your first fusion before it finishes. Tell the side commander — every seat wants the same two buildings on the same clock.

## Economy

Energy is the binding constraint the whole game, metal is not. I was energy-stalled at 8:03 and again, fully, at 14:03.

- Reclaim the wreck fields early: it lifted income to ~55 and banked 600 by 5:05. Reclaim H1, H4, G4, G5 freely; **G6 and E6 are under his guns** — I lost a constructor at 30:23 reclaiming E6 inside the F6 corvipe's reach.
- **Do not start mohos while stalling.** They draw ~300 e/s for metal you cannot spend. Fusion first; while the fusion builds, put the commander and any free constructor on *basic* solars, which need no build energy and break the stall immediately.
- Metal overflowed four times (2,600 → 3,500 → 4,760 → 7,100). Convert it the moment the bank passes ~1,000: construction turrets on the plants (each adds roughly 13 metal/s of Stouts), nanos, a second vehicle plant around 12:00, and transfers to whichever ally is drawing down. I sent 1,500 to t1 at 16:51 and that was right.

## Two things the hands get wrong here

1. **The odds count far guns.** At 26:15 the hands kept stepping my body back from a Shiva fight at D7 because the C7 Intimidator (4,950 reach) covered the ground. Name the target party explicitly in the packet and say the far cannon does not change that fight, or you will never close.
2. **A walk already in progress is hard to stop.** Reweighting a direction does not pull a body off a route it is already on; it re-picks only at the next decision. Twice I watched a body finish walking into a gun I had already forbidden. Write routes you are willing to see completed, and recall through a named waypoint (exit_north) rather than trusting a cancellation.

Also: a *lone, fresh* gun in your half is worth killing as one body from a direction that stays out of the other guns' reach — but identify it first. At 16:31 I committed the whole body to a new "Agitator beside spot_52" that turned out to be a Punisher sitting inside the G7 nest. Check the gun's id and position against the nest before you leave spot_44.

## The last ten minutes: experimentals

Apex at +50 reaches experimentals, and this is where the game is actually won or lost.

- **Kill the gantry, not the Juggernaut.** His second gantry stood at C8 at 28:10 with a corjugg frame inside it; we killed both plants at C8 by 28:40 but the Behemoth (20,000 metal, 149k HP, reach 800) walked. Hit gantries the turn you see them.
- **Only Ambassadors answer a Juggernaut.** Reach 1,300 against its 800-950. Shell and step back; keep Whistlers, constructors and the commander entirely out of its reach, and note the death blast reaches ~640. Switch your plant to Ambassadors only.
- At 31:56 a corkorg (29,000 metal, 149k HP, reach 950) walked into our east and went for home at G1. A Pulsar (armanni, 3,500 metal, 1,400 reach, 10,800 damage) is the right home answer — but **each shot costs 1,500 energy**. I had to pause both plants at 33:06 so the Pulsar could fire, and should have asked an ally for an energy transfer several turns earlier. Build the Pulsar at the first sighting of a Juggernaut heading your way, not when it is 1,700 out, and bank the energy for it deliberately.
- When his experimentals come for your corner, say so to the side plainly and early: t1/t2's Ambassadors and tier-2 plants are what actually kills one. Your corner alone cannot.

## Scoreboard worth keeping in mind

At 28:00 the trade read 19,300 of his buildings destroyed for 4,300 of ours — and that came almost entirely from the western push with the allies, in the half-hour after I stopped feeding bodies east. Two of his commanders were sitting at A8 at 29:27. Your seat's job on this map is: survive the early air and raids, build the biggest economy on the team, and bring a large body west on the northern route. The east base is the team's last stop, not your first one.
