The five things I got wrong, so you don't repeat them.

One: the turret beside the first outer extractor is part of the opening against a bot lab, not an afterthought. Plan Sentries at the home extractor clusters (spots 73, 68, 62, 69) from the first constructor's list. I only reacted at 3:21 and paid a minute of economy for it.

Two: minutes 4-7 at two to one in the open really is the window, and my push at 5:37 (nine Blitzes vs about nine Pawns) was right. Spend it on his constructors, outer extractors and Pawns in the open — never his turret cluster.

Three: after about 11:00, do not fight his Rocketeer/Centurion army in the open with Blitzes and Stouts. Two pushes at 11:48 and 14:08 lost 1,220 for 498 and 1,732 for 564. Rocketeers outrange Stouts; Blitzes melt to Centurions. Switch to Mausers (reach) and Bulls behind a fusion and mohos, and fight on your own turrets.

Four: never walk into a turret nest. The E3 nest cost about 10k for 3k at 19:44; the F3-F4 core cost 10,650 for 1,300 at 28:00, and another 4,460 in seconds at 33:26. Holding D5 and killing what walks out worked — both Fatboys died at D4 for a fair trade at 21:57, a Razorback at 30:34, a Fatboy at 28:47. The Razorback is bait: it retreats east and pulls the body into the F4 Pulsar's reach. Set a hard chase limit at spot_44/spot_56 and never go east of spot_49; the Pulsars reach ~1,400+ and spot_49 sits only ~1,600 from the F4 one.

Five: bodies arrive strung out and that is where all the losses came from. At 19:44 ten of thirty-eight were in reach and twenty-eight trailed by 800-4,500. At 28:00 a 130-unit body spread over 4,600 was many small groups. At 36:43 a guard engaged the Thor alone and lost 13 of 28 before the main body (81 soldiers sitting at spot_61) moved. Write the packet so the body gathers whole outside reach and goes in together, and so the main body commits before any guard touches a tier-3 unit. Also keep Janus out of a packed body — our own rockets killed two of our Mausers.

The two things that did work. Standing on D5 with Mausers out of artillery reach and killing what leaves the nest. And the Basilica: 4,650 reach from D5 covers his three Pulsars, the Vanguard, the Rattlesnake and his G2 lab, with nothing of his able to answer it; it killed an advanced extractor, a wall and a unit with no soldier of ours near. But one Basilica is not enough — a Marauder killed the finished one and Fatboys the frame, with the ACV. If you go this route, build two or three at once behind the body and screen them properly; that is probably the line that wins this game.
