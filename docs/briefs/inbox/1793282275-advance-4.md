Buildings he rebuilds (vehicle plant H1, Pulsar frame at H3) are worth a detour; his commander was never found after 4:00, so a factory-kill sweep is the win condition rather than a commander snipe.

Four packet mistakes of mine to not repeat, all of which cost minutes. First, naming a fight place but not naming the place where he actually shoots you: at 16:28 my army was walking away under Hound fire at spot_56/58 because that spot was not in the fight list — say "close on whatever shoots you, anywhere west of spot_46". Second, "advance to spot_X now" when the group is already past spot_X sends it backwards; check the group's reported place before writing an advance. Third, "no detachments" and "never answer raiders" are both traps when written absolutely: at 26:09 X4 outweighed each Fatboy party fourfold, so the hands would only offer detachments and my rule had the body walk past them under fire. Write "eight at each party, the rest marches on". Fourth, a clause like "kill Fatboys on the way" let X4 chase a bearing all the way home at 24:18-25:07 and left C5's nine Bulls alone bleeding under the gantry's Overwatch — any clause that can point backwards will point backwards.

Two mechanical things: a new plant stands with no list and sits idle (happened twice, plant_8715 and plant_25389), so check every new factory the turn after it finishes; and plant output joining a far-flung group drags that group's reported place home, which makes you think it retreated — route new output to the home guard instead and lift the company cap (0) when you want the body to march as one formation. Mid-retreat, do not rewrite the packet; it re-asks every group. Constructors left idle build redundant Sentries, sometimes in a plant's exit lane, which strands units; give them wreck fields to reclaim and forward spots to fortify instead.
