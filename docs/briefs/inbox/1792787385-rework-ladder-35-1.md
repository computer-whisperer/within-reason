Later, his Welder/Centurion/Rocketeer/Mace parties eat small Blitz groups the same way; Blitzes lose to Centurions, so send Stouts home and keep the home body together behind the turrets with the commander.

His tier 2 (Hound, 650 range) showed at 13:45, so the tier-1 lead has a short clock. March the middle road early with one big body, do not wait to be bigger, and reclaim the wrecks in our own middle for about 1,500 metal while constructors retake C6/C7/D6/D7 with turrets.
