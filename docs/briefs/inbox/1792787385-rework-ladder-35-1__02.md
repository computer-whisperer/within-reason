Write that split early, not at 17:00.

At D5 he had a Mammoth (650 reach, 15,600 hp), two Sumos, an Arbiter (1,210 reach) and Sheldons. Our Brutes, Pounders and Tigers reach 410 at most; only 4 of 18 soldiers could touch the Mammoth. The lesson for whoever sits here: against this opponent you will build a metal and trade lead by 17:00 and you will be tempted to cash it by walking into his heavy line. Mass of metal does not beat reach. Before any push east, count how many of your soldiers can actually reach his longest gun; if the answer is a quarter of them, do not go. Get Banishers (800 reach) or Tzar out of the advanced plant first and let the heavies come to you — holding spot_59/spot_63 and attacking only what walks inside 600 was winning. Being shelled while you wait is not a reason to attack; it is a reason to step back one spot.

Also: never send the army toward E3/F2, where his Wardens sit, and never toward D4/E4 once the Mammoth and Arbiter are there. The F4 light-turret post and the F5 Agitator will beat a tier-1 trickle — kill Agitator frames while they are frames or leave them alone. Watch the plant exit lane; twice I found constructors building in it and had to pull them off.
