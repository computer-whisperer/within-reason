We won at 21:30 from the W corner on Comet Catcher Remake, but the win came in spite of the last ten minutes, not because of them. Here is what I'd keep and what I'd do differently.

Opening (keep almost verbatim). Armada line: extractor, extractor, solar, solar, vehicle plant by ~0:34, third extractor, then commander assists the plant at low priority. Plant order: Rover, con, 2 Rovers, con, 3 Blitz, con, 3 Blitz, con, then Blitzes. Put a light turret beside every outer extractor from the very first one — this is the lesson from the game before and it paid again here: a single enemy Rover arrived at 2:06, killed the spot_69 extractor twice, chased a constructor home, and shot a home extractor before leaving at 2:55. Total cost one extractor, because turrets were already queued. Without them a lone Rover farms your whole east line. The constructor lists that worked: first con east/north-east through 69, 76, 63, 61 (radar), 64; second con west through 62, 54, 50, 45; each spot gets a Sentry.

Two commander mistakes to avoid. First, at 1:35 energy stalled (35 stored) because commander assist draws more than 70/s, so I ordered two solars at full priority — and by 4:45 energy was full at 1597/1600 while metal was empty. That was a wasted ~30 seconds of commander time. Read both stores before buying energy; an assisting commander's stall is transient. Second, the commander stood in the plant's exit lane for 30 seconds at 4:24 and blocked output. Keep it out of the lane; send it to take spots (71, 64) with Sentries instead, which is what finally worked.

The real curve lesson. By 7:26 I had 1764 metal banked and energy balanced at 240 — the bank was unspendable because energy, not metal, is the limit (Stouts cost ~93 e/s per plant, a nano 3200 e). Solars cost no energy to run, so the answer is a wall of solars early and often: I put the commander on 6 and a new con on 3 plus a nano. Later the same wall appeared again at 14:42 (2266 metal, 740 energy in, plants wanting 1500) and I paid for a fusion from the bank. Build the energy ahead of the metal both times; you will not out-bank this opponent, you out-spend him. Advanced vehicle plant at 11:20 off 24 extractors vs his ~14 was the right timing — before his tier 2 matters.

The push is where I lost the game's shape. Everything after 10:00 was one recurring failure: the body halts in ranks whenever any armed enemy has it in reach, so single units freeze twenty-plus Stouts indefinitely. group_J froze at spot_47 (10:03-10:42), at spot_20 under one pecking Blitz (11:26-11:48), and worst, at spot_20 for minutes against one kiting Bull (460 range vs Stout 350) while Shellshockers trickled us for 2.9k metal a minute. What actually breaks the freeze: name a fresh mark as the first sentence of the packet (push_1, push_2), set lane raw and company 0, say reinforcements follow and are not waited for, forbid every detachment size, and if a single out-ranging unit is the problem either detach a few faster units to hunt it or simply `go` the whole body to the thing you want dead. The 16:05 decision to walk the whole body ~1,500 to his AVP instead of trading at range was the turn that won the game; make that decision ten minutes earlier.

Commit fully to nests. At 12:08 group_J was inside his F2-F3 nest (Gauntlet 1220 reach, Overwatch, Beamer, Claw, Sentries, ~2,500 metal of turrets). Killing the turrets cost ~2,400 of Stouts but walking out under the Gauntlet would have cost the same for nothing. Once you have paid the approach, go in. Same at the commander kill: all in, footwork fan only so the body spreads under the D-gun but never steps back; two Stouts to the D-gun is the price.

The reinforcement trap — the one thing I never solved. From 17:50 on I formed two big Stout groups (group_S2, group_I2, ~35 Stouts, 6-8k metal) to join the kill and they never arrived. New Stouts leaving the plants kept joining those groups at home, so the group's anchor stayed at home while a few members stood at his front; the groups were strung across the whole map and their best-rated move was repeatedly a walk home. Strike signals at 18:18 and 18:28 did nothing. Meanwhile group_J bled from 38 soldiers down to 5. Do not let the fighting group be the group that new plant output joins. Send new production to a named reserve at a rear spot (spot_47 worked as an idea) and move it forward as a separate body only when it is whole. And once a group is strung out, do not re-order it every turn — I finally reset by gathering everything at spot_23 (E3) on one formation, fan only, with a single signal to go at a fixed time, and I held still through four turns while it gathered. That held and won.
