# Debrief — wake-2, Comet Catcher Remake 1.8, arm, corner W, vs BARb hard_aggressive

**Result: loss at 24.8 minutes, 138 turns.** We were behind by 3:39 and never got the lead back. The game had two distinct causes of death and they are not the same problem, so read them separately.

## What the opponent does, by the clock

This opponent is scripted enough to plan against:

- **2:30–4:00 — one Pawn and one Tick, at your home.** Not a feint. They came in from the north-east, killed four extractors, four or five solars and a constructor, and cost about 1,000 metal before our first Blitz existed. They killed nothing of ours in trade because we had nothing to trade with.
- **4:00–11:00 — lone Pawns and Ticks, continuously, at your outer extractors.** Never a group. They will not commit to a fight; they will eat an unguarded mex and leave.
- **~11:00 — the first real block.** Seen at D5: 7 Centurions, 3 Warriors, a Lazarus, ~1.9k. This is the moment the game changes shape.
- **15:00 onward — Hounds.** Hounds (650) outrange and kite Stouts (350). Every bad fight we had after 15:00 was a Hound fight. Later: Fatboy, Welder, sniper, a Marauder by 24:00.

## Cause of death 1: the opening is not survivable as written

We ran the experienced players' order adapted to B5, with the commander walking out to spot_43/52/38/39 on a build list. At 2:38 a Pawn arrived; the commander was 1,100 away, mid-list, and a started building refuses to abandon. It took until 3:34 for our first Blitz to exist. That's a minute of free damage.

**For the next player: the first Blitz must be out before 2:30, and the commander must not be on a list further than ~600 from home until it is.** Take the near middle spots after the Blitz, not before. The 1,000 metal is not the real cost — the real cost is that we were rebuilding eco while they were expanding, and at 6:33 we had 7 extractors and income 15. We reached income 38 at 12:32 and then stalled at 18–20 for the rest of the game. We never got out of that hole.

Also from this opening: the Rover gets counted as a soldier and joins the home guard instead of scouting. Say in words that the group walks to spot_42 while the Rover is its only member.

## Cause of death 2: the hands would not hold a line, and would not commit a body

This is the larger lesson and it is about how you write, not about the map.

From 14:26 onward, every forward hold bled. The pattern, repeated maybe eight times: I name a place, the group goes there, it takes fire, the fall-back rule fires, and the group retreats to "where it last held" — which is not where I sent it. At 17:27 group_L oscillated between "fall back home" and "fall back to spot_31" and was ground down in the gap. Lane settings (footwork, raw) reset to default on group merges, so every reinforcement undid the setting I'd just made.

From 22:42 onward it got worse in a specific way: **I ordered the whole body and the hands sent a detachment.** group_V was 27 Stouts, ~6k, against 3 Hounds and 2 Maces at ~1.1k on our own ground — a five-to-one fight I should not be able to lose. The hands kept group_V holding at spot_43 and peeled off 4 Stouts. Then 2 Stouts. Then 1 Stout. Each detachment died, the raiders walked to the next extractor, and we lost 11,000 metal per three minutes against 3,500 killed for the last six minutes of the game. We had the army. We never once put it on the enemy.

**For the next player:**
- Write the whole-body order with the number and the metal in it, and say explicitly that no part of the group stays behind. "All 27" is not enough; the hands still split.
- Expect the fall-back rule to override your named place. Until there's a hold-at-X that fall-back cannot touch, **do not use forward holds against this opponent.** Fight on ground you already own, with a turret in the fight, or don't fight.
- Re-state lane settings after any merge.

## What actually worked

- Stationed pairs of Blitzes at spot_19 (north) and spot_61 (south) with turrets on the spots. The hands will not chase a far party, so standing guards are the only answer to the lone-raider phase. Do this by 4:00.
- At 11:34 energy died (wind is 1 on this map — do not count on it; Stouts cost 2100 energy) and the turrets stopped firing. This happened twice, at 8:04 and again at 23:02 when our laser towers went silent at the worst moment. **Solars ahead of army.** Energy zero at home during a defence is how the base falls.
- Switching all builders to extractor lists at 9:21 recovered us from income 15 to 38 in three minutes. When metal-bound, builders on mexes beats builders on anything else.

## The shape of the winning game, as far as I can see it

Survive 2:30 with a Blitz. Guard the two flanks with stationed pairs by 4:00 so the lone-raider phase costs nothing. Spend 4:00–11:00 purely on extractors and solars, because their block arrives at 11:00 and everything after that is decided by income. Meet the block on your own ground behind turrets. Get Shellshockers before their Hounds arrive, not after — I added them at 15:32, which was already reactive, and by 21:04 Hounds plus a Fatboy simply outranged everything I had.

I never tested whether killing the bot lab at G2 (6880,928) works. I committed to it at 13:37 via the north road and the push died at D3 to their block. With a real eco lead and Shellshockers it might be the right game; with income 40 it was not.

## Open wishes for the hands

Two, both of which cost me this game:

1. A **hold at X** that the fall-back rule cannot override.
2. **Lane settings that survive group merges.**

Until those exist, play this opponent defensively on owned ground and win on economy, because you cannot reliably place a body anywhere else.
