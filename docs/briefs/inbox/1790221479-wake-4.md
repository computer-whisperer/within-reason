## The two real mistakes

**1. Blitzes are not a line unit here, and you will find out at 12:23.**
Blitz mass raids fine (group_E cleared middle-north extractors at 9:13) but dissolves on contact with anything real. At 10:51 we lost 11 Blitzes to *two* Centurions. At 12:23 we lost 16 units in 12 seconds at D3/D4 to a Gunslinger, a Hound, Centurions and LLTs — that is a T2 bot lab talking, and hard_aggressive has one by then. Switch the plants to Stouts before you take that hit, not after. The Stout ball then worked immediately: 34 Stouts broke the E3 nest at 14:30 (beamer, Gauntlet, 2 LLT, mex, radar) and killed the Advanced Bot Lab at G2 at 16:10 for a 9.6k-to-5.8k three-minute trade.

**2. Don't try for the AVP.** Started at 12:23, dropped at 14:14 having produced nothing: five builders kept opening frames at different sites and none got past 0%, while all the metal went to Stouts regardless. Tier-1 Stout mass plus HLTs is the line that actually stood. If you want T2 on this map, commit one builder to one site and say so, or skip it.

Also: budget roughly **8 solars per plant**. At 8:11 we had two plants making Blitzes at 900 E each, energy pinned at 0, 2100 metal banked, and every near builder idle on solars it had no energy to build. That stall is where the economy lead stopped compounding.

## Why the game was lost: the army would not gather

From 18:56 to 21:05 — over two minutes of real game, a dozen turns — the whole task was "put the army in one place and walk it onto the commander." It never fully happened.

- group_O held at spot_39 and ignored the order to leave **three turns running**, 51 seconds of holding on a single wake. It only moved after the orders were rewritten with a *closer* meeting point and **one plain order per group**.
- group_P, told to "attack" a party near G2, went from 30 soldiers to 14 while drifting back and forth for more than a minute. Attacking a party at range with no engagement is how you lose an army for free.
- At 20:28 the push sat still because the hands were still acting on the previous "advance to spot_13".
- At 21:05 both groups, 88 soldiers and 20,000 metal, reached spot_11 and the hands started walking them *back* to spot_3.

The lesson for whoever sits here next, and it is the whole game:

> **A rally is one order, one destination, one group at a time, and the destination must be close.** Compound orders ("gather at spot_8 then push via spot_3, spot_11, spot_6 onto the commander") do not survive the hands. Issue the gather. Confirm arrival. Then issue the attack.

And: **an order in flight costs units.** group_P lost 10 tanks while the last orders were travelling, 5 of them to a single D-gun. Budget for that latency; don't re-aim a group that is already mid-move unless it is losing.

## If you play this again

1. Same opening, turrets before far extractors, group_A named as the scout.
2. Eight solars per plant before the second plant.
3. Stouts from ~10:00, no Blitz line, no AVP.
4. Take the E3 nest and the G2 lab — that part works and buys you a 2:1 trade.
5. **Then stop and gather, in single orders, at a point the army can reach in under 30 seconds.** We had the metal to win twice over from 16:10 onward and spent the last five minutes walking it in circles.
