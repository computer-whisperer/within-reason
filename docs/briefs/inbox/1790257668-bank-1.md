# Debrief: bank-1 — Comet Catcher Remake 1.8, arm, corner W, vs BARb hard_aggressive. Lost at 29.2 min.

You will read the score at 15:00 — 9.8k army against 4k seen, 22 extractors — and think you are winning. I did. The game was already decided by then in two places that don't show up in that number: the opening's metal, and the fact that your hands will not hold a push. Everything below is about those two.

## The opening: the plant is the clock, metal is the constraint

Order from A4: extractors on 28 and 30, then solars, then the vehicle plant. What went wrong for me: I let the commander run its full solar list, and **metal hit 0 at 2:05 with energy full at 1,200**. That is the whole opening failing in one line. Four solars went up before the plant; the fourth and later ones were paid for out of a bank that the plant needed.

For you:
- **Two or three solars, then the plant. Never four.** If energy is full and rising, the next solar is a mistake regardless of what the list says — cut it the moment you see it, don't wait for the stall.
- After the plant, the commander's job is to *guard the plant between single builds*, not to run a long list. And put it on `assist`-style steps, not a queue list: **a queue list takes the commander off the hands' menu entirely.** I lost a commander response to raiding Pawns twice for this reason (3:32 and 22:56) — it stood 460 away from a Pawn eating our extractor and ignored it. Cancel the list the instant you want the commander to react to anything.
- The store should never sit at 0. If it does, you are building the wrong thing, not building too slowly.

## The plant's exit lane

The plant at spot_36 had our own spot_36 extractor in its exit lane. Blitzes sat stuck for 30 seconds; I reclaimed the extractor at 3:46 to free our only factory. **Check the lane before you place the plant, and don't put an extractor on a spot that sits between a factory and open ground.** I later cancelled a second AVP at spot_36 for the same reason. This cost me 14.7 minutes of yard trouble across the game.

## Scouting

I lost the first Rover at 2:43 to a lone Pawn, and did not see the enemy base for the rest of the game. Every commit I made after that was toward "where the base should be" (G1). **A scout that falls back when threatened never scouts.** Give it a run-and-never-fight route by the south early, and send a second one before the first is confirmed dead. Not knowing where the base was is why my 15:01 north-edge commit walked into a fortified E1–E4 column (Gauntlet, HLTs, beamers) that I had never seen.

## The real problem: the hands will not hold a push

This is the thing to plan around, because it is not fixable by writing better instructions mid-game. I tried for twelve minutes and failed.

What the hands do:
1. **Attack orders end in "fall back to where it last held."** So every advance undoes itself — the ball walks forward, then walks back to its station. I worked around it by moving the group's station forward step by step (spot_13, then 8, then 3) so the fall-back point follows the advance. That works, and it is slow.
2. **They split a group into 4- and 8-soldier detachments** that trickle home one by one. At 20:31 group_J became seven detachments of eight and I lost 9 extractors in 3 minutes. Saying "no detachments" does nothing.
3. **They issue attack, then fall back 3 seconds later**, so a ball with 700-range fire on it simply walks away while being shot. At 25:44 two Fatboys and a Sharpshooter killed ~26 units in 30 seconds while this happened.
4. **They never `send_against` from a holding group.** At 19:50 a 2.6k AVP died unfinished to three Pawns at home while two groups of 70 soldiers held nearby.
5. **They never pick reclaim.** 4.6k of wrecks sat on ground we held. Bot-lab Lazarus bots work wrecks on their own — that's the way to get it.

What actually worked, and what I'd do from the start:
- **One ball, named party, follow/focus/march on.** Turn on follow/focus/march for the attacking group so an engaging ball stays on its target. From 21:41 to 23:49, one-ball attacks against explicitly named parties gave me my only positive trades of the game (killed a Fatboy and two Sharpshooters; 10.3k killed vs 9.6k lost).
- **Name the party in the order, and re-name it every turn.** At 26:21 group_J stood still doing nothing because the parties my previous orders named had left sight. The hands had nothing to engage and did not look for a substitute.
- **Don't re-ask an attack that's already running.** When the ball is engaged as ordered, leave the packet alone; re-asking resets it.

## Their army, in the order it arrives

Pawns and Ticks raid single unturreted extractors from about 2:30 on; put a turret on every extractor as it goes up, not later. Around 15:00 T2 arrives: Hounds (650 range), Gunslingers, Centurions. Then **Fatboys**, and Fatboys ended the game — splash killed 14 of a packed ball in 11 seconds at 27:34, and three of them broke the army at 28:36 (30 units in 9 seconds). Against Fatboys: **don't pack the ball**, and a Gauntlet at 1,220 range outranges them at 700. I built that at 27:34, about ten minutes late. Mausers from an Advanced Vehicle Plant are the other answer to their E-column turrets — I tried to get there twice and never did, because the AVP kept dying or getting cancelled.

Friendly fire was 13.2% of our damage, three quarters of it the Stout. A ball that is three-quarters Stout shooting into a packed melee is shooting itself.

## The shape of the loss

Between 15:00 and 18:00 I traded 5.4k lost against 2.0k killed while my army was nominally twice theirs, entirely from oscillating pushes and detachments. I then spent 18:07–21:00 turning to economy instead of fixing army control, and the economy fell 26 → 18 anyway because the raids were unanswered. By the time one-ball attacks started working at 21:41 they were T2 and fortified and I was rebuilding extractors instead of pushing.

**If you take one thing:** get the opening's metal right (two or three solars, plant, guard it, store never 0), keep a scout alive, and from the first fight onward run **one ball, follow/focus/march, one named party at a time, station moved forward by hand**. Do not trust the army-strength number; trust whether the ball is standing on the enemy or walking home.
