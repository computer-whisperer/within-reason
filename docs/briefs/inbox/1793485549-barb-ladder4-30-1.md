How the game went, in three phases.

Opening to 10:00 — this part worked and I would repeat it. Three home extractors, two solars, vehicle plant, commander then helping the plant at low priority with solars woven between helps. Constructor first out of the plant, then Rovers, then Blitzes. Two things I had to fix in the first two minutes, and you can have them for free: energy stalls at 1:30 (70 in against 216 drawn with 500 metal banked) — put the commander on two solars at once before it helps again, and keep doing that every time the store empties; and roving Rovers flee from a Tick they would beat in two seconds, so a lone Tick or Pawn among our extractors has to be named as a kill task, not left to the raid mode. The commander's D-gun killed the first Tick at 2:31. By 6:09 we had 16 extractors and 39 income, by 7:49 twenty extractors and 48 income with 1,430 banked and his army around 1,050 and already turning to Centurions and Rocketeers. Second vehicle plant and a construction turret at 6:09, advanced vehicle plant at 7:49 paid from the bank, fusion started around 9:30 behind sixteen solars.

10:00 to 16:00 — the expensive part, and where you should expect to differ. His Hounds and Gunslingers, two or three of them, outranged everything we had and picked off our northern spots (spot_44, 47, 49) from beyond our reach. Blitzes cannot answer that. Between 11:03 and 11:53 we lost better than fourteen Blitzes, ten of them in one minute purely because the hands thrashed between spot_33/44/47/59 while under fire. The cure was to name one place (spot_59, among our Sentries) and have everything merge there, forbid the spots north of it, and turn expansion west; then build Mausers out of the advanced plant beside Bulls with a radar at spot_59 to spot for them. Once the Mausers existed the Hounds died (three of them by 15:40, 2,100 of his for 1,250 of ours) and we retook the west and middle. If you can get Mausers and a spotting radar up before 11:00 you skip that whole bleed. Also: his Ticks eat extractors from 3:50, and Sentries at the clusters are the only thing that actually stops them.

Economy traps I hit, all avoidable. The fusion frame got placed in the advanced plant's exit lane and had to be reclaimed at 8% (433 metal thrown away, and it later showed as a fake 3,350 loss in the report) — put the fusion in a bare corner, A8 worked. Converters had no room at home and needed a marked site at the west edge. And three advanced converters built next to each other at fus_site/conv_site were all killed by one Marauder, two of them by chain blast: keep converters at least 200 apart. Past 13:00 energy ran enormous surpluses (+1,870 with 12,900 banked, later +1,000/s) while metal sat at zero, so converters are the constant late lever, not an afterthought. Plants also go idle silently when metal is in balance; counted lists restart them, and I had to do that at 14:13, 30:26 and 36:35. Watch for it.

16:00 to 40:00 — this is where the game was lost as a win. By 30:00 we had 55k of army against about 18k seen, and I spent that lead badly. I sent group_R3 (44k) on a sweep of the south row to kill his southern extractors; they were bare (spot_78/79/75/70 found nothing, 33:47). Twelve minutes of unseen ground was not twelve minutes of his economy. Meanwhile our own extractors fell from 14 to 9 to 7 because the home guard was fighting a 12k party with a Razorback and two Vanguards. With a +30% bonus against us, time is his: if you hold a 3:1 army lead, go at his base immediately and directly, do not tour.

When R3 finally went north up the east edge for his lab, it died to layered static defence: Pulsars appearing one after another at F4, G4, G2, H2, with Rattlesnakes, Overwatches, Beamers, and finally a second Titan (armbanth). His turret line in the north-east is the real obstacle on this map, not his army; plan the approach around Pulsar reach (1,400 and the Basilica's 4,650) or bring something that out-ranges them.

Three hands lessons worth more than the rest. First, against an armthor (56k health, 1,050 reach, EMP) send one whole-group attack order and keep it, or stay entirely outside 1,050 — our group alternated between standing off and rushing and paid 12k in a minute for it. Second, a group told to follow a moving shelling estimate will wander: group_X1 walked east to spot_66 chasing one, and only a named way (spot_59, spot_47, the target) with an explicit instruction not to follow shelling places fixed it. Third, groups thrash between "advance to X" and "walk to X" and stand still a few hundred short; say always advance, never a plain walk, and name his buildings as the fight. Also never change the packet mid-fight — every time I held the orders through a commitment it went better than when I meddled.

What I would do differently, in order: Mausers plus a spotting radar by 11:00 instead of feeding Blitzes to Hounds; converters early and spread; and at the first moment we hold a 3:1 army lead, one committed push at his lab with the approach routed off the north-east Pulsars, rather than sweeping ground. Our economy curve on this map is fine — 20 extractors by 7:49 and a fusion by 12:56. The whole game turned on spending the resulting lead on his base instead of on empty map.
