The three things that cost the game

1. A group fed from a plant is not a body. This bit me twice in identical shape (11:11 and 13:35) and cost about 2,200 metal for nearly nothing. New Stouts joined group_O by name but stood at the plant — lane blocked by solars, then trickling across the map — so the group's Blitz front fought Centurions and Rocketeers alone and died. Never order a push with a group that a plant is still feeding. Name a gather place, say the group waits there until it is whole, and only then give the route. Better: have plants feed a *home* body and move soldiers to the push group in named batches.

2. Wrong instrument, repeatedly. Blitzes against Centurions is a losing trade; Blitzes also cannot catch the 5-9 Pawn/Blitz raids that took our extractors from 21 to 17 in three minutes. Stouts can't catch raiders either. Against raids, the answer was turrets plus a pooled home body beside them, not chase. Against his tier 3, Starlights (950 reach) and Pit Bulls (730) worked when they finally stood — too late. Get the advanced vehicle plant far earlier than 16:51 and have the Razorback answer *built before* the Razorback arrives.

3. The home guard shredded into fragments every few minutes. At 9:41 it was pairs chasing single Pawns; at 23:49 nine 1-2 Blitz groups chasing Ticks while Pawns took two spots; at 28:42 eighteen fragments while a Razorback ate the base. I re-pooled it by hand five or six times and it came apart again each time. Write the home guard once as a single named group with an explicit "fights whole, beside the turrets, never leaves the ring, lone Ticks are the Sentries' problem" rule, and say that new soldiers join that group and hold at its place — don't re-pool reactively.

What I would do differently
Hit him before 12:00 with one whole formed body, or else don't attack at all and tech instead. The middle ground — pushing at 16:20 and 21:35 and 25:17 with a lead I kept failing to deliver — gave him every minute he needed to reach tier 3. At +20% his expansion and tier 2 come sooner, so the window is earlier than you expect. If you are at 2x army at 15:00 and he is still tier 2, that is the moment; after his gantry stands your Stouts are irrelevant.

Also: get the advanced vehicle plant down by about 12:00, not 16:51, and put mohos on it immediately. My economy was fine (fusion at 21:05, a second at 24:37, income 88 with wreck reclaim) — the wrecks in our half (8k at one point) are real metal and two constructors on them pay. Energy went empty twice under Bulls+Mausers+mohos; plan 1,100-1,800 draw before building the units that draw it.
