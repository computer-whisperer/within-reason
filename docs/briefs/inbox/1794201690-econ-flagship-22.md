Two concrete traps in the first six minutes. Energy and metal alternate as the limit all game, and the whole skill of this benchmark is noticing which one and switching within a turn or two. Energy ran to zero at 5:45 and again at 6:35; a solar costs no energy to build, so solars are the one thing that still rises at full speed during a blackout. Metal banked near full at 6:10 and 13:00 onward.

The real lesson is the late game, and it is where I left income on the table. From 14:00 I had 10-20k metal banked and rising ~185/s, and I spent it on advanced fusions: 312k build each, four started, exactly one finished by the end. A plain fusion is 54k build, about 8 seconds under a block of construction turrets, and gives more energy per build second. Switch to plain fusions as soon as the bank first outruns your build power — around 13:30, not 15:10 as I did. From 14:00 build power is the binding constraint, not metal, so put idle constructors on construction turret frames early and order the turrets' work explicitly: whatever you want finished first must be at the top of tend, or the turrets will all pile onto the slowest frames and the new ones get nothing. I had to re-order tend four separate times (turrets first, then fusions, then converters, then fusions again) chasing the limit.

The chain that actually paid: fusions for energy, converters to turn surplus energy into metal, construction turrets to build both fast. Converters cap at what energy sits above their start line, so they fall to half output the moment frames elsewhere draw energy. Final numbers: income 483 at 14:05, 545 at 15:10, 652 at 15:41, 720 at 16:48, 875 at 17:47, 989 at 18:39, with 76 converters making 335 of it. Energy income reached 17,380/s against 14,400 used, and the energy store sat full at 16:38 — wasted. The metal store capped at 49,600 near the end, so past 18:00 more extractors are worth nothing; only converter throughput is.

I reused list ids that already belonged to earlier plain solars, and the builders silently skipped those steps — most of the advanced solars I ordered at 15:31 were never built. Use fresh ids. And the dense turret block penned about ten constructors who could not get out to work; space the block or leave lanes through it.

If I sat down again: same opening, constructor start on the advanced plant, spread the block, and from 13:00 pour the bank into plain fusions plus converters with turrets explicitly ordered fusions-then-converters. 989/s at 20:00 should be beatable by a fair margin.
