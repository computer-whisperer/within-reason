## What bled metal (all of it avoidable)
- **Blitzes vs Thugs/Aggravators (6:57).** Blitz trades badly from here on. Switch to Stouts and Janus, then Whistlers and Janus — they outrange Thugs.
- **Feeding in one at a time (9:10).** Soldiers walked into a 640-metal block singly. Gather into one body at spot_41 beside the Sentries and fight only when the body outweighs the block.
- **Chasing south of spot_41 (12:38):** 1,130 for 610 in a minute, under Wolverine artillery from out of sight. Don't chase off the line.
- **The H7 turret nest:** ~1,400 metal in one minute at 17:02, ~1,650 the next, ~1,000/min again at 24:15. Stay **west** of H7 until the body outweighs the nest many times over (that was ~23:17, not before). Route via spot_66, 67, 79, 75.
- **The F7/F8 field** (laser towers plus the Viper at G8, reach 730): ~2,000/min. Bring Mausers and shell the Viper first; I did that at 20:30 and 21:01 and it worked.
- **D8 at 27:51:** 0.8 to 1 against a Doom turret and a 1,245-reach Agitator, ~2,700 metal per 10 s. I pulled back to spot_79, gathered 49 reinforcements, and only went in once t1/t2 could come to D7/C8. Do that the first time, not the second.

## Two hands rules to turn off, and when
- **march** — at 25:03 the body stood 365 short of spot_75 for 30 s waiting on a tail 1,400 back. Turn march off for the main group once it is one body on a known route.
- **flee** — at 26:34, 58 soldiers were stepping out of turret reach while the rest died, so the attack trickled into the Shiva. Turning flee off committed the whole body at 1.7:1 and killed the Shiva and the gantry. **Turn flee off for any committed assault on a turret line**; leave it on for the line-holding phase.

## Economy notes
- Advanced constructor at 7:15: mohos on our extractors home-outward, plus **four advanced solars** to carry the moho energy cost. Build the energy with the mohos, not after.
- Banking is the recurring failure. 1,400 at 7:35 (second plant), 1,764/1,800 at 8:01 (sent 900 to t1), 3,268 at 14:55, 5,400 at 26:00 (fourth plant + two construction turrets), 5,000 at 28:53. **Open a plant or a storage one turn earlier than feels right**; the income outran my build power all game.
- The reverse happens too: t0 empty for two minutes at 19:34 with three plants idle while t2 banked 4,000. Ask for the transfer as soon as you see the split — I was a turn late both directions.
- Energy, not metal, is the thing that stalls (9:00, 23:01, 29:00). Converters are the first cut.
- **Exit lanes**: constructors stuck in plant exit lanes at 14:12, 15:55 and 29:00. Reclaiming the stuck one clears it; better, keep the lists moving them out.
