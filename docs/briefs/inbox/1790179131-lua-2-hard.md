# Debrief: lua-2-hard (Comet Catcher, arm, corner W, vs BARb hard): lost at 28.5 min

## What happened, in three phases

**0:00–5:30, the raided opening.** I ran the players' order: extractors on 45 and 50, three solars, the plant, then the commander out to 43/52/38/39 with turrets. A single Pawn and a Tick took three extractors before our first fighting unit existed. By 3:26 we had lost 337 metal and killed nothing. The commander was 1,100 away building and its attack rule never fired. We sat at 2–3 extractors against the 5 wanted by minute four, and income stayed at 8 until 5:00.

**5:30–17:48, recovery without a lead.** Turret-before-extractor and Blitzes hunting raiders brought us to 13–18 extractors and income 25–36. A Blitz raid through the south-middle spots at 10:48 was called off at 11:48 when their army showed. From then on, endless small raids (Pawn trios, lone Pawns) kept us at 13–15 extractors. I spent many turns rewriting the army's rules: detachments, patrols, pairs, one ball, back again. We won a fight at C3/B3 at 16:19 but lost a plant doing it.

**17:48–28.5, the failed commit and the slow death.** With 6.2k army against 3.7k seen, I sent the whole ball (25) along the north edge toward their lab at G2. At C3 it walked into a Mace/Centurion block of about 14 we had not seen. It killed 9, lost about 12, and was wiped out by 19:35. Sixteen Pawns then walked into the base while the replacement ball chased line bots at C3. Their tier 2 (Welder, Hounds, Sharpshooter, Fatboy) arrived at 20:45. Raiders killed our solars, energy hit zero at 24:23 and metal income fell from 28 to 10. We stabilised at home late (trade even at 27:16) but were down 32k lost against 19k killed.

## What to do differently

1. **Have a fighter before the first extractor leaves home.** The first raider arrives around 2:30. Put the first Blitz ahead of the scout car, or keep the commander near the outer extractors with a working attack rule. Check that rule actually fires by watching the first contact; ours never did, early or late (2:51, 22:46, 23:48).
2. **Turret beside every outer extractor from the start, not from 3:00.** This was the one change that turned the early trade.
3. **Never commit the ball without scouting their block first.** Their line army parks at C3, between us and the north route to G1–G2. Send a scout or a cheap probe down the route before the ball. If you can't see it, assume it is there.
4. **One ball, one rule set, written once.** Five small groups and eight rewrites between 14:51 and 17:09 cost more than any single raid. Single Stouts and pairs got eaten; detachments wandered. Answer small raids with turrets and the commander; keep the army as one leashed ball.
5. **Protect energy.** Solars at home are the soft target once raiders get in. Spread them, wall them with a turret, or bank energy storage. Losing them at 24:23 halved the rest of the game.
6. **Distance checks in leash rules.** My "stay home" rule sent the commander 1,800 away to spot_19 at 22:51, because the target came from a raid list rather than from the home fight. Test any leash against the positions it will actually receive.
