We won at 27:00 from the west corner (B8) against BARb hard_aggressive on Comet Catcher Remake. Most of what follows is about the second half, because the first fifteen minutes went by the book and the last twelve did not.

The map and the opponent. His base is at G1/H2 and has been every game on this line: bot lab at about (7200,800), commander at H2, extractors and solars at G1, extractors at G4 and H3. Scouting found it at 2:41 and you can plan on it. A bot lab means Pawns and Ticks early, so Blitzes are the right answer and win roughly 2:1 in the open; that window is 4-7 minutes and it closes hard. At 9:36 three Centurions under a Sentry at F4 ate six of my Blitzes for a Sentry and some Pawns. Treat the Blitz raid as finished then and bring it home; switch the plant to Stouts and Janus and the advanced plant to Bulls. His tier 2 (Gunslinger, Hound) showed at 14:13, his first moho at 19:04, his first tier-3 Marauder at 26:05. If the game runs that long he has a gantry somewhere, and the game before this one lost 26k to a nuke, so an anti-nuke at home is cheap insurance.

The opening that worked, unchanged from the last two wins: two extractors, two solars, vehicle plant, third extractor, two more solars low (they cover the 1:30 energy stall), then guard the plant low. Plant list: Rover, constructor, two Rovers, constructor, three Blitzes, constructor, three Blitzes, then Blitzes. Name the spots in the commander's list and put a light turret beside each home extractor before about 2:10 — a lone Tick killed one at 2:27 in an earlier game, and a turretless frame at C6 died at 4:18 here. Give each constructor a fixed expansion route of extractor-then-turret pairs the moment it exists, or it will stand at the plant assisting. Scout with a Rover early even though it dies; mine bought his whole base layout for 31 metal. The advanced vehicle plant goes up around 5:30 on a named constructor by id (the commander cannot build it); check the frame's percentage rises within a minute.

Economy notes. Energy is the binding constraint twice: once in the opening stall, and again from about 10:30 onward because Starlight shots and plant assists drain thousands a second. Plain solars cost no energy to build, so when energy is pinned they are the only thing you can add; mohos and fusions come after. I ran three fusions by the end. At 23:30 I was fourteen seconds from empty and had to pull the commander and two assisting constructors off the plants onto solars purely to cut their assist draw. Watch for metal banking with energy flat — I sat on 2.1k metal at 11:43 — and spend it on build power and a second advanced plant rather than letting it pile up.

The real lesson is the army, and it is about how the hands read your packet. Three failures, each cost minutes:

A gather written as "until push_two is given, gather at spot_49" was read as standing. At 17:25 push_two was given and the group still rated holding at 0.62. Write the push as the current job, not as a condition on a gather. I lost ten minutes of tempo to that phrasing twice.

Groups will not hold a line. The hands split the main body into seven or eight pieces chasing single Pawns at 10:26, 12:49 and 21:02, and while it was split a 1k Centurion party and a 5-Pawn party killed D6/E5/E6. Forbid detachments by size, cap the hunters at one or two Blitzes per lone raider, and set standing pickets — Blitzes at spot_56 east and spot_43 north — so raiders meet soldiers before extractors. Even then, at 22:40 and 23:20 the main body kept turning back to Welder raiders at our own F5 extractors while a Rattlesnake shelled it; I had to explicitly forbid attacking parties at our own extractors and hand F5 to a separate Stout group with Sentries.

The army does not close on a nest. From 18:44 to 22:40 it repeatedly stood at 400-500 range taking fire from a Beamer, then a Fatboy, then a Gauntlet and a cloaked Rattlesnake at F2/F3, trading 3.1k for 0.6k in bad minutes. Naming the static gun by its id as the move helps; chasing mobile Fatboys and snipers that back off under cover does not. What finally worked at 22:50 was abandoning the F2/F3 nest entirely and going round east — G5, G4, H3, his bot lab at spot_12, then G1 — out of the Rattlesnake's reach. That is the same route that won the previous game. Do that earlier.

What I would do differently: keep pushing expansion past 5:00 harder than I did (I was down to 20 extractors at 12:49), gather the whole heavy body before touching a nest rather than bleeding into it piecemeal (the 19:45 regather to ~19.7k was right and I should have done it at 18:00), and go east around the middle instead of grinding F3. Also: plants go idle silently when their list is spent — mine stood idle at 16:55, 17:55, 18:24, 20:02, 22:20, 25:05 — so always end a plant list with an uncounted repeating entry, and point new soldiers straight into the main group rather than letting them muster in an exit lane. Keep constructors out of plant exit lanes; I moved three off them mid-game.
