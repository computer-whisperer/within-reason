This was an economy-only run: NullAI easy never attacked, so nothing I did involved defence, and no turret was ever needed except as build power. What worked, and I would do again in the same order:
- Opening: three home extractors, three solars, vehicle plant by ~1:10. Drop the far home spot (spot_36, 588 away) from the commander's list and let a constructor take it; the simulator said the walk pushed the plant to 1:42, and skipping it got the plant up half a minute sooner.
- Give the plant an explicit count of construction vehicles (I used 16, twice). Left to the hands the plant sat idle, because another constructor rated only 0.09. Constructors are the whole game here; never let the plant idle.
- Re-split the map so no two constructor lists share spots, and hand each new constructor a route the moment it leaves the plant. Idle or plant-assisting constructors were my commonest waste early.
- Read which resource is the cap every turn and buy only the cheap one. At 1:55 metal was empty and energy full, so the commander's assist on the plant bought nothing — I sent it 1,000-1,400 out for two extractors instead. At 7:40 metal was nearly full and energy zero, so plain solars (no energy cost to build) beat starting a fusion that costs 18,000 energy.
- Advanced vehicle plant with the commander plus three constructors on it, standing ~6:35. The commander cannot build armavp itself, but it can help a frame a constructor starts.
- Pivot from solars to build power once the first fusion stands (~8:50): a dense block of construction turrets, turrets building turrets first, and the advanced plant making advanced constructors so mohos go on every spot. 71 spots at 6.9 m/s each is the biggest single lever in the game.
- Reclaim the T1 plant around 10:20 once you have enough constructors; its products were eating 843 e/s of turret power.

The three mistakes that cost the second half, in order of size:
1. Converter frames placed with `start` outside the turret block's reach decayed at 1% and never stood. I lost dozens of frames this way between 12:30 and 19:00. Converters belong inside the block, placed normally, with builders staying on each frame. Never use `start` for a frame no turret can reach.
2. The turret block had too few frame slots — 3 of 18 free all late game. Build the block bigger and more open than feels necessary; late on, metal is free and frames, not build power, are the bottleneck.
3. The advanced plant kept producing armacv with ~2,900 build power of helpers on it near the end. Stop constructor production once you have enough builders for the frames you can actually hold; that energy and build power should be on fusions and converters.

Ordering notes for the turret list: it is a strict priority and it bites. Turrets ranked advanced fusion above converters and the converters decayed; I flipped to converters first (380 metal for 10.3 m/s repays in 37 s — it is the best buy whenever energy is spare). Late, with metal wasting and energy the cost, the right order was fusion > advanced fusion > converter and turrets last, and I cancelled turret lists entirely in the final two minutes.

Energy, not build power, caps converter count in the midgame: eleven converters saturated 6,600 spare energy at 15:30, so more converters simply waited. The sequence that unlocks each step is solars → first fusion (+1,860) → advanced fusions (+8,960 for two, +34k by four) → converters to turn the surplus back into metal. Get the second and third advanced fusions down earlier than I did; they were my real limiter from 13:00 on.

His commander sat at G1 and shot one of my constructors to 46% — the last two metal spots next to it are not worth contesting in an econ game, and keep builders away from it.
