Between 2:36 and 5:11 single Grunts (corak) killed five extractors or extractor frames, a half-built turret and a constructor, and we spent four turns chasing one of them with the commander and single Blitzes. First, every constructor list at an outer spot is turret FIRST, then extractor — I only switched to that at 5:11 and the losses stopped. The finished light turret at spot_62 killed a Grunt by itself; the 16%-built one at spot_63 lost the extractor, the frame and the constructor. Second, Stouts (350 reach) outrange Grunts, so get the plant onto Stouts early rather than more Blitzes. Note also that the commander's own attack on a lone raider was rated 0.42 by the hands, under the 0.5 they need, so the commander will not actually chase unless you take it off its build list — and taking the commander off its list to chase one Grunt is a bad trade.
