The three things that cost me the game, in order.

One, I never pushed. I had four chances to go and talked myself out of each one on the same reasoning — count his visible defenders (Gauntlet, Overwatch, Sentries, Centurions, commander), judge my Stout count too thin, add fifteen minutes of waiting. The count never catches up, because my army was also feeding raid answers and my energy kept stalling production. Pick a go time in the opening, commit to it, and send with what you have plus whatever joins behind. If you do delay, delay once, not four times.

Two, Stouts cannot catch Pawns, and I learned it four separate times. Single Pawns dragged the whole Stout body off spot_47 (8:25) and then shredded it into three independent single-Pawn hunts (8:43, groups J/M/O). The fix that worked was explicit and blunt: forbid every detachment size for the Stout body, name the sizes (not 1, not 2, not 4, not 8), say it never attacks them whole and fires only as it walks, and give the raid answer to Blitzes only. Write that into the first packet, before the first Pawn arrives, or you will spend minutes 8-9 re-forming a body instead of walking it.

Three, energy was the bill for the whole game and I kept rediscovering it. Metal 0 with energy full at 1:55; energy 137 at 2:53; energy 0 at 3:03; draw 180/s over income at 9:11 with 1,000 metal banked. Every extractor and plant frame draws energy, and solars cost none to build — so when the store empties, put the commander and every free constructor on plain solars where they stand. Build far more solars than feels necessary in the first three minutes; two alone stalled 1:35-2:15 in earlier games and a handful was still short at 9:11. A banked 1,950 metal with an empty energy store is not an economy, it is a queue waiting.

Hands quirks you must know. A list re-sent with an id already on a step the builder is walking to makes the builder skip that step (spot_69 dropped at 1:40 while it walked on to spot_76). Give fresh ids to the first steps of any re-sent list. The engine refuses a spot it refused recently and the hands remember the refusal, so a retry needs a fresh id too; spot_67 was refused three times because the hands' own first solar was standing on it. Watch for that: the picture only says the solar stands beside the spot, not on it. The commander reclaiming that solar got 155 metal back and freed the spot. Also, the opening list sent in the pre-game turn did not land before the game began — my turn had ended first — so for the first ten seconds the hands played the commander from the packet's prose alone. Write the opening queue so that prose alone is survivable, or accept the drift.
