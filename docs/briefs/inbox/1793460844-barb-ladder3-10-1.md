What to do differently here

Do not push into F3/F4/G4 with tanks, ever. That nest is the opponent's whole defence and it ate two separate assaults. If you want his base, go along the north: row 1 via the east or north edge (spots 52, 38, 26, 24, 13, then 8, 3, 11, 6, 10, 5 = his lab, 12), avoiding the D4/E3/E4/F3/F4 belt. His south row (E8-H8, H7) and the H column are outside his middle posts' reach and are the cheap expansion.

Convert the lead early and on economy, not on a hero push. At 10:00 we were banking 700 with three plants and no tier 2 in sight; the advanced vehicle plant was not started until 13:45 and mohos not until 16:00. That is five to eight minutes late against a +10% hard opponent who techs. Start the advanced plant as soon as the bank floats, put the first advanced constructor on mohos, and get long-reach units (Mausers 820, Starlights 950) out before his 1380-1400 posts exist rather than after.

Hold our half with Sentries beside every extractor as the extractors go up. Late in the game we were down to 3-5 extractors with 19k of wrecks lying at home; reclaim kept income alive (34 → 112) but you cannot win from there.

Hands lessons that cost us real metal

Never mark a region whose centre is his nest. An old `his_half` mark kept pulling B1 back toward F4, and at 14:25 the walk home from G3 routed straight through the F3/F4 turrets and wiped 4.1k in a minute. Write retreat routes as explicit spot sequences away from his turrets.

The default behaviour of the hands is to fragment: groups split into 1-4 tank hunts after Ticks and small raids constantly (5:50, 22:42, 28:40, 35:54). Every packet needs "fight as one body" said plainly, and say it to every group, not to a group name — group names die and respawn (G9 dissolved into S10/T10/U10 and two of those parked inside a Pulsar's reach). Consolidating all field groups onto one named group worked every time I did it (28:50: 20 tanks, 10.3k, stragglers joining).

The "eight may go against small parties" allowance I added at 35:05 was a mistake: it immediately shattered an 18k body into 8-tank hunts and lost 5k/min for 2k. If you need a home guard, make it a separate standing group at a named home spot, not a detachment allowance.

Give constructors explicit expansion lists early. Both of mine sat helping the plant until 2:13. Also watch plant exit lanes — plant_6893 was blocked by our own solar and nano at 15:22 and needed reclaiming.
