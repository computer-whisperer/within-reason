The one thing you must know before you write a single order: on this map our commander starts under water at M4, and the harness could not put ships or hovercraft into any group. I declared typed sea groups, then any-slot navy groups, then plain any-slot groups; Supporters never took a slot, and later Mangonels and Caymans never took a slot either. That means every floating unit you build is unorderable — it sits at home and shoots only what wanders into reach. I spent six turns rediscovering this (4:11 Supporters, 8:43 Garpikes can't fire from deep water, 10:03 hovers also ungroupable). Do not repeat that arc. Assume floating = immobile decoration until you have verified otherwise in your own first two minutes with one cheap ship and one declared group.

What this did to the game. His start is B4, also under water, roughly 11,600 west; his economy is in the sea and on islets, and my Rascals found no land extractor of his in rows 1-3. So the enemy is somewhere you cannot reach with anything you can command, while his Supporters, Herrings (650 reach) and submarines pick off our construction ships and shoreline tidals with impunity. He killed at least three construction ships and two Supporters and shelled home water from outside our sight. I was never raided on land at all. The whole game was: his cheap navy taxes our coast, we grow inland, nobody can finish anybody. That is the shape of the timeout.

What worked, and is worth keeping. The land half of this map is free. We reached 12 extractors by 4:11, 19 by 5:21, 39 by 11:30 with 89 metal a second, and 122 a second by 12:44, his land half entirely unoccupied. A vehicle plant on land at landing (12950,3150) built from the sea start is the right pivot and it worked cleanly. Torpedo launchers (cortl) at home and at the N5 cluster are the only thing I found that actually deters his boats from a spot, and I built them far too late. An Agitator (corpun, 1,245 reach) plus a radar at landing is the answer to a Herring parked off our shore out of sight — I only ordered that at 12:17.

The real failure was energy, twice, and it cost the mid-game. From 10:47 to 12:17 we were flat-stalled: 785 in against 844 out, store at 39, while 3,400 metal sat banked. Cause: I queued advanced solars, which cost 4,000 energy each to build, during a stall — you cannot build your way out of an energy hole with things that eat energy. Plain solars cost no energy and are paid for in metal; switch to those and put your construction turrets on the solar frames before any factory product. A 600-power advanced constructor being assisted draws about 500 energy a second and will hold the stall open by itself. Get the fusion started early with several builders, not one: mine crawled at 5% for minutes because the commander and two vehicles were assisting a hovercraft platform building a Mangonel no group could command.

Three build-prerequisite traps I paid for in turns. The advanced aircraft plant (coraap) is built only by a construction aircraft (corca) — not by an advanced construction vehicle (coracv), which was my wrong guess at 11:47 after the plain corcv had waited on an empty list forever. Any list item nobody on the field can legally start simply never starts; nothing warns you. And the construction aircraft is our weakest builder, so the plant it starts sits near 0% unless you pile others onto the frame.

What I would do differently, in order. Verify groupability of a ship in the first minute. Take the land half fast as I did, but put two or three torpedo launchers along our own waterline by 4:00, not 4:40 piecemeal, and a radar plus Agitator at landing by 7:00 — that ends the coastal tax and buys the game. Then go straight to air: aircraft are the only thing here we can group and the only thing that reaches his water. Skip the Garpike and hovercraft detours entirely; they cost me 8:43 to 10:03. Build the aircraft plant by about 7:30 instead of 10:20, fusion in parallel with many builders, then the advanced aircraft plant and Anglers — torpedo bombers, because his army is entirely boats and submarines and his commander itself sits under water at B4. At 14:00 my advanced aircraft plant was at 63% with the first Anglers still unbuilt; that is the whole difference between this timeout and a kill.

One last note on the commander: at 4:51 it was rated 0.39 to attack a raiding Supporter against 0.42 to walk home, it could not attack while running its build list, and after driving the boat off it abandoned a 9%-built torpedo launcher to go build a solar.
