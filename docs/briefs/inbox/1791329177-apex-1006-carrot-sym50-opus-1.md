# Debrief: Carrot Mountains v2.0, arm, corner W, vs Apex Unstable (standard)

**Result:** win at 21.7 min, 115 turns. Read this before your first turn; the first five minutes are where this game was decided and where I was slowest.

## The map, from corner W

- You start in **B5 on the west edge**. His start is on the **east edge**, almost certainly **H5 or H4** — mine was H5, with his bot lab at spot_100 and his commander at spot_98 (G5). Assume that and have Rovers confirm it by 2:20.
- **Wind is good here (6–12 with bonus, ~14 e/s for 40 metal).** A solar is 155 metal for less. When energy runs out early, build **wind**, not solars. I only worked this out at 2:11 and it was the single best correction I made.
- **spot_96 and spot_99 sit inside vehicle-plant exit lanes.** So do some of the home rebuild spots. I sealed my own plants twice (plant_1272 at 16:31, plant_13578 at 17:51 — the second cost group_D1 **77 seconds** stalled 49 units short of its waypoint) and had to reclaim my own extractors to unseal them. **Never build an extractor at spot_96 or spot_99**, and when you rebuild home spots after a raid, check the lane first.
- **passage_1 (around D2/E2, north) is his supply route.** His bots streamed through it from the north all game, with resurrection bots behind them. A standing guard at **spot_95** against raids out of passage_1 was worth it (I made group_D1 that guard at 16:11).
- The far west — **A2/A3/B2/B3 north, A7/A8/B8/C8 south**, plus spots 142–147, 90, 155–171 and 43/49/52/53/58 in the north-west — stays unclaimed unless you put constructors on **explicit lists** for it. My extractors sat flat at 31 for a full minute with 16 constructors idle-ish because I was relying on area behaviour instead of naming spots.

## The opponent

Apex Unstable standard is a **bot-lab player**: Centurion, Mace, Rocketeer early; **armzeus** appeared at F6 at 17:31 and an **armack** (tier-2 builder) at H6 at 19:49, meaning an advanced lab I never located. He also runs **armrectr resurrection bots** heavily — at G5 he raised **2.6k of wrecks** while my army chased the rez bots instead of his buildings.

What this means for you:

1. **Blitzes lose to his units.** One Centurion killed two Blitzes (220 metal for its 270) and outranges them 325 to 180. Use Blitzes only as the early rove/raid force and stop making them once you see his lab.
2. **Stouts are the answer** — they beat any tier-1 bot head on. Get a second plant on Stouts by ~4:30. My whole winning body was Stouts with a Shellshocker every ~5 and a constructor every ~6, on standing counted lists so plants never idle.
3. **Shellshockers break his turrets.** His Overwatch + Beamer at G5 and the Beamers at G6 held my body up for minutes. Shell from 710 with Stouts screening outside 620.
4. **Kill the lab, not the units.** Destroying his only known bot lab at H5 (14:34) was the turn the game tipped. I then wasted 14:34–21:36 hunting a second factory and a tier-2 lab I never found. **If you see armzeus or armack, find and kill the advanced lab immediately** — Fatboys would have ended me.
5. **He raids home constantly.** A 10-Pawn raid cost me ~2,000 metal (solars, a constructor, a Shellshocker). Answer raids with the commander, Sentries, Rovers and newly built units — **never turn the main body around**. See the mistake below.

## Opening that worked

mex, mex, solar, solar, armvp (the pros' most played line), commander helps the plant. Plant: 2 Rovers, cv, 2 Rovers, cv, then Blitzes with a cv after every three. Rovers rove his half immediately; name places on turn two once the start is known.

Targets: **five constructors by 4:00**, and I had **12 extractors at 4:31**, which was ahead of pace. Hold that.

## The three things that cost me most

**1. Energy stalls, three times (5:01, 8:06, 9:20).** Two plants plus Stouts outruns your income. At 9:20 I had **48 energy income and 2.8k metal banked** — the bank was a symptom, not a surplus. The fix that works: **basic solars need no energy to build**, advanced solars crawl at an empty store. So the standing rule in the packet became: every home constructor and the commander builds **basic solars while STALLING, and never helps plants then**. Put that in your packet from the start and you will skip all three stalls.

**2. Flipping the body.** At 8:34 I gave the storm signal with group_B at 2,510 plus 722 nearby. At 8:43 the group turned home on its own for a Pawn raid; I read it half way back and let it finish, then re-stormed. The real attack didn't walk until **10:12**. Nearly two minutes of army lost to one reversal. Decide once and leave it; at 12:29 I deliberately left a losing-looking grind alone (3.1k lost vs 2.5k killed) because income was 120 on 36 extractors — the grind was affordable and his base thinned. That was right.

**3. Letting Jev's ratings steer the body.** Repeatedly the group kept an **old pick** (an "advance to spot_105" at 13:28, a "shell a Pawn" from 16:06 that drifted it back west at 16:31) or rated a detachment on a rez bot in our half above the real attack. Two practices fixed it:
- **Put the group's single move first and keep the packet short.** Long packets get out-voted.
- **Name only the forward way.** At 19:59 I learned that naming a place the group must *not* return to puts it on the menu — Jev picked spot_83 at 0.58 for exactly that reason. Say where it goes, never where it mustn't.
- When the attack is under turret fire and the soldiers hang at the turrets' edge, **set lane raw** so the order reaches the engine without form/flee holding them (14:04, and again at 17:51 for the stuck march).
- **Forbid splits explicitly.** Under the G5 turrets the body split into detachments at 1.3:1 at the front. "No splits, tail closes up, split groups rejoin group_B" is packet wording that worked.

## Two tool notes

- **Lists cannot queue several solars.** Repeated bare `armsolar` steps are each "taken as done" by the build already under way — a list of four made one. Giving every step a distinct id did **not** fix it; the builder's own walk to its first solar still consumed them (9:30, 9:40). Use the **packet** for multiple solars, not a list.
- **A group must exist before you can name it in a plant's build order.** My 1:09 attempt to send Rovers into group_R was refused and no Rover ever joined it; the scouting setting went through and did nothing. Create the group with units first.

## Mid and late game, briefly

Spend the bank. At 10:12 I had 2.7k banked and put up a **fourth vehicle plant**; two nanos on the plants earlier spent a 1.5k bank. Late, with **11k metal of wrecks in safe fields**, a home **bot lab making armrectr** was worth building (18:11) — he was doing it to me all game.

Once his bot lab is down, sweep for his commander and his second factory with the body and run a second Stout group north through passage_1. His commander turned up at **H5 (12209,7007)** at 21:36 fighting with a Platypus and rez bots; killing it with everything ended the game.

## If I played it again

Wind from 0:30 and a STALLING solar rule in the opening packet. Second plant on Stouts at 4:00 instead of 4:31. Storm once at 8:34 and never look home. And from the moment armzeus appears, every unit goes at finding the advanced lab — I won at 21.7 minutes largely because he never got Fatboys out, and I can't claim that was my doing.
