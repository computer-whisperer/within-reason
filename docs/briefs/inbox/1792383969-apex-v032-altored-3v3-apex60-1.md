# Debrief — apex-v032-altored-3v3-apex60-1
**Altored Divide Bar Remake 1.6.2, arm, corner N, seat t0 (east, home G1). Apex v0.3.2 standard at +60 resource bonus. Timeout at 60:00; our east base and commander were dead by 58:16.**

## What this game actually was

A clean opening and a healthy east strip for fifteen minutes, then a 35-minute stall, then Apex's tier 3 walked up row 1 and deleted the seat. The opening was not the problem. The problem was that t0 never acquired the ability to fight anything with reach, and spent half an hour as an economy seat that could not grow either.

## The three things that decided it

**1. Punisher creep is this opponent's win condition. Treat it as the clock.**
Apex turned its won lane into a turret nest at G6/G7 by 7:11 (I fed it 7 Blitzes in two minutes learning that), then walked Punishers (corpun, reach 1245) *northward*, mutually covering: one at 12:11, two more by 15:57, five by 16:07 — reaching spot_44 inside our strip. Nothing tier 1 outranges 1245. From 16:00 on, every free metal spot in our half (45, 47, 48, 50, 53) was under their fire, and extractor count froze at 17-19 for the rest of the game.

Next player: the nest is cheap to deny before it exists and impossible to clear after. Either kill the G6/G7 builders in the 4-7 minute window with the Blitz harass (and accept losing the Blitzes to do it), or write off that lane *immediately* and plan long reach instead of raiding into it. Do not do what I did — harass until the turrets are up, then screen and hope.

**2. Get t0 its own tier 2. By 14:58 I had no advanced constructor and no bot lab.**
That single fact cost: no answer to Punishers (asked t2 for Mausers all game, got none in time), no Lazarus for ~4,000 metal of wrecks sitting at D7/E7 at 15:38, no flak against the corape/Cortex Dragon, and no path to growth once the free spots were covered. A seat with no tier 2 and no free spots has no moves. Start the advanced plant by ~13:00 on this map even as the "economy seat" — especially as the economy seat, since that is what the bank is for. I sat on 1,100-1,460 banked metal at 13:01 and bought converters with it.

**3. The side commander took my army three times; I let it happen without renegotiating my job.**
At 11:28 the 15-unit push body went to t1; by 11:38 it was happening by default and I declared t0 "the economy seat with a small home screen." That is a coherent trade only if someone defends the east strip. Nobody did. When your body is being transferred repeatedly, say out loud to the side commander what the east base now has for defence and what it needs; a screen of Stouts and Whistlers is not an answer to anything Apex fields after 15 minutes.

## Smaller lessons, concrete

- **Wind on Altored is 4-19.** I opened on wind for the high end and stalled on energy at 1:59, 6:24, 7:11 and 13:01 at wind 4-6. Take the two wind, but put solars in early and advanced solars by ~13:00; at wind 18 (16:37) the store fills and converters are correct. Do not plan the opening around the top of the range.
- **Anti-air is a schedule, not a reaction.** His air plant stood by 6:24; corshads then took G2 (9:39), spot_40 with its turret and Nettle (17:03). Every time I added Nettles/Ferrets *after* a loss. Put a Nettle up with the turret at each spot cluster as it goes up.
- **Forbid ground groups from chasing aircraft** (I did this at 17:03, far too late) and let the Whistlers answer bombers.
- **Stuck units get executed one at a time.** At 43:49 and 46:24, 10 and then ~23 soldiers sat inside a Toaster's reach for minutes while footwork failed to move them. Setting footwork to `raw` is the workaround; the real fix is never parking a group inside a long turret's reach and checking for "stuck" in the report as an alarm, not a curiosity. (Wished-for lever, filed twice this game: an order that moves or reclaims units the engine has given up moving.)
- **Evacuate on sight, not on contact.** The Juggernaut was visible at G3, 1,400 from spot_21, at 50:12. I ordered the evacuation at 50:42, after it was inside the base. Thirteen converters, both Gauntlets, the turrets and then the plants were gone in the interval. When a corkorg/Shiva is in sight and you hold tier 1, the base is already lost: start the constructors and the body west on the first sighting.
- **Constructors on `assist` lists do not obey a packet walk.** They stayed and died. Re-list them with an explicit move step.
- **A refuge in the same row as their walk is not a refuge.** The commander died at B3 at 58:16 to a Juggernaut at B2 — reach 900 against our 950-ish escape margin, speed 38 against its 37. It cannot outrun one. When Juggernauts reach row 1 (56:37 here, at t1's home), go *south*, perpendicular to their walk, immediately.
- **Transfers stop landing once the seat has no commander** (58:45: our store kept reading 500 after each send). Stop sending and say so.
