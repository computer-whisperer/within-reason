WHAT I'D TELL THE NEXT PLAYER

Assume the hands may stop moving groups for ten minutes or more and have a plan that survives it. The one thing that kept working through both stalls was bot-run lists: counted produce lists and build lists act without the hands. I used that at 25:07 to spend an 11.7k bank on Bulwarks (cordoom, 950 reach, which outranges a Tzar's 650) and Scorpions at home and at both yards, at 38:16 for advanced radars at B7/D7 and more Bulwarks, and at 39:49 to walk the commander east to a safe mark by giving it a build list at the destination. A commander on a build list moves even when nothing else does. If your body is frozen and bleeding, stop feeding new production into it — route new units into fresh groups at home and spend the bank on static reach instead.

Don't mass Tigers against these two. Build for reach: Shivas (800) and Tzars, with Bulwarks behind. Keep the body as one block and gather before going in; every time mine strung out it died piecemeal to things it couldn't see. Get advanced radar up early — being shelled from beyond sight was the shape of nearly every loss.

Build a Prevailer before you need it, and keep a T2 builder alive. thebluegecko puts a silo at B2 and will nuke your home and then your plants. Allies killed his anti-nuke at A5 at 34:34; nobody killed his silo, and that was the game.

Take u6bkep's E2 base early while it's thin. Route east of his turrets via spot_162, spot_136, spot_112, spot_94 and avoid the E3 Beamers and the D3 turrets. And when you commit a body to a direction, don't re-aim it across the map — the walk costs more than the target is worth, which I learned twice at 16:37 and 34:34.

Late, if the base is gone, the commander's survival is a loop plus cheap builds. Keep it moving: Dragon's Teeth cost 8 metal and no energy, so they still get built with an empty energy store, where a Thistle frame would freeze the commander in place as a missile target. Mix Thistles back in when energy returns. Pick the loop corners farthest from the air sweep. Mine dodged a missile that way.
