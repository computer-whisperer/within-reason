What the map is. Every extractor draws about 30.6 metal a second, so metal is never the limit — energy and build power are. Wind is a steady 30; a turbine gives 25 e/s for 40 metal, so turbines by the dozen are the right energy. Solars cost no energy to build, so if you stall with an empty energy store and a full metal store, solars are the way out. Our plateau (A1–B2) holds about a third of the map's metal. Its only exit to the middle highway is one pass about 352 wide; the highway runs roughly 13k east to his corner (his economy is in L–M, his forward tier-2 camp was at J1 near 9850,850).
