## What to do differently

1. **Energy before the second plant.** Energy sat at zero from about 4:29 to after 7:57, with metal banking to 500–1,700. Two plants plus nanos outran the solars. Put down solars (or an energy converter plan) ahead of every build-power step, and check energy on every wake until about 8:00.

2. **The first three minutes need a turret or a Blitz earlier.** One Pawn and two Ticks took four extractors between 2:48 and 3:10 while the only soldier was a Rover. Put a light turret on the first outer extractor, or bring the first Blitz forward in the plant queue. Hard_aggressive raids by 2:30.

3. **Never commit the ball where a party can come into sight.** This is the lesson that decided the game. Telling the hands in words not to chase, "advance only", or "never north of spot_32" did not hold. They chase any party they see: 8 Stouts lost at 15:51, 13 at 18:52, 13 at 20:23, and then the collapse. What did work at 18:10 was a raw lane along a spot route (44-46-40-...). If you attack:
   - Use raw movement with the route spelled out.
   - Keep a separate Blitz pair whose only job is Pawns.
   - Don't go past spot_44 (F4) without an answer to Fatboys (Bulls or Janus in numbers).

   Otherwise, hold where no party is in sight, and let the enemy come to you. Our wins came at home: spot_39 at 11:46, and spot_38/39 at 21:53 and 23:32.

4. **Stouts alone lose to their tier-2 block.** Maces, Centurions, Hounds (650 range) and Fatboys beat a pure Stout ball. Hounds also pick off the pairs the hands peel off. Shellshockers (710) outrange Hounds. Janus and Bulls are the Fatboy answer. Start the AVP and that mix before about 15:00, not after the army is gone.

5. **They reach tier 3 around 23:00** (Marauder, Razorback, Sharpshooter, Gunslinger). Expect tier-3 raiders in your half by then. Keep a rebuild crew with light turrets on the home extractors (spot_61, 63, 50, 45, 52), and keep the commander free to D-gun.
