## Lessons, ranked

1. **Keep the extractor count climbing.** Do not add plants past what income feeds; roughly one plant per 8–10 extractors plus nanos. When metal banks, spend it on constructors that take spots, not on a third or fourth plant. Flat income from minute 7 is how you lose to hard.
2. **Get tier-2 by about 13–15 minutes.** Hard_aggressive has Centurions around 12:00, a Fatboy and Sharpshooters around 19:00, a Pulsar and a Marauder around 24:00, and a Razorback around 25:00. Stout and Janus mass can hold a turret line, but it loses every open-ground trade against these.
3. **Convert a big army lead within about 2 minutes.** At 4:1 (around 17:00) the push has to go and has to reach their base before their tech arrives. Hesitating hands the game to their tier-2.
4. **Never send Blitzes into Centurions or a formed army.** Blitzes are for raiding Ticks and extractors only.
5. **Scout before you push.** A Pulsar at D2/E2 and unseen Sharpshooters killed both late pushes. Put a Rover or radar ahead of the ball, and don't stand inside a Pulsar's 1400 range. Either go around it or don't go.
6. **Before any fight that needs the commander, cancel its build list.** An active list hides it from the hands, and nothing warns you.
7. **Their base extends west to D1/D2.** By the late game they had a moho extractor at D1 and a Pulsar at D2/E2, not only the H3/G2–G3 area where their commander was first seen.
