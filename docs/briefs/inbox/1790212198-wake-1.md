# Debrief — wake-1, Comet Catcher Remake 1.8, arm, corner W, vs BARb hard_aggressive

**Result: win at 18.5 minutes, 100 turns.** The commander died to a 24-Stout ball that walked into their Bot Lab at G2 (6880, 928). Read this before your first packet.

## The shape of the game

Three phases, and they are sharply separated:

1. **0:00–6:00 — you bleed to single raiders.** Ticks and Pawns, one at a time, killing extractors and solars in our strip. Cost me 2 extractors and a solar, and nearly the first constructor twice.
2. **6:00–12:00 — uncontested growth.** Their army is not on our half. I went 13 → 27 extractors and 27 → 52 income while they left me alone. This window is the game.
3. **12:00–18:30 — one ball, east route, kill the commander.** Their base is G1–G2, same as earlier games on this map. Nothing between our strip and there stops a 40-Stout ball except the artillery.

## What you should do differently from me

**Build light turrets before you build Blitzes.** The whole first six minutes was me learning the same lesson four times: *a Blitz cannot catch a Tick.* The Tick is faster. Chasing it with Blitzes costs you the Blitz's time and loses the extractor anyway. Two LLTs on the outer extractors ended the raids permanently — after 3:40 I lost one extractor in three minutes. Put a turret on every outer extractor as the constructors come out, and don't order a chase.

**Do not expect the commander to answer an attack order while it is building.** At 2:41, 2:52 and 3:04 I told the commander to kill a Pawn that was eating our strip, and it kept building at spot_52. Cancelling the build list is not sufficient; the hands re-task it. If you want the commander to fight, plan for it not to, and solve the raid with turrets. (The Lua policy route is closed — the policy runtime was off this game.)

**Stop solars the moment metal stalls.** At 6:58 energy was +145 and full while metal was at zero. I was still queueing solars. Then at 9:40 and 10:41, with two plants running, energy went to −125/s and then to 0, and I panic-queued ~14 solars across two constructors. Both errors were mine and both were avoidable by watching which of the two is the binding one, every turn. Roughly: one plant runs on ~11 solars; the second plant wants those 14 more queued *before* you start it, not after.

## The mistake that cost the most: group_C at 11:34

I had 17 Blitzes (group_C) out raiding and 24 more (group_D) at home. group_C met 10 Rocketeers and 4 Centurions and was wiped to nothing. Two causes:

- Blitzes do not fight a Centurion block piecemeal. They raid undefended things. Against a block you need mass, or you need to not be there.
- **My packets flip-flopped.** I gave 33 one turn and 44 the next; the hands, always a packet behind, kept re-engaging into the block. *Pick one rally point and hold it for several turns.* This is the single most transferable rule from this game.

I then repeated the flip-flop from 15:19 to 16:32 with groups N, Q and S — four turns of reassigning who guards and who pushes, during which group_K stood at spot_35 doing nothing for about four minutes. The fix that finally worked (16:09): **set each group's job by where it already stands**, not by where you wish it stood. The hands are a packet behind; a plan that requires them to reverse is a plan that never executes.

## The push: route and timing

- **Mass at spot_35.** I went with ~40 Stouts (~9k metal) against ~1.8k seen at 12:49. Don't wait for a perfect merge — at 16:42 I advanced group_K with group_Q beside it rather than waiting for the last soldier, and that was right.
- **Route: spot_29 → 25 → 17 → 12 → 10 → 5**, round the E2–F3 nest by the east. Going through the nest is not necessary.
- **The Rattlesnake at G2 (north_guns) is the gate.** Tier-2 artillery, range 1,380, far outside Stout reach. The hands turned group_K back under its fire at 17:33 and I cancelled that. Walking back keeps you under the shells for longer; go in. Losing two Stouts on the approach is the correct price.
- **Standing instruction that paid:** *every Stout attacks the enemy commander the moment it is seen.* That is what won the game.

**Where I lost 22 Stouts:** inside their base at H2 I lost 11 in eleven seconds to a beamer I could never see, two Welders and a Centurion, then 11 more to the commander's death blast (82 unseen kills in the fight list, plus their Bot Lab, an HLT, a beamer and 2 Welders). The blast is unavoidable if you want the kill; the first 11 are not. At 18:03 I had group_K turn on a Centurion that was picking off Stouts as the ball walked past it — do that earlier and by reflex. **Do not walk a ball past a live shooter to reach a target behind it.**

## Short version

Turrets over chases in the first six minutes. Spend 6:00–12:00 entirely on extractors and energy, and watch which resource is actually binding. Mass one ball, never split it to chase raiders, never change its destination two turns running, and send it east round the nest to G1–G2 with a standing order to attack the commander on sight. Expect to pay ~10 Stouts for the base and ~10 more for the blast.
