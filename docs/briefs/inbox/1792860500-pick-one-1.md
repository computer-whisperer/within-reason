# pick-one-1: the player's debrief

Match `run/matches/1792860500-pick-one-1/00`, build f51a2a0fa330, against BARb stable hard_aggressive on Comet Catcher Remake 1.8: Win after 24.4 min; 129 turns. Debriefed by claude-opus-5 (effort low) in 41 s, in a shape of its own (DECIDED / NEXT PLAYER / WISHED was asked); since snapshot: 7-day +0.0 points, extra usage +0.00.

We won at 24.4 minutes on Comet Catcher Remake from the west box (B8 start) against BARb hard_aggressive, and the shape of the game is simple: the opening and the economy were never in doubt, three pushes into his turret nest cost us about 7,000 metal for little, and the game was actually won by sitting out of reach until we had Bulls and twenty-thousand metal of army, then walking through him in one body from 17:00 on. Expect to be bored and rich from 12:00 to 16:00; that is correct, not a failure.

Opening, which I would repeat without changes: extractor, extractor, solar, solar, vehicle plant, third extractor, then the commander helps the plant at low priority. Plant list Rover, constructor, 2 Rovers, constructor, then Blitzes with a constructor every third unit. We were level with the strong players' median at 1:10 and at or above their upper quartile by 3:54. Two things cost me metal here and are avoidable. First, "stop" in a constructor's list drops whatever it is holding: I dropped a solar at 98% with 151 metal in it and another started extractor, twice in four minutes. Give a new list that begins with the thing it is already building rather than stopping it. Second, the plant's first units all joined one group, so the first Blitz went scouting instead of chasing his raider; put each unit type in its own group from the first order.

His single early Rover will be in our south-west base by 2:00 and will eat frames at C8 for ten minutes if you let it — it killed two extractor frames and chased a constructor off a turret. A Rover outruns a Blitz (168 vs 101), so Blitzes only drive it off. The answer is the light turret beside the home and outer extractors, early, which is in the kept lessons and which I did build at spot_73 and spot_76. Also note my raid rule only fired on three-or-more units: a Stout+Janus pair at D4 and later a lone Bull both walked in freely. Write the detachment rule for pairs and singles from the start; a four-soldier detachment was enough.

His base is in the opposite corner, H2 at about (7202, 848), found at 2:14 by the early scout. The thing that matters for the whole game is his turret nest at F3/F4 across the middle: an Overwatch plus radar at F3, a Beamer and two Sentries at F4, with three to five armart behind them and Stout/Janus parties roving in front. I attacked it three times. At 10:09 group_Z arrived strung out 1,200 deep and traded 1.3k for 1.3k. At 11:49 I pushed with 4.2k because the group was already engaged and bleeding, and lost 1.5k for about 80 metal killed. Do not feed that nest with T1. The correct order, which I only reached at 12:09, is: hold a rally point out of the Overwatch's reach (I marked rally_z in E6), guard the middle, and expand instead — north-west A4/B3/C3 and the whole middle row of spots behind our own turrets.

The economy lesson is the one I would most want the next player to have. Energy was the binding constraint from 2:00 to 15:00, repeatedly, while metal banked: 1.3k banked metal at 2:46, 1.7k at 7:51, 2.1k at 13:49, 2.7k at 14:29. Every time I noticed late. Build energy second and continuously, not in reactive batches of three solars; an advanced vehicle plant and a fusion both stall on draw you have not built yet. Watch for the inverse too: by 16:37 energy was full at 10.2k with metal at 0, and two advanced converters on the advanced constructor were the right answer. Sequence that worked: second vehicle plant at home ~5:33, advanced vehicle plant ~11:19, advanced solars for its draw, advanced constructor 14:29, fusion immediately, then mohos on home spots, converters when energy caps.

Place the advanced plant yourself and check where it actually goes. I marked an avp_yard near home at (2000,5050); the frame appeared forward at C5 anyway, pulled the commander out of position, and I cancelled and re-placed once before accepting the C5 frame (it was behind our own turrets, so it was fine). Keep builders out of plant exit lanes — I pulled constructors and even the commander out of lanes at 2:46, 9:19 and 22:55, and one constructor stood idle there for sixty seconds.

Bulls are what break the nest. From about 15:18 I built Bulls only out of the advanced plant with two construction turrets on it, gathered at rally_z, and pushed at 16:50 with roughly 10k against his 2k seen. That push worked, though at 18:46 we still lost 4.4k of soldiers for 1.2k of his soldiers plus 4.0k of buildings — acceptable, because buildings do not come back. Shellshockers (710 metal) are the right counter to his armart; Janus for turret posts. The F3/F4 post was dead by 21:05.

Two routing faults cost real time and are easy to avoid. At 17:07 the group dithered between spots after the artillery died; I gave an explicit waypoint route from D3 through his extractor line (spot_21, spot_13) then the E3/F3/F4 turrets then onward to his base, with footwork set to fan and form rather than flee or march so it commits onto turrets. That worked. Then at 21:45 the group stalled 46 seconds on a stale "advance to his_half" course and would not re-pick the route — the fix was deleting the his_half mark so the course ended. Prefer explicit waypoint routes over region marks for the main body, and delete a region mark once you have replaced it with a route.

The endgame was a sweep, not a battle. From 21:05 the army grew from 16.3k to 22.6k while walking: killed a Pulsar frame plus two advanced constructors and two mohos at G4 (~6.1k) at 22:25, his H2 base at 23:15, nine converters and mohos at G1/G2 at 23:35, his advanced vehicle plant at G2 (6503,1004) at 23:56, his experimental gantry frame at G2 (6175,1023) at 24:09, and then his commander in sight at F2 (6122,1254) with 21k of army beside it. Make killing the commander the only job with no step-back when you see it. Meanwhile he sent single raiders and an armbeaver into our empty north-west — a Gauntlet at B1, a post at A2, an armfav eating a storage frame at A8. Keep six Stouts as a home hunting group and walk the commander to a defended back yard rather than chasing with it. Also send a constructor to reclaim the battlefield; constructor_7820 picked up 4.5k from E4/F4/E3, which paid for the lost pushes.

If I played it again: same opening, turrets on the outer extractors before his Rover arrives, energy built steadily from 2:00 on, no T1 push into F3/F4 at all, advanced plant at 10:00 instead of 11:19, and one push at 16:00 with Bulls. That is probably a win several minutes earlier.
