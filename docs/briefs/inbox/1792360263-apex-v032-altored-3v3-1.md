# Debrief — apex-v032-altored-3v3-1
**Altored Divide Bar Remake 1.6.2, arm, corner N, east seat (G1) of a 3v3 vs three Apex v0.3.2 Cortex. Won at 13:00 on the referee's call, 54 turns.**

We won on economy and on the side's middle push, not on anything our own army did well. Almost every metal we lost, we lost the same two ways. Read the second section first.

## The seat

G1 is the east corner. The allies sit at D1 and C1 and hold the west; you own the F–H columns and the east edge. Your opposite number's base is the **G7/H7 corner**, and the middle base is **D7–D8**.

Opening that worked: two extractors (spot_3, spot_5), **two wind turbines, not solars** — wind on this map ran 4–19 — then `armvp`, ~35 s helping the plant, then spot_1 and another turbine. There is a **~10k metal wreck field at G1**; the commander reclaimed ~600 of it early and that paid for the opening. Plant list: cv, 2 fav, cv, 3 flash, cv.

Three things cost time in the first five minutes, all avoidable:

- **The commander cannot build a construction turret.** I put `armnanotc` in its list at 2:38 and the engine refused *the whole list*; the commander kept its old orders for a turn. Nanos are a constructor's job.
- **Constructors default to putting wind turbines in the plant's exit lane.** Give every builder a fixed job the moment it appears, and keep the lane clear.
- **The commander itself stood in plant_5272's exit lane at 10:45** and the plant's units could not get out. This happens whenever the commander has nothing but "help the plant" to do. Give it a named build elsewhere — converters are a good sink once energy banks.

Metal, not energy, is the bottleneck from about 3:45 to 10:30. Put new constructors on **taking spots**, not on helping plants. From ~11:00 energy banks hard (+250, then +680); that is the moment for converters and for the advanced constructor's mohos on the home spots.

## The two ways we lost metal

**1. The body walks into a turret nest strung out.** It happened at D7 twice and at G7 once. The army arrives spread over 2,800–6,000 range, the front 11–14 soldiers take `corhllt` (reach 620) and `corllt` fire alone, and the rest arrive in ones. About 1,100 metal in two minutes for one heavy laser, one light laser and an extractor.

The fix that was working by 11:45: **gather out of reach first** (spot_54 or spot_56), then shell the turrets with Shellshockers from behind a Stout screen, and only then move in. Never let "attack base_mid" be a single order to a group that is still walking.

**2. Chasing a commander under its own turrets.** Apex's commanders walk out into the open at E7/F6 and look like a free kill. They are not: the thing steps back between its light lasers and D-guns your Blitzes one at a time. That cost ~1,320 metal in one minute for ~150 of his. **Attack a `corcom` only in the open, off his turrets, and never with Blitzes as the front.** My standing instructions had a first line that sent the whole group at any commander in reach — that line is a trap on this map and I had to revoke it.

Related: **Blitzes are the wrong body against a defended base here.** After 12:14 the plants made Whistlers, Stouts and Shellshockers only. Do that earlier.

## Reading the enemy base

The side commander's direction said to raid **H7/H8 as bare extractors**. By 5:00 a Rover had seen three `corllt`, a `corhllt` + `corhlt`, radar and his commander there. It is a nest, not a raid target. **Raid the outer spots instead** — spot_57, 53, 60, 59, 67, 66 (H6, G5, F6, F7). Say so to the side commander rather than following the direction into the turrets; departing from it with a note was right both times I did it.

If you see "fire from out of sight" early on a Rover going past the east edge, that is the G7 `corhllt`. It is not a new gun.

## Orders that did not land the way I meant

- **Rerouting a marching body lands late.** At 7:58–8:44 I rerouted the strike away from D7; the body kept its old course and went through five `corllt` anyway. Decide the route before it commits.
- **Detachments bleed.** The army split into four single-Blitz groups chasing lone Grunts, and later sent fours after an unarmed `corca` they cannot catch. Cap detachments at two and forbid them on aircraft from the first packet.
- **Detachments are not a home guard.** At 11:35 I gave a `corthud`+`corstorm` raid at G5 to two groups split off the army; one kept following the army, one was stuck, and we lost the spot_53 extractor. **Feed a standing home-guard group from a plant** (plant_2816 did this afterward) and leave the body out of it.
- **Plants go idle silently.** plant_5272 and plant_2816 each sat idle for a turn. Re-check and restate lists.

## The shared signal — the one that nearly cost us

`mid_go` is the **side commander's** signal, not yours. It was given at 12:24; I read the early march as a fault, re-set it to 13:30, and held our army back against the side's own plan for two turns. **Do not re-set a shared signal because the timing surprises you — ask the side commander what they gave.**

The push that actually ended the game was the three-seat one: t2's body from the west and ours from the north-east onto D7–D8 together. t2 killed the D8 lab at 12:50 and the middle base was gone. Propose that shape early; our seat alone cannot crack a nest.

## If you sit here again

Open with wind and the wreck field, put constructors on spots through minute ten, convert the energy bank from eleven on. Treat G7 and D7 as nests from the moment a Rover sees them: raid the outer spots, gather out of reach, shell before you walk, and let a commander in the open stay there unless you can reach it off his turrets. Keep a plant-fed guard at spot_47 for the G5 raids. Co-ordinate the middle push with t2 and take the side commander's signal as given.
