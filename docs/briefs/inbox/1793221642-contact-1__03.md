What actually cost the game
Everything that went wrong went wrong through the same mechanism: the body split. At 10:52 chasing four Pounders broke the army into chases of 8, 8, 2, 4 and seven Incisors died a few at a time to the Pounders' 315 reach for two kills. At 12:42 chasing three lone raiders had broken the army into roughly ten groups and killed the push entirely. At 30:05 it was splitting Brutes off into Tzar splash. Each time I had to rewrite the packet to forbid detachments, and each rewrite cost a turn and re-asked groups mid-chase. Write the rule into the first packet instead: there are exactly two jobs, and they never swap. A dedicated small Incisor group is the only raider answer for our half; the Brute/Lasher body never turns for a raider, ever, and forbid every detachment size explicitly (the hands will offer 8, then 2, then 4 if you only forbid one). Also expect the hunter group to be renamed by its own splits — mine went A2 to E2 to G2 — so name it by place or re-read the id each turn.

The pushes. push, push2, push3 all left spot_44/spot_32 and all died the same way: the body walks into reach it cannot pin. Standing off and shelling from Lasher reach (14:21) lost Lashers one at a time, 2,895 against 675 in a minute; all-in at once (14:39) was better when I had 5,080 vs ~1,350. Later, against tier 3, neither works: at 32:45 every attack streamed 3-10 of 50+ into reach, the hands then read "outweighed" and walked back, and the army ping-ponged between the attack order and its step-back place losing ~7k a minute. The worst single moment was 26:50 — a plain "go spot_13" walk took the body west through C4 into a Bulwark, Scorpions and a Karganeth and lost 10,500 in a minute. Do not give a bare walk order across the middle; name the route waypoints (spot_49, spot_47, guns_5, spot_52) and never a destination past ground you have not seen.

Economy. I stalled energy repeatedly at 1:35, 11:32, 12:52, 13:32 — always while metal banked (599, 960, 1,072). Solars cost no energy to build, so banked metal should become solars the moment income lags draw. Also: constructors were each building an energy storage, which adds no income — keep energy storage off builders' lists entirely. Later the opposite problem: both stores full and wasting from 28:38 on (metal 10,500, energy 22,900) because build power was short, not metal. The fix that worked and should come far earlier is construction turrets on the plants, extra plants, and above all converters — at 35:35 nine constructors building ~22 tier-1 converters (cormakr, 1 metal, 70 energy to 1 metal) took income from 75 to 115 for free while the energy store sat at 24k with ~2,000/s spare. Give the advanced constructors cormmkr, the tier-1s cormakr, and spread them over four separate marks so a bomber chain takes three at most.

Tech timing is where I was simply late. His advanced factory stood at 14:21; I started my advanced vehicle plant at 14:21 in reaction, with a bank of 150, so it crawled in at 55/s income. He had tier 3 (Karganeth, Shiva, then Tzars, then a Catapult gantry) by 27:15 and I started my gantry at 28:48. From there he had 1,350-reach Catapults and I had nothing to answer with. Next time start the advanced plant off a deliberate bank well before 14:00 and the gantry as soon as both stores run full, not after seeing his first Catapult. The units that actually earned their metal against him were Lashers (575 reach, outranged his 315 Pounders) and Banishers (800, outrange Tzar and Tiger); Brutes in the open under Catapults/Tzars/Shivas were food. From the gantry: Shivas and one Catapult to match his reach.
