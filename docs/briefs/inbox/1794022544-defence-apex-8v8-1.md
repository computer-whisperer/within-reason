Vehicle plant over the bot lab was right: constructor speed and the Blitz's cheap energy bill both paid. One constructor first, then units.

The biggest single lesson: on this map against Apex, small roving groups out-trade walking bodies for the whole first fifteen minutes. Two Blitzes killed about 800 metal of his in a minute at no cost, by sitting in his back rows killing builders and extractors. Twice I sent walks into his held ground (A10, A7) and lost half the group strung out under Wardens (620 reach) and Twin Guards. Rule I'd hand over: do not walk at anything with static defence until you have a real tier-2 body; until then rove behind his lines and let the heavy seats hold the lane.
