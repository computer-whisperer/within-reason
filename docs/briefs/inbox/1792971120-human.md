LESSONS FOR THE NEXT PLAYER

Check at 0:30 that an extractor actually stands. The refusals are real on this map and the plant will land on your extractor spot if you let the list run. Re-issue to different spots rather than re-sending the same list; I lost ~1:45 of economy and never bought it back.

Do not start advanced solars in an energy stall. Plain solars cost no energy to build and go up at full speed through a stall.

The gecko pair goes air by the late twenties. Wasps at 28:51, Dragons by 37:55. Banisher anti-air will not fire while any ground target is in reach, so a mixed body has no AA in practice. Have Furies in the body before 28:00, not after you have already lost 40k to aircraft. I was reacting a plant-switch behind the air the whole second half.

Never let the body stand within reach of static tier-2/3 guns. Both of my big losses (25:45, and the out-of-sight Persecutor at 20:29) were the body sitting under Persecutor/Cerberus range while shelling. Kill the gun first as one closed body, or route around it.

A fast enemy party will not be caught by chasing. I spent 40:43 to 41:30 redirecting the body spot to spot behind a Bull/Tzar group and it walked into our base anyway. Sit on the base under your own AA and let it come, or intercept ahead of it, never behind.

Fire the nuke only at things that cannot move, and move your own body out of the blast first. His anti-nuke at D2 covers 2000; standing turret nests and gun clusters 2,300+ away are the good targets, and that is what the second and third missiles did well.

Keep constructors out of plant exit lanes. It cost me a sealed Lasher plant (plant_12284 idle while asked for Lashers every 5 s) and a stalled turret frame, twice, late, when build power mattered most.

The commander will walk toward a fight despite a step-back order. If an enemy party is in the base, move the commander to a named far spot and keep re-issuing it; mine died at 41:55 doing exactly this and its death blast took our nanos and flak with it.

Ally t7 shares the south-east corner and will cap your spots north. Plan on reclaim (there is ~2,600 of wrecks at D5/C5) and on the west edge for expansion rather than fighting t7 for H5-H7.
