What went right, briefly. Mistake one, and the big one: energy. I was energy-bound from 5:00 to past 25:00, repeatedly, and I kept re-discovering it. At 5:06 energy stalled with 619 metal banked. At 7:31 metal was full at 1973 and energy at 98. At 8:00 I learned that advanced solars cost 4000 energy each to build and so cannot be built during an energy stall — switch to plain solars (no build energy, 9 s each) when energy is at zero. Then I over-corrected, stopped all generators at 10:03 with energy full, and by 20:13 was energy-bound again with 1241 metal banked; again at 23:50 with 6.7k banked; again at 25:22 with 6.3k banked. Advice: on this map with 20+ spots, decide the generator line once and keep it running ahead of the metal curve. Get a fusion started by ~12:00, not 22:00 (mine finished at 26:32, +2060 E, and the game was already decided-by-stalemate). When energy floats with metal at zero, converters are the fix (I built 6 at 10:03, two advanced at 31:17); when metal floats with energy at zero, plain solars. Expect to be doing both at different minutes and don't treat either as a one-time decision.
