# Debrief — apex-v032-altored-3v3-apex20-1

**Altored Divide Bar Remake 1.6.2, our side arm, corner N, start G1 (east end of our strip). Allies start D1 and C1. Opponent: Apex v0.3.2 standard, Cortex, +20%. Won at 17.5 min by referee, 87 turns.**

## Read this before your first packet

**Check the wind.** On this map wind was 4–19, which is enough that the commander's opening solars are better spent as wind turbines — mine were converted at 0:05 and that held all game. But wind that weak does not cover a vehicle plant plus the commander building at the same time: energy hit zero at 1:45 and expansion froze at two extractors from 1:35 to 2:15. Plan the opening with energy ahead of the plant, not beside it, and expect to park both builders on turbines for a minute if you don't.

**Give every constructor its own explicit build list, by 2:50 at the latest.** Left to the hands, they skip taken spots, duplicate each other, and build a wind turbine where an extractor was wanted (that is exactly what cost me spot_1 at 1:35). I ended up with one list per constructor, split so no two overlapped, and expansion ran clean after that. Expansion order that worked: east strip 1, 7, 21, 12, 6, 13, 17, 20, then south 28, 24, 33.

**There is free metal at home.** The wreck field at G1 is ~625 metal and F2 is ~500. At 3:00 I was sitting on 16 metal and 1,250 energy; sending the commander to take those apart lifted income to ~31/s and unstuck production. Do it earlier than I did, and pair it with two energy converters while energy is pinned full.

**Rovers must never attack.** I lost three of four Rovers to single Grunts by 2:05 because the hands kept re-sending them at a soldier that beats them. Write "Rovers only roam his east side; never attack, chase, or detach after any soldier" into the first packet, not the fifth.

## The opponent's shape

Apex raids with lone Grunts at your outer extractors and constructors from ~3:00 onward, and again hard at 15:19 once you push new spots at F7/F8 with no turrets — three Grunts killed two constructors, an extractor frame, and a third constructor before my orders landed. **Put a Nettle at each new forward extractor as part of the build list.** Later it brings corshad and corca air; two Nettles at home at 13:25 was the right call.

Its commanders rebuild. I killed one at F8 (14:21), one at C8 (16:56), and a third was still alive at A7 when the referee called it. Its plants went down one by one (G7 at 5:28, H7 at 11:52, D8 by allies, C8 by allies at 15:44) and it kept re-seeding labs and air plants behind the line — a corap at C7 at 13:25, an air plant at C7 at 13:35. **Expect to sweep west along row 8 and keep going into column A.**

## The one lesson that cost the most: commander kills

I tried three times. The pattern is identical every time:

- 5:28, 11 Blitzes onto a corcom at H7: killed the lab, lost 4 Blitzes to D-gun, commander lived.
- 9:17–9:36, 28-tank body at E7, 3.9:1 odds: **lost 10 Blitzes, commander lived.** The hands trickled the body in so only 1–2 tanks were ever in reach at once; each one died to a D-gun shot in turn.
- 14:21 at F8 and 16:37 at C8: worked, but only because the commander *walked into* the body. At C8 I had 26 tanks within reach at 9.4:1 and it cost three Stouts.

**A commander dies when the whole body is already surrounding it, not when you send the body at it.** The hands cannot deliver a simultaneous close — turning flee and march off for the group helped a little at 9:27 and was not enough. So: do not attack a commander on your initiative at anything under ~8:1 with the body already arrived. Route the body through his economy instead and kill the commander on the turn it blunders in. Switching the body off the corcom and onto his economy (F7/F8, then G7/H7/H8) at 5:28 and 10:55 is what actually won the map.

## Raid answers will eat your body if you let them

My 15:19 raid order backfired badly: three detachments totalling 20 soldiers, 14 of them Stouts, chased three Grunts that Stouts cannot catch, and the body itself drifted back east. The fix that held (15:39): **name one small fast group — for me group_O, four Blitzes — as the only raid duty for a named cell range, and say the body does not turn back.** Keep that sentence in the packet from the start.

## Two small things that will bite you

- **The commander will park in a plant's exit lane and block it.** It did at 2:05 and again at 15:09–16:37 on plant_12114. A build list is the way to walk it out — but at 15:09 my list was *refused* because the commander cannot build advanced solars. Give it something it can actually build (Nettles at nearby spots worked at 16:37).
- **Production mix that carried the mid-game:** Blitzes early with cv interleaved, then from 10:55 Stouts + Janus + 2 Shellshockers. Blitzes are what answer raiders; Stouts are the body and cannot chase.
