Debrief for the next player: Comet Catcher Remake 1.8, corner W (start B6), vs BARb stable hard_aggressive +20%. I won at 20.4 min, but most of the game was spent paying for mistakes you can skip.

What the opponent does, in order
- A lone Tick appears around 2:10 and goes straight for your outer extractors. It killed two of mine and chewed on a constructor because nothing I owned could catch it — all three Rovers were away scouting. Pawn streams follow.
- His factory is a bot lab at G1, the far north-east corner. Pawns and Ticks, so his army is slower than yours; the window roughly 4:00-7:00 is yours if you use it.
- First line units (Mace, Rocketeer) around 9:30 at F2. By ~13:00 he has a Mace/Centurion/Rocketeer blob at E2/E3 sitting under a Gauntlet, an Overwatch and Sentries.
- Tier 2 arrives mid-game: Hounds (reach 650, speed 69) that kite tanks and pick off Bulls around 16:40 at D4, and a cloaked Sharpshooter (reach 900, 2,500 a shot every 10 s) that parked at D2 from ~17:50.
- His turret posts are at E2/F2 and around his base; his commander sits at H1 beside his energy under five turrets and a Rattlesnake.

Opening that worked
Pool Armada line: 2 extractors, 2 solars, vehicle plant by ~0:34. Commander then helps the plant at low priority, putting a third extractor at 73 or 68 and solars in the gaps. Home spots 67, 74, 73, 68; next ring 62, 69, 61, 54, 63, 76, 50, 64, 52, 59, 71. Constructor 1 east (69, 76, 63, 64, 71), constructor 2 north (62, 54, 50, 45, 61, 52, 43). Plant list: Rover, con, 2 Rovers, con, 2 Blitz, Rover, con, 3 Blitz, con, then Blitz/Stout. 18 extractors and 43 income by 8:00 is a fine curve.

Do these differently
- Keep one Rover home from the start. Do not send all three scouting. The Tick costs you two extractors and several minutes of commander time otherwise, and a turret beside every outer extractor from the first ring is cheaper than the commander walking back.
- Never send the commander to fight a raider with "stop" in front of its list: I dropped a solar at 98% (151 metal) that way. Re-check that turret steps actually got built — the hands skipped a light turret at 73 and went straight to the extractor, and I had to re-send the list.
- Plan energy before the advanced plant. I stalled at zero energy for three separate turns from 9:30 to 11:40 because Bulls draw ~340/s each and two construction turrets were pouring 565/s into one Bull. Build the fusion (and plenty of plain solars) before you unlock Bulls, and forbid the construction turrets from helping the advanced plant until the fusion stands. Converters off surplus energy (I had 8.6k banked with +200 surplus at 8:52) are a good one-turn fix but not a substitute.
- Do not fight his army beside his turrets. I pushed into E2/E3 at 13:34 and lost ~2,800 metal for ~1,000. Later, at 20:22, I sent 21 soldiers onto his commander at H1 under five turrets: his D-gun killed 12, turrets took more, 16 lost (5,850 metal) for his bot lab. Both times the lesson was the same. Kill from range or not at all; a 20-unit body that closes on a commander is food.
- Do not hunt the cloaked Sharpshooter. Fencing group_home off that ground failed (it followed his Bull up there anyway), charging it failed (it slipped out of sight and the group stood with no order), and the chase cost a Bull and several Stouts. Just stay out of D2, 21, 23, 24 and let it sit.
- Never self-destruct a building to clear a lane. I blew a solar blocking the plant's exit: the real blast is 1,000 damage over 120 radius and it killed a stuck Blitz and three neighbours. Reclaim, or move the lane.

What actually won it
Economy behind turrets, then overwhelming mass. By 15:44 I had 9.9k of army against ~2,600 of his seen; by 18:53, 24k against ~2,800. The winning shape: hold the whole army on our side at spot_47 beside our turret until it is clearly larger, then strike round the south and up the east edge — spot_58, 55, 57, 60, 51, 48, 42, 34, 29, 25, 17, 12 to his lab at G1 — avoiding E2/F2 entirely. A second advanced vehicle plant plus a second fusion at 14:24 (metal was banking 1,300 at 100-140/s) doubles Bull output and is worth buying. Mix: Bulls, Janus for splash against Pawn blobs, Stouts as the body. Jaguars (speed 90) and Blitzes are what you have to catch Hounds. Keep pickets Blitz-only so the heavy units pool into the strike group, and keep a home group at spot_47 taking new production to answer raids of 3 or more.

One mechanical warning: strike groups bounced between waypoints (five arrivals at spot_51/48 in three minutes). Restarting the route from where the group stands fixed it both times. Expect to re-issue a route rather than trusting it to run to the end.
