Mistakes to not repeat.
- 2:20-3:00 the plant stood idle for 40 seconds: my hands rated "build a construction vehicle" at 0.24-0.43, under the pick bar, so nothing came out. Fix: give the plant a counted produce order (I used 20 construction vehicles) in the opening packet — counted entries build without asking. Do this at 1:20, not 3:00.
- Over-ordering frames. At 14:47 I had five advanced fusions, 13 advanced converters and 10 turret frames all open at once: build power spread thin, nothing finished, metal store drained to 2.9k and energy fell under three quarters so all converters went idle and income dropped 314 → 291. Rule: keep one advanced fusion per block under construction, rank frames in tend by handle so the most-built finishes first, and stop placing new frames when the store is draining.
- The three-quarters energy rule bit twice. Converters stop below 75% of the energy store, so a burst of frames costs you the converter income as well as the build. Watch the energy store level, not just energy income.
- Tend order mattered more than anything else I touched: turret frames first (so a new block grows itself), then advanced fusions, then advanced converters, and never the plants — the two plants were drawing ~2,500 e/s through 3,000 bp of turrets at 14:07 for units I did not need.
- armacv (advanced constructor) cannot build tier-1 solars; its list silently waited at 8:57. Split the work: armacvs place moho/advanced frames, tier-1 constructors finish them by id.
- Early list-id clashes credited several frames to the wrong spots (#e1, #e2, #e23, #e56, #e59 at 9:41) and I had to re-list with fresh ids. Use distinct, deliberate ids.
- 7:14-7:34 was the one real energy crunch: store at 0, 540/s in against 980 wanted, 2.5k metal idle, first fusion creeping at 0.4%/s. Solars from the commander and several constructors fixed it, but placing them off the block's ground (a solar_field mark NE of home) matters so they don't eat block slots.

Then stop at roughly the extractor count you can hold by 12:00 and pour everything into two or three turret blocks laid before 13:00, advanced fusions one at a time per block, converters placed continuously as energy surplus appears, keep-alive on in the last minute so no frame decays. The aim is both advanced fusions standing by 14:00-15:00 rather than 15:39 and 18:49.
