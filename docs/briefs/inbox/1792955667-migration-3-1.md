The opening that worked

Two things cost me metal in the first four minutes and both are avoidable. And I lost the spot_73 extractor to a single Tick at ~2:25 and spot_62 plus its half-built turret to a Pawn at ~3:42. His first raiders come in one at a time, early, at B8 and A7. Put light turrets at the home extractors before 2:10, not after the raider is seen; a turret at 67% dies the same as no turret. Blitzes chasing single Pawns around the map is wasted time — they are faster, so they do win, but the picket is the cheaper answer.

The army lesson, which is the whole game

Blitzes beat Pawns 2:1 in the open; that window is 4-7 minutes and worth using. After that they lose. The F5 fight at 11:56 ran even, by 12:45 Blitzes were losing 1430 to 756 against armrock+armwar, and at 14:31 his armmav/armfido T2 shredded them. Switch off Blitz mass by ~12:30: Blitzes become a picket against Pawns, plants go to Stouts and Shellshockers, AVP makes Bulls, later Mausers (820 outranges Hound 650) and Starlights (950 outranges sniper 900, Fatboy 700, Razorback 475). Picking the unit that outranges his current thing was the single most reliable lever I had.

The repeated mistake: I pushed six times and cancelled or bled on most of them. Every bad minute had the same shape — a partial body chasing something into his ground under unseen turrets or snipers. 16:00 chasing Hounds into E3/E4: lost 1.9k to 0.5k. 19:38 chasing the Fatboy into F3: 4.7k to 1.3k. 20:18 kited at spot_37: 3.5k to 0.5k. 26:05 group_Z2 walked into the experimental gantry nest at F2 (2 Razorbacks, sniper, Rattlesnake, Pit Bull) at 0.9:1 and lost 4.2k in ten seconds. Against that, the things that worked were whole-body attacks on a named thing: killing the Gauntlet frame at E3 at 17:29 with ~6k committed, the Fatboy at 21:18, 62 soldiers/34k gathered at spot_47 before push_six at 28:44. Do not set a push time and then let reinforcements trickle. Gather, check the body's metal is actually in one place (mine was spread 3.7k + 1.7k at 16:59 and the push had to slip), then commit with fan-only footwork so it doesn't flinch.

Also: Z2 once stood 50 seconds at spot_18 under a cloaked Rattlesnake on a G2 ledge taking 21k damage despite orders on the guns. If a group is losing health and not moving, give it one move order to the thing itself rather than another target in a list.
