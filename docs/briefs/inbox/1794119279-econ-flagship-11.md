What the shape of this game is. Income at 20:00 is the whole score, so the game is three phases and each one has a different bottleneck. Phase one (0-7 min): extractors, nothing else. Phase two (7-13 min): energy, then advanced plants and advanced extractors (mohos) on the spots you already hold. Phase three (13-20 min): the bank is useless unless you can convert it, and build power at the converter/fusion block is the only limit that matters. I lost the game in phase three — I ended with 41k metal banked at the cap and was still placing fusion frames at 19:15.

What I would do differently, in order of value. First, build the construction-turret block and its fusions far earlier — by 8-9 minutes, not 12-15. At 15:17 my second advanced fusion was rising 0.5%/s and the plain fusion 0.8%/s under 24 turrets; with 9,000 turret power late a plain fusion finished in about 6 seconds. Turrets are the compounding asset, and I added 18 more only at 15:17 out of idle tier-1 constructors that had been doing nothing. Second, at the late stage prefer plain fusions over advanced ones: an advanced fusion was ~35 s at the block, a plain one ~6 s, and the converters queued behind them only need energy, not elegance. Third, keep a standing count of advanced construction vehicles: from 10:33 on, armacv output was the limiting factor on mohos (76 spots, each moho worth ~6.9 more than a basic extractor), and one advanced plant was not enough — I added a second only at ~11:30, too late.
