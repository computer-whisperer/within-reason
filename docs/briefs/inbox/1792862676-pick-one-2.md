Opening: the pool line worked and I'd repeat it unchanged — extractor, extractor, solar, solar, vehicle plant (started 0:35), third extractor, then the commander helping the plant at low priority. From the plant: Rover, con, 2 Rovers, con, then Blitzes with a con every three. By 2:46 we were at 5 extractors and 13 metal/s, above the strong players' upper quartile, and by 4:06 at 9 extractors and 24 metal/s. Nothing of that needed changing.

The one thing to fix before it happens: energy. At 1:30 we made 70/s against a 217/s draw with the store empty and everything crawling; at 4:06 170 against 318; and it stalled again at 15:51 (900 in, 953 out) with 737 metal banked. Energy ahead of every new plant, and at the advanced plant put the solars down before you start it, not after. When metal is empty and energy is full, the answer is more extractors, not more solars — that was true at 8:46 and I was slow to see it.

His raids: single Pawns and a Tick from about 2:00 onward, one at a time, at our outer extractors (A6/spot_54, B3/C3, spot_74, spot_68). They killed unfinished turrets and extractors all game; we went 15 extractors down to 13 at one point. Blitzes are faster than Pawns and do catch them, but a 3,000-metal army cannot — at 8:16 I watched a big group chase Pawns round D6-E8 for fifty seconds for even trades while strung out 1,300 deep. Keep a small home guard of Blitzes (a dozen was right) as pickets on the raided spots plus a Sentry beside each outer extractor, and never let the main body turn round for a raider. Pickets at B3/C3 specifically: a Rover kept killing frames there until four Blitzes sat on it.

His base: a nest at G1 with Beamer and Sentries, a Dragon's Claw, and a Gauntlet at H2 that reaches a long way. His bot lab is at G1; we never actually found it by sight until very late. Grinding into that nest piecemeal cost me 9 Blitzes in two minutes at 13:17 and more at 15:31 — twice. The right shape is: gather the whole body at spot_16 (F2), out of turret reach, wait for the Bulls and the second wave, then go in once, Beamer first, lab second, and no stepping back once the order is given. We had 4.5x his army by 11:20 and 5.5:1 at the nest; the only thing that ever killed our soldiers was walking into static defence in a thin line.

The expensive lesson is about the hands, not BARb. From 16:20 to 17:28 the push kept failing for mechanical reasons: three to five soldiers jammed in the advanced plant's exit lane at home counted as the group's "front", so the whole body nearly walked home to gather on them; and once a group has a walk order it keeps it and runs from fire instead of fighting. Two things that worked: say the group's move as an attack/advance on a named thing (his_lab) rather than "gather" or "walk to", and put each new plant's output into its own group so a jam at home can never be the main body's front. Three separate times a builder or the commander stood in a plant's exit lane and stopped Bulls leaving — commander solars at spot_69/C7 and a constructor's advanced solar both did it. Build all home energy in a back corner (I used a named spot in A8, back_yard) and keep lanes clear.

Timings that worked out: second vehicle plant at home around 5:36, advanced vehicle plant at home about 11:20 with advanced solars for its draw, Bulls out by 15:30, mohos on the home spots once the advanced constructor appeared and energy was full. Turrets over the extractors as they go up, not later.
