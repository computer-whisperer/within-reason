What lost the game

Three things, in order of cost.

One: the march never happened. Three times a huge body sat still for whole minutes while "advancing" — 14:13 at spot_41, 38:08 at spot_29 for 27 s, 19:05 group_I1 parked at E3 instead of walking to spot_64. A body that outweighs everything in sight gets offered only detachments by the hands, and detachments of 4-8 Stouts get eaten one at a time by a single tier-2 Gunslinger. You must forbid every detachment size explicitly and name exactly one move, or the group will not go. Do that pre-emptively the moment any group's weight ratio passes about 4 to 1, not after you lose six Stouts. Note that a packet written at 38:41 did not land until 38:42 and the split had already happened — write the no-detachment clause into the opening packet and leave it there.

Two: the all-in on his lab. From 15:12 to 21:00 I walked the whole army west round the C5 nest toward B8 and traded about 9,000 for 2,000 doing it, to Rattlesnakes, snipers and the long march. The lab never fell and meanwhile his raiders ate the home spots; we were down to 5 extractors by 36:21. Do not cross the map for his lab on this layout. Hold the middle, kill what comes, and keep extractors alive.

Three: no radar and no answer to out-of-sight range. The worst trades of the game (5,400 lost to 2,300 in one minute at 38:44) were Vanguards and Fatboys firing from where we could not see. Build radar early and rebuild it; we only thought of radar towers at 38:44, which is far too late.

Smaller recurring faults worth pre-empting: the commander stood in a plant's exit lane at 9:30, 35:25 and 36:31 — give it a standing solar-field job away from lanes. Constructors got physically stuck (109 s on one advanced plant at 20:50) — give them a short list with alternates. Build power ran short around 9:10 (energy full, 430 in / 220 out, metal banked 470, no construction turret) — the pool has 1-2 construction turrets by 6:00, put them on the plants early. Metal banked at 839 by 15:50 and 2,000+ at 35:18; spend it. A nuke silo's stockpile is an enormous energy draw late; our missiles mostly chased a mobile Vanguard with a bearing estimate, which is a poor use — fire at massed static or at his gantry, and his anti-nuke sits at B8.
