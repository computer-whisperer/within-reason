# For an Opus player, read beside the brief

The brief above is what the project knows about the game. This section is about you: what an Opus player does in
this seat that the game punishes, measured in recorded games. A Sonnet player does not read it.

**Your orders land late.** Each turn you read a picture and write orders; the orders take effect when your calls are
made, 5 to 20 seconds after the wake in realtime games with people (human-26: 33 turns, median 11 s), and the report's
"while your orders were on their way (N s) we lost ..." line says what that cost last time. So the packet you write
fits the moment you saw, not the moment it lands. In human-26 at 3:27 red's player saw a quiet base and a Blitz group
stuck at the plant, and wrote "group_B_t2: the raid. It goes west now ... Enemy armfav at our home extractors are not
its business once it has left spot_18"; it landed at 3:39 on seven Rovers burning the base, and the hands did what it
said: the Blitzes walked away to spot_18 and spot_27 while the extractors died.

**Raid responses are organised by the allowances you leave Jev in your packets while other things are occurring.** Jev
reads the whole packet every second against the picture of that second and picks each group's next move from a menu;
what the packet does not allow, it does not do, and what the packet puts last, it weighs least. Your packets tend to
be exact: a route named stop by stop, and then a sentence that closes the exception. Measured offline on human-26's
3:33 (six Blitzes at home, seven Rovers on the H2 extractor): with the raid rule last in a paragraph that began with
a gather and a route, the attack rated 0.43 and nothing was played for twenty seconds; with the same rule as the
packet's first paragraph, 0.60 and the attack went. So:

- Every paragraph that gives a group a job says what interrupts it, before the job: "group_B: a party of three or
  more at our extractors, constructors or home is its business first, whatever it was walking to, and its route
  resumes when none stands in our base. Its route: spot_18, then spot_27 ...".
- The allowance has a scale, or it overcorrects: a lone Rover at an outer extractor is the picket's or one soldier's,
  never the body's (player-57: the body left its advance for single Rovers at home and thrashed for a minute); a
  swarm at the base is everyone's. Say both.
- Do not write the exclusion ("not its business") unless you mean it against the raid you cannot see yet.

**The `recently seen` lines of the report are for this.** A party that burned one base and left it intact, out of
sight now and last seen moving toward our side, is on its way to the other base. Human-26 2:50 to 3:33: seven Rovers
left yellow's base, were out of sight for 27 seconds crossing the middle, and arrived at red's. Write the allowance
for them before they arrive, in the packet that is already on its way.

**What the players before you wrote for you, from the games against people of 2026-10-23 to 10-25.** Each is one
player's reading of its own game in its debrief, held against the notes it wrote while it played; none is measured
over games.

- **Ask for another seat's body with a place and a minute, never for "support".** Comet Catcher, 2026-10-23: our
  north body (2,300) and thebluegecko's (2,100) advanced and backed between spot_15 and spot_20 for minutes, losing
  units each swing, with an allied body standing beside it; the asks of 10:42, 11:25 and 19:29 brought nothing, and
  the one that ended the game, at 21:27, named his base at B4 and said now. Comet Catcher, 2026-10-25: four urgent
  asks from 11:38 to 14:25, for bodies 1,700 to 3,000 away, came after the north lane was lost; ask when a lane
  starts to turn (about 8:00 there), not when it has gone.
- **Leave a packet alone while a fight is going your way.** A changed packet is read again whole and every group's
  move asked again under it; on 2026-10-25 the north seat's player sent nothing at 13:53, 14:11 and 16:41, with its
  body winning the trade at home, and judged that right.
- **Put the commander's help to the plant at low priority once, in the first packet.** On 2026-10-25 the store ran
  dry at 1:30, 4:39 and 5:33, each time the commander helping the plant at full priority, and the player fixed it three
  times.
- **Walk the commander out before the fight reaches it, to a named place far off, with nothing else to do.** Boreal
  Falls 10:51 (it D-gunned 11 of 12 Blitzes and died, its blast taking the plant and the last constructor), Eye of
  Horus 23:33 and All That Glitters 41:55: each commander died walking out with the enemy already in its base.
