# thebluegecko's unit sheet, 2026-10-23

Data, not claims. The glossary sheet (`run/glossary_sheet.py`, exported 2026-10-19 as `docs/glossary/units.csv`) was
shared with the experienced players for their per-unit opinions; thebluegecko filled 24 rows (all Armada, all tier 1:
the plants, the economy buildings, the three mines, the radar, the torpedo launcher, the Gauntlet, a gunship, the
scout bot, a skirmisher bot, an artillery tank and the Blitz), about 530 words, in the sheet's last columns ("what it
is for, when to build it, when not"; "beats / loses to"; "tips, micro, mistakes"; the "verdict" column used for free
text in nine rows; the "who" column empty, the attribution the user's). `gecko-units-2026-10-23.csv` beside this file
holds those rows as downloaded (the key `unit`, the name, faction, tier and class, and his five columns); the sheet is
https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1pyO-pGMLFNar-hbyJ8nkiff3pAVg6ZymQ1pZq05Ef6I/. His words go into the lessons
layer verbatim (docs/briefs/lessons.md) when imported; nothing here is a rule.
