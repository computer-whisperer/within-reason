# Tier 3: the gantry's units, what people build, and what beats what (2026-11-08)

The user's ask: "In the most recent game against u6bkep both sides deployed tier 3 units, but only u6bkep used his
correctly. We also don't have a good understanding for the rock-paper-scissors of that level of unit. See what you
can find of what people use, how their stats stack up, and what the player should know about them for better
effect." Four reads: the game, the unit files, the public team games, and an engine duel bench at equal metal. No
code decides anything new; the harness changes, the glossary corrections and the brief article are listed at the end.

The scripts: `run/tier3.py` (`pool`, `weapons`, `matrix <duel dir>`, `game <match dir>`) and `run/nuke_direct.py`. The duel batches:
`run/matches/1794189*-duel-t3-matrix4` and `-duel-t3-ball2`. Rerun before quoting a number elsewhere.

The blast thins with distance as the fact layer says: 300 from the centre a unit takes 7,200 to 7,700 of the

A Behemoth walks out of a nuclear fireball with 96 percent of its health. Killing one takes 23 Apocalypse missiles
on the same spot, 34,500 metal and 4.3 million energy and 69 minutes of one silo, for a 20,000-metal unit; the
commander's D-gun does it in one shot for 500 energy. A missile kills everything of the gantry up to the Karganeth
and nothing above it: a Thor keeps three quarters, a Titan four fifths. Nukes are for what cannot walk and for the
cheap body packed together, not for the giants.

One thing seen and not explained: on Comet Catcher, with the Apocalypse aimed 25 north of the Behemoth, the unit was
shoved a few elmos south and two seconds after the warhead's 15,075 it took another 319,590, leaving it 0.7
percent (four runs of four; the Thor in the same place died, three of three). Aimed 25 south, east or west of it, or anywhere on
flat Full Metal Plate, there was no second blow, and the Armageddon in the same place added 1,235. It follows the
ground the blast throws the unit onto, not the warhead; nothing here says how to cause it on purpose. A reading of the source, not measured (the user asked whether it is the directional-damage rule): it is not, since that rule multiplies a hit by 1 to 2 (`modrules.lua` `flankingBonus`, mode 1, min 1, max 2) and the warhead's own blow was 15,075 from all four sides; the engine's ground-impact damage (a unit that the blast has set skidding and that finds the ground below it falls, and takes its impact speed times its mass times 0.02 when it lands, `GroundMoveType.cpp` 81, 1533-1549, 1604-1608; the user's second question, the crater dropping the ground under it, is that route) is the right size for the small second blows seen elsewhere (690 to 1,340: a Behemoth's mass of 20,000 makes 400 a blow per elmo a frame of impact speed, and a fall into a crater is a few elmos a frame) and far too small for this one, which would need 800 elmos a frame in a single impact. The spot is the foot of a hill that rises 80 elmos to the north, and the one aim that did it put the crater into that hillside. The big blow stays unexplained; a run logged every frame would say whether it is one impact or many. The replay of one such run (`run/matches/1794194775-replay-nuke-behemoth-second-blow`, from the demo in `1794193872-duel-nuke5-comet-n/01/demos`; no desync) corrects the geometry: the engine has the Behemoth standing at (6600, 2992), where it was given, not at the (6594, 3014) the director's log reports, so the aim called "25 north" was within 7 of the unit, a direct hit, and the aims that gave no second blow all landed 23 to 47 south of it. In the replay the warhead lands at 5:13 (95 % left), the unit slides 22 south off the toe of the hill over the next two seconds, and at 5:15 it is at 0 %. Why the director's log places the unit 22 off is not looked into. Watched in the client by the user (2026-11-08 night): the Behemoth is perched on the side of a crater, the missile drops the ground out from under it and it falls. The fall is the cause as seen; the reading above that a fall cannot be this large rests on one ground impact of the engine's formula and is not re-checked (the user: no more sim time on the question).

## 6. What this says

1. A gantry unit is bought for a job, not for the tier: the cheap reach (Shiva, Karganeth, Keres) is the line tank
   that out-reaches line tanks; the Thor is the front that stuns; the Vanguard, Catapult, Daedalus, Myrmidon and
   Astraeus are guns that need a front; the Razorback and Demon are fast and thin, for what cannot hold them off;
   the Titan and the giants are one body with reach, anti-air and no retreat needed.
2. A front with the long gun behind it is the composition: Thor and Starlights for Armada; the same shape is Shivas
   in number for Cortex (thebluegecko's 117 in the Full Metal Plate game, none lost).
3. It goes as a body. Fourteen arrivals killed 4,120; people's brawlers that die have lived two and a half minutes.
4. Every ground composition without something that fires up loses to its metal in gunships; ours must carry
   anti-air, and his that does not is the bombers' and gunships' target before it is the army's.
5. Against a Thor: aircraft, or stay outside 540 and kill what stands behind it; never Razorbacks or Bulls into it.
   Against Titans: Pulsars, or numbers of the cheap reach. Against any of them at home: the commander's D-gun.
6. The gantry needs build power beside it like any plant: alone, 67 s a Razorback, 200 s a Titan, 433 s a Behemoth.
7. A nuclear missile kills the gantry's cheap and middle units and takes a twentieth of a Behemoth; the giants'
   answers are the D-gun, the long tower and what they cannot fire at.

## 7. What was written, and what is proposed

Written (2026-11-08): the brief article `docs/briefs/derived/tier3.md` (in the manifest after tier 2); the glossary
texts of the Razorback, Shiva, Behemoth, Titan, Thor and Demon, which the unit files and the bench contradict (the
Thor and the Behemoth cannot be stunned and were said to have no resistance; the Behemoth and the Titan fire at
aircraft and were said not to; the Shiva was said to lose to Bulls and Pulsars; the Razorback was "the gantry's
answer when the enemy has an army"); the claims K-units-t3-* in `docs/knowledge/units.md`.

The duel harness, found broken for any unit that fires on energy and fixed: `--economy fed` gave its fusion before
the cheat flag had come back from the server, so the give was refused and never repeated (now repeated until one
stands, and duels wait for it); `fed*N` gives N fusions and an energy store (a Pulsar's shot takes 1,500 at once);
a fed batch's sites stand 2,600 from the commanders (an army on the nearest site shot one team's fusions); a Legion
unit on the command line turns the faction on. `docs/harness/duels.md` says so. The nuke check of 2026-10-09 was fed, so the break dates
from the console `/give` of 2026-10-24 or later; `fed` batches between then and now are suspect.

Proposed, not built:

- *The unit tables' damage column* sums guns of different reach. A reach-banded figure (damage at the longest gun,
  damage up close) would have said what the Titan and the Juggernaut are without a bench.
- *A matchup line for the player* when a gantry unit of his is first seen: what of ours beats it at equal metal,
  from this matrix. Today the report introduces a type with its glossary line only.
- *The same bench one tier down* (tier-2 units against each other and against tier-1 mass, with energy now
  working), and with a stun source and an anti-air escort added, which this one leaves out.
