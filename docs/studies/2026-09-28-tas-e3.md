# A hand-built TAS of player-14's E3 fight at 9:17

Phases 1 and 2 of `docs/design/2026-09-28-tas-micro.md`: the instrument (a timed script for our side, the recorded
track for theirs, seeds, the per-second log; `docs/harness/duels.md`, "Scripts, tracks, seeds and the per-second
log"), then a script for our Blitzes written against the engine until the position falls. Every margin here comes
from a `duels.csv` this worktree wrote (`run/matches/<batch>/`, read with `run/scenario_table.py`); the margin is our
value left minus theirs (metal x health over what each side started the fight with). Worktree `bar_bots-tas`, branch
`tas-micro`, Comet Catcher Remake 1.8, speed 50, two engines.

**What it says.**
- **Their side replays as it went.** Under the record's orders and the recorded track, the enemy keeps 0.688 of its
  value at 30 s (live 0.650) in the 900 cut and 0.690 (live 0.687) in the 1,250 cut, sd 0.03-0.04 over eight. The
  60 s numbers do not match (live 1.000): the live Centurions walked home and were repaired, which a duel does not do.
- **Our side keeps more than it did live**: 0.52 (900 cut) and 0.42 (1,250 cut) against 0.287. The retreat at 5 s
  is in the script both times, so the difference is what hit the Blitzes on the way out: units outside even the
  1,250 cut (a sixth Pawn at 1,443), and the live enemy's own attack orders (on the track they only walk, shooting
  what passes in reach; they do not chase). Open; the per-second logs of both batches have every Blitz's health.
- **The loss at E3 was the retreat, not the position as cut.** The same fourteen Blitzes attack-moving (the director)
  wipe the five-unit position in 25 s, 8 of 8, keeping 0.30 of their value; with the record's orders (focus on one
  Centurion, fights round the turrets, home at 5 s) they leave with 0.52 and the enemy keeps 0.69. The enemy's
  reinforcements change that: against the 1,250 cut the same attack-move is wiped 8 of 8 (-0.33), leaving the enemy a
  third. So the position the live game met was the turrets, the Centurions **and the Pawns and Rocketeers walking in
  from behind the turrets**, and the retreat at 5 s cost the difference between -0.17 and +0.30 against the first.
- **Energy did not decide it.** Two turrets over a 25 s fight fire from their storage either way (+0.297 fed, +0.330
  bare, within one sd); the TAS batches below are fed, since the live turrets had an economy behind them.
- **The replay is stable**: margin sd 0.03-0.08 over eight for four of the six arms, 0.125 for the director against
  the wide cut (whether the Pawns arrive before the Blitzes finish the Centurions).

**F3 re-cut.** The player-15 F3 cut (9:50, r 900) holds eight Pawns and none of the four Rocketeers under a light
turret that the player's note says took the fight: they stood at (5480-5860, 1630-1840), 1,000-1,300 from the place,
