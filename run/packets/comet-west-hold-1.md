commander: while we have no factory, what it builds next depends on what stands. With 0 extractors: an extractor at spot_45. With 1 extractor: an extractor at spot_50. With 2 extractors and 0 solar collectors: a solar collector (armsolar) at home. With 2 extractors and 1 solar collector and no factory: the vehicle plant (armvp) at home, and nothing else before it. From the moment the plant stands its standing job is helping the plant, for the whole game; it leaves the plant only to build one solar collector when the energy line reads STALLING, and goes back to helping the plant. It also builds one light turret (armllt) beside each outer extractor of ours that has none (spot_36 and spot_54 are within its walk), one turret at a time and back to the plant after each; the home spots need no turret, its own laser covers them. It attacks a raider party at a building of ours within its short walk, never chases a scout car or a Tick, and never walks out to far spots: no extractors and no radar from it beyond spot_50.

plant (the vehicle plant): what it builds next depends on how many constructors we have and on the size of our army. With 0, 1, 2 or 3 constructors: a construction vehicle (armcv). With 4 or more constructors, while our soldiers are none, a handful or a group: a Blitz (armflash). With 4 or more constructors, once our soldiers are a real army: a Stout (armstump), one after another for the whole game, with one Blitz after every four Stouts. It never builds Rovers, Shellshockers, Janus, Whistlers, Pincers, Beavers or Groundhogs, and it never builds nothing while metal is there.

constructors: their standing job is taking free metal spots on our strip, nearest home first (spot_36, spot_54, spot_28, spot_30, spot_62, spot_67, spot_19, spot_68, spot_74, spot_14, spot_7, spot_2), with one light turret (armllt) beside each outer extractor and never a second turret at the same spot. Constructors never build at the middle spots and never go to them: not spot_43, spot_61, spot_52, spot_38, spot_39, spot_63, spot_22, spot_26, spot_73 or spot_69. A constructor walks back toward home when enemy soldiers are near it. A constructor builds one solar collector (armsolar) only when the energy line reads STALLING; while energy is banking or in balance it builds no solar. When no free spot on our strip is left, a constructor stands at home and builds nothing more: no more solars, no more turrets, no radar, no second factory.

group_A: the plant's soldiers gather into group_A, which stands at spot_45, north of the plant beside the commander, and holds home and our strip from there. A lone Tick or scout car at an extractor of ours is met by one soldier sent from group_A (send_against 1), which stays at that extractor as its picket; that is the only detachment group_A ever sends, and it never splits otherwise. A party of two or more soldiers that is killing a building of ours within 1,200 of spot_45 is met by the whole group, which walks back to spot_45 the moment that party is dead or gone. group_A never goes after a party farther than 1,200 from spot_45 however heavily we outweigh it, never attacks a constructor with the whole group, and never advances to the middle or to the enemy base: not spot_38, spot_39, spot_43, spot_52, spot_61, passage_2 or passage_3. When a party outweighs group_A it falls back to home, beside the plant, and returns to spot_45 when the party is gone. group_A holds our strip and grows; the game is won later, not by it.
