commander: while we have no factory, what it builds next depends on what stands. With 0 extractors: an extractor at spot_45. With 1 extractor: an extractor at spot_50. With 2 extractors and 0 solar collectors: a solar collector (armsolar) at home. With 2 extractors and 1 solar collector and no factory: the vehicle plant (armvp) at home, and nothing else before it. From the moment the plant stands its standing job is helping the plant, for the whole game; it leaves the plant only to build one solar collector when the energy line reads STALLING, and goes back to helping the plant. It also builds one light turret (armllt) beside each outer extractor of ours that has none (spot_36 and spot_54 are within its walk), one turret at a time and back to the plant after each; the home spots need no turret, its own laser covers them. It attacks a raider party at a building of ours within its short walk, never chases a scout car or a Tick, and never walks out to far spots: no extractors and no radar from it beyond spot_50.

plant (the vehicle plant): what it builds next depends on how many constructors we have and on the size of our army. With 0, 1, 2 or 3 constructors: a construction vehicle (armcv). With 4 or more constructors, while our soldiers are none, a handful or a group: a Blitz (armflash). With 4 or more constructors, once our soldiers are a real army: a Stout (armstump), one after another for the whole game, with one Blitz after every four Stouts. It never builds Rovers, Shellshockers, Janus, Whistlers, Pincers, Beavers or Groundhogs, and it never builds nothing while metal is there.

With no free spot left in the table, a constructor stands at home and builds nothing more: no more solars, no more turrets, no radar, no second factory.

group_A: the plant's soldiers gather into group_A. Its place is spot_45, north of the plant beside the commander: when it stands anywhere else with nothing to fight it walks to spot_45, and there it holds, covering home and our strip. A lone armflea, armfav or armpw at an extractor of ours is met by one soldier sent from group_A (send 1); that is the only detachment group_A ever sends: it never sends two, four or eight. When a party outweighs group_A, group_A walks to home, beside the plant and under the commander's laser, and holds there; it walks back to spot_45 when that party is gone. Soldiers that left group_A as a detachment join group_A again when their party is dead or gone.
