commander: while we have no factory, what it builds next depends on what stands. With 0 extractors: an extractor on the metal nearest it. With 1 extractor: an extractor on the metal nearest it. With 2 extractors and 0 solar collectors: a solar collector (armsolar) at home. With 2 extractors and 1 solar collector and no factory: the vehicle plant (armvp) at home, and nothing else before it. From the moment the plant stands its standing job is helping the plant, for the whole game; it leaves the plant only to build solar collectors when the energy line reads STALLING, two at a time, and goes back to helping the plant. It attacks a raider party at a building of ours within its short walk, never chases a scout car or a Tick, and never walks east of C1.

plant (the vehicle plant): what it builds next depends on how many constructors we have and on the size of our army. With 0, 1, 2 or 3 constructors: a construction vehicle (armcv). With 4 or more constructors, while our soldiers are none, a handful or a group: a Blitz (armflash). With 4 or more constructors, once our soldiers are a real army: a Stout (armstump), one after another for the whole game, with one Blitz after every four Stouts. With 8 or more constructors and energy STALLING: a construction vehicle every fifth unit. It never builds Rovers, Shellshockers, Janus, Whistlers, Pincers, Beavers or Groundhogs, and it never builds nothing while metal is there.

constructors: every extractor on this map draws the same metal, so a constructor's job is the carpet: it builds an extractor on the metal nearest it, one after another, for the whole game, each beside the last, staying in A1, A2, B1 and B2, our two columns. When the energy line reads STALLING, a constructor builds two solar collectors (armsolar) and then goes back to extractors; with energy banking or in balance, no solar. Constructors never build energy converters, never build at C1 or east of it, and walk back toward home when enemy soldiers are near them.

group_A: the plant's soldiers gather into group_A. Its place is B1, the cell east of home on the north plateau: when it stands anywhere else with nothing to fight it walks to B1, and there it holds, covering our two columns. A lone armflea, armfav or armpw at an extractor of ours is met by one soldier sent from group_A (send 1); that is the only detachment group_A ever sends. A party of two or more soldiers that is killing a building of ours in A1, A2, B1 or B2 is attacked by the whole group, which walks back to B1 the moment that party is dead or gone. Once group_A is a real army of 12 or more soldiers it advances to D1, holds there, and when it still outweighs everything in sight advances to F1 and holds; it never advances east of F1. When a party outweighs group_A, group_A walks to B1 and holds there, or to home when B1 is lost. Soldiers that left group_A as a detachment join group_A again when their party is dead or gone.
