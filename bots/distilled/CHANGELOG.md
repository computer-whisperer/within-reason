# WRDistilled changelog

The player-facing record: what changed for someone who plays against the bot. The development record, with the
rules, their sources and the benchmarks, is `README.md`. The top section is the release notes of the version it names
(`run/release_package.py` takes it).

## 0.2.0 (2026-11-07)

The first release as a download. Everything a development game ran to this date played as "0.1"; this is that bot:

- Tier 1 vehicles with a tier 2 economy behind them, and a nuclear silo when the game lasts.
- Expands over the metal spots, defends the base with turrets, answers raids with what is near.
- One body of soldiers in formation; scouts before it; air when the economy allows.
- Plays Armada and Cortex; Legion is wired up and little tested.
- Windows and Linux. No options yet: the lobby's AI options are the engine's defaults.
